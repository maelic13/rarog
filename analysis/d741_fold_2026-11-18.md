# D.7.4.1: the compact kernel's constants folded into Rarog's units (2026-11-18)

D.7.4 built the compact kernel in the donor's units behind a scaffold: the
evaluation multiplied by 2536/1024 at the wrapper and every score divided
back at the boundary. This step converts each constant by its dimension,
deletes the scaffold, and checks the folded kernel against the scaffold at
zero games. PLAN D.7.4.1 holds the decisions and deviations; the rule is
`analysis/search_shape_inventory_2026-11-13.md` section 5, *The fold*.

## The fold as applied

k = 1024/2536. A margin in evaluation units is multiplied by k, a per-unit
coefficient divided by it; ratios, plies, counts, history amounts and the
exchange table are unchanged (the donor's piece values stay, maintainer
decision 2026-11-18). Where a whole unit would move a value by more than 1%
the value is held in sixteenths (`_16`); two per-unit steps are held in
1024ths (`_1024`); the depth- and history-multiplied slopes of razoring,
futility and ProbCut's raise are held in sixteenths, because their rounding
grows with depth. Two premises of the inventory were false and are
corrected in it: the correction's update slope is a ratio and stays, and
the correction tables' saturation is in evaluation units, so the compact
kernel takes its own folded limits (5,897 keyed, 6,629 continuation).
Futility's and bad-noisy futility's values leave their rules in whole units
(`(s + 8).div_euclid(16)`); their sixteenths stay inside the gates.

## Layer A: decisions on the donor's records

The parity test (`search::compact::parity`) reads the folded rules on the
donor's trace (D.7.4's trace-a, 250 positions): each record's evaluation
fields converted to Rarog's units and rounded, each decision required to
match. Two excuses, each counted:

- an **exception**: a decision bracketed by moving the converted inputs one
  unit either way (the rounding the fold itself introduces), with the moved
  outputs' classes aligned up to that decision; under 2% of every kind;
- a **distance**: a record on a decisive score, whose value the fold does
  not convert; `VALUE_NONE` excuses nothing.

`fold_a7.log` (`analysis/artifacts/d74-parity-2026-11-17/`): 47 kinds, **0
mismatches**; exceptions at most 1.44% of a kind (`singular_result`, 52 of
3,609; `lmr` 303 of 58,399); every plant fails its own kind; the three seeds
converted by the wrong dimension (futility, razoring, LMR) fail theirs; 15
of 246 seeds no record detects, each with a reason checked on the trace
(the floors of `rfp_min_16` and `nmp_min_16` hold over one to sixteen
sixteenths; `double_quiet_16`'s nearest record is 41/16 of 717 away;
`fds_tt_pv_deep` reaches no bar in 1,449 records; the trace holds no
keyed-table update; the remaining PV-only and thread seeds are sparse).

The check itself was wrong three times before it passed, each found by a
planted change it should have caught or a count that could not be true:

- a distance granted on `VALUE_NONE` excused real failures: the unreduced
  plant failed nothing until distances were restricted to decisive scores;
- the coverage of seeds compared outputs of different shapes, so every seed
  read as detected; it now compares like-shaped outputs only;
- bracketing dropped moved outputs whose shape changed (a gate that stops
  firing drops its amounts), so 53 `corr_update` and 35 `tt_cut_bonus`
  decisions inside the rounding read as mismatches; classes are now aligned
  up to the decision.

Three plants were too small to be seen through the rounding and were resized
(`eval_bonus_scale`, `lmr_quiet`, `fds_quiet`, each +512).

## A defect the check did not see

The compact bench ran 224 million nodes at its position 1 on the first
folded build: futility's and bad-noisy futility's values were returned in
sixteenths and the node raised its best score to them. The parity check had
treated both as intermediate values and never compared them. Both now
return whole units, and the check compares them as quantities.

### Position 202: a timeout the decided quantity drops

Position 202 (`6B1/8/5k2/8/4pK2/4P3/8/8 b - - 4 77`) timed out at depth 14;
the scaffold took 184,579 nodes and the twin 621,964. The compare drops a
missing depth, so the read above stands on 495 positions at depth 14. The
cause, from zero-game reruns (`p202/`):

- the depth-14 root search fails low twenty times, its score falling from
  −1,236 to below −19,872, the display's cap for an ordinary score; the
  last search, its window opened fully below, was stopped at a 590-second
  cap after about 1.5 billion nodes without finishing (`folded_long.txt`);
- the scores are not evaluations (a won KQB against K reads about −1,150):
  an instrumented copy shows the table returning ±31,743, a mate the
  fifty-move clock could falsify read back as the highest ordinary score
  (`score_from_tt`, the donor's and Stockfish's rule too), and the fail-high
  blend mixing it with beta;
- the class predates the fold: on positions from the same line the scaffold
  also reports ordinary scores for mates (−9,504 at 27.8 million nodes at
  depth 16, where the folded build takes 8.1 million), the classic kernel
  reports mate −36, −32 and −40 on the same three, and the twin stays
  ordinary but small (350,402 to 782,568 nodes).

So the fold did not cause it; the folded tree reached it at depth 14 where
the scaffold's did not. The compact kernel's handling of the downgraded
mate band departs from the donor's in tree size by one to two orders of
magnitude. The donor's band differs from Rarog's in where it sits
(`MAX_PLY` 240, the decisive band from 31,519; Rarog's 128 and 31,744) and
it also downgrades tablebase scores. Open, below.

## The five tests the fold exposed

- **MultiPV:** each line searches every window at the iteration's depth
  (`CompactWindow::at_iteration_depth`); the one-line search keeps the
  donor's shallower re-search after a fail-high. A line settled shallower
  had outranked one settled at the depth (`a_helper_searches_every_line`).
  Bench position 11 at MultiPV 2, depth 13: 629,744 nodes.
- **Draw score:** the folded draw score keeps the donor's jitter, a unit
  either side of zero (maintainer decision 2026-11-18); `Kernel::draw_spread`
  states it (0 classic, 1 compact) and the three draw anchors read it.
- **Seldepth:** the failure was a bound line printed while the iteration was
  still searching, a stored score having ended its first moves early, not the
  fail-high reduction; the test holds completed lines to their depth.
- **Allocation guard:** the folded tree was too small at depth 13 for the
  test to mean anything (41,888 nodes, 8.7 times depth 5); it searches to
  depth 15 (137,458, 28.6 times), its own commit.
