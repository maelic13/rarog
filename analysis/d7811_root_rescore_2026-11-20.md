# D.7.8.11: the root re-scored as each move is searched — the screen, 2026-11-20

PLAN D.7.8.11, an `R2` card with a zero-game screen whose rule was frozen
in PLAN before the counters ran (`1bbab9f`). Outcome: **`NO_CHANGE`**, the
code reverted, no games.

## What was built

The donor's root picker (Reckless `movepick.rs`, the `NODE::ROOT`
branches, at `0756957`) in Rarog's root list, recipe
`analysis/arm_patches/1bbab9f-d7811-root-rescore.patch` (applies to
`1bbab9f`; SHA-256 `134aab10…d98e`, proved to reproduce the diff after the
revert):

- the noisy moves are scored once the table move's search is done, and the
  noisy moves left are re-scored each time a good one is handed over;
- a noisy move is classed good or bad when it is picked, on the bar of its
  current score and the good moves so far, a failure kept in the order it
  failed;
- the quiets left are re-scored before each quiet is handed over;
- the table move, the pool's searched moves and a helper's rotation keep
  their places above the re-scored tiers; a move outside a narrowed root's
  lines is passed over once it is reached, so it still counts toward the
  bar, the donor's rule.

On tables that do not change between picks the order is the staged
picker's, ties included: the root's existing order tests passed unchanged.
Two new tests, a quiet whose history rose after the first quiet is picked
next, and a capture whose history rose after the table move's search is the
first noisy pick, passed, and both failed with the re-scoring planted off
(the static-order test passing then, as it should). `bench 13` with the
change: 3,369,926 / EBF 2.326, against 3,320,355 / 2.317 (+1.5%).

## The screen

Two `diag` counters over `bench 13` at stride 1 (the diag build's bench
3,369,926 nodes, the release build's figure): every re-score with two moves
or more left in its tier (the noisy re-score after the table move and after
each good noisy move, the quiet re-score before each quiet), and those
whose re-score changes the move the selection step takes next. Output:
`analysis/artifacts/d78-night/rescore/screen_bench13.txt`.

| Counter | Sum over 40 positions |
|---|---|
| `root_rescores` | 33,313 |
| `root_rescore_changes` | 319 |
| `root_iterations` | 520 |

The share is **0.96%** (319 / 33,313), under the frozen 1%: the step closes
`NO_CHANGE`.

**Prediction (frozen in PLAN before the counters ran):** 20%, 80% band 5%
to 45%; `bench 13` moving by under 15% with P 0.7.

**Calibration:** a miss in magnitude, by a factor of twenty. The assumption
that failed is that one root move's subtree moves the root side's history
scores enough to reorder the moves left; the histories trained over the
earlier iterations, and the static terms of the quiet order (escape,
check, threat, offence), dominate what one subtree adds. The bench moved
+1.5%, inside its band: a change at one pick in a hundred reshapes the
trees after it, as tie order did (D.7.8.8), without saying anything about
strength.

**Reading the result.** The share sits close to the line, and `bench 13`
searches to depth 13 where the rated games average about 18; the rule was
set on `bench 13` and is read as frozen. **Retry trigger:** a change that
makes the root's histories move faster between root moves (a root-specific
history such as Stockfish's low-ply history, or D.7.11's fit raising the
history weights of the quiet order), after which this screen is run again
on the recipe.
