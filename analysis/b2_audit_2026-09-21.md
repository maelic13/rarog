- The B.2.4b PGN holds **249** games (W 121); the ledger's 248 (W 120) is
  the 124 complete pairs the test decided on. One game of a 125th pair was
  written after the stop.
- RAR-S75's row registers a 2,000-game fitted-against-unfitted diagnostic
  at the final theta. PLAN B.2.3 replaced it with B.2.4b before the tune
  ended, and B.2.4b scored the prediction, so the whole fit is known to
  ±31 Elo only. RAR-S76 (+118.72 ± 10.62 at 3,900) plus RAR-S77 (+4.43 ±
  2.90) bound it consistently. No games are recommended for it.
- RAR-M59's 10,000-game Colosseum run was **not played and will not be**:
  Colosseum closed its step 10.9m on 2026-09-19 without pursuing the target.
  Post-fix binaries have played 21,055 fastchess games (RAR-S77, B.2.4b)
  without a time loss. Every binary in Colosseum's own qualification runs
  predates `c0e6ef7` (the ratings binary was built at `d69d294`, which does
  not contain it), so they do not test the fix; they lost no game on time
  either: 86,450 games across the nine `colosseum-qual-*` directories, every
  termination `normal`.

The verdict, ratings and recovery results were read from Colosseum's
`docs/architecture/phase-10-qualification.md`, not recounted.

Rarog's run files on the staged CLI (`tools/bin/colosseum-cli.exe`, sha256
`550CE5D0…DA11`, a local build of Colosseum `cli` at `0b78c29`): every file
dry-runs with exit 0; `sprt-default.toml` resolves to normalized `[0, 3]`,
α = β = 0.05, 3,000 ms + 30 ms, 20 ms margin, Hash 64, Threads 1, 14 slots,
placement auto with one core of headroom, per-slot processes, UHO random, no
adjudication; `spsa_config_to_colosseum.py b23core --iterations 5000 --check`
exits 0.

What stands between this and replacing fastchess and weather-factory:

1. `cli-v0.1.0` is not tagged; Colosseum 10.10 is open.
2. The staged binary is neither the qualified one (`da248afc…`, `9f94aa9`)
   nor a release. `setup_tools.ps1` must stage the tagged archive and pin its
   SHA-256.
3. No Colosseum path carries `sprt.ps1`'s and `spsa.ps1`'s guards (idle host,
   bench-verified sidecar, compiler equality, dirty tree, per-run manifest).
4. The manifest parity run and the short live run with a deliberately
   mismatched sidecar are not done.
5. `spsa` is the least proven part for Rarog's use: qualified on four
   coordinates at `r_end` 0.03, where two of four missed the per-coordinate
   criterion while the full −56 Elo was recovered. No 82-coordinate tune at
   `r_end` 0.0031 has been strength-gated on it. B.2.7 carries its own SPRT,
   so it is that test; weather-factory stays installed until B.2.7 gates.
