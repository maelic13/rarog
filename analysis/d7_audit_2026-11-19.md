# D.7 audit: the compact kernel against its donor, RAR-S128's tune, and what D.7 does next (2026-11-19)

Asked by the maintainer 2026-11-19 with RAR-S128's tune paused at iteration
2,000: is the compact kernel correct and close to its donor, was the tune
set up well, what has it gained, and how much is left. This record holds the
findings and their evidence; PLAN D.7 holds the steps they became, and
[RAR-S129](../docs/experiments/RAR-S129.md) the two matches played for it.

## Method

- The node body (`src/search/compact/node.rs`) read against Reckless
  `0756957`'s `search.rs` statement by statement by the session's main
  model; the rules (`rules.rs`) are covered by D.7.4's layer A and were read
  only where a finding led.
- The state that feeds the rules, which layer A cannot see (histories,
  correction tables, picker, quiescence, the table's semantics, the wrapper,
  the root window, SEE), reviewed independently by a second model against
  the donor's source, read-only.
- The tune read from its run directory (`tools/results/d76-fit`) with
  `tools/spsa_read.py` and the session's scripts, and two registered
  matches (RAR-S129).

## The tune (RAR-S128)

- **Sound:** the option wiring is generated from the seed table
  (`tune.rs`, `SEED_FIELDS`), so a name cannot reach another seed; no fault,
  time loss, rail or clamped arm; the reader's replay of the centres from
  the drawn signs agrees to 1e-12; after the first 250 iterations the
  per-iteration spread of wins less losses is the game noise (3.3 to 3.5
  against 6.6 over the first hundred), so no coordinate swamps the others.
- **The start was coarse:** the perturbation is two steps, 18.75% of a
  seed, at iteration 1 (the first iteration scored 25 to 0 with 5 draws)

  coefficient, and the time manager's constants, fitted to the classic

  sparsely, and the seeds that are plies or counts, which a fit moves by

## The score scale (A3), and the decision

Rarog's `score cp` was never calibrated. From the first 700 games of
RAR-S126 (`wdl50.py`, a logistic fit of the mover's win on its score, moves
from ply 16), the classic kernel wins half its games at about +120 with 58
or more points of material on the board, +164 between 30 and 57 and +244
below 30; the unfitted compact kernel at +116, +108 and +204. The fit
moved a scale that already meant different things at different material.

**Decision (maintainer's to the agent, 2026-11-19: "as best we can for
strength"):**

1. *The kernel's scale stays free and lives in the wrapper.* Its two
   coordinates gained +24.9 Elo in 2,000 iterations; a refold would spread
   that one scale over about eighty margins and need redoing after every
   fit, for the same play.
2. *The reported score is calibrated at the boundary* (D.7.12): `score cp`
   is the kernel's score divided by a material-dependent constant fitted
   on the kernel's own games, so +100 is an even chance of winning, the
   convention of Stockfish and of the donor. Mate and tablebase scores pass
   unchanged.
3. *The calibration never feeds back.* Search and time decisions read the
   kernel's score; refitting the calibration cannot change a move or a
   node count.
4. *The time manager's constants join the finishing surface* (D.7.9,
   D.7.11), which fits them to the kernel's scale.
5. *A finer kernel unit is screened once* (D.7.8.10): the fitted wrapper
   truncates the HCE to between half and four fifths of its resolution,
   and the donor works about 3.5 times finer than the fitted kernel.
6. *2026-11-13's "no unit translation stays in the engine"* covers the
   kernel's inside, as it did; the calibration is output only.
7. *Phase F:* training targets record the kernel's score with the
   calibration in force; the wrapper and the calibration are refitted with
   a network, not carried.

## The finishing fit's design

Rule 7c's shape is kept: the perturbation's two steps exist to lower the
floor, and a symmetric difference is unbiased on a curved surface. What
changes is the surface and the start:

- it starts from the baked centres, with ranges by the same rule round
  them;
- A1's scale, quiescence's correction scale, A9's limits, A10's second
  coefficient and the time manager's constants are coordinates;
- a seed that is a ply or a count is not a coordinate: those the first fit
  moved keep their baked values;
- the activation read drops what changes nothing, as before; the curvature
  sweep is not repeated, the first fit having stopped live;
- its time control is set by D.7.10's read.

## Maintainer decisions, 2026-11-19

1. The tune stops at the 2,000 checkpoint and its centres are baked in:
   the new starting point.
2. No gate at this state. The classic kernel is deleted before the
   repairs; if the kernel fails its gate after the finishing fit it is
   discarded and the classic restored from its last commit.
3. Clock use (D.6.28) moves into D.7 before the fit.
4. Rarog's own dropped forms are not retried inside D.7; only a failed
   gate reopens them.
5. Compactness is a secondary aim and blocks nothing a need is shown for.
6. After D.7 every D.6 step is reviewed on the new kernel: does the problem
   exist there, and if it does a solution is discussed before any change.
