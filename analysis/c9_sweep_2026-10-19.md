## Observations, not decisions

- **`ocb` at 24** is the one point beyond 4 SE in the improving direction.
  It is a fifth of the line, in 0.7% of rows: a slightly more drawish
  general opposite-bishop regime. C.5.2's three follow-ups closed the
  opposite-bishop rule on conversion, so this is recorded, not scheduled.
- **The tree is hypersensitive** to coordinates the outcomes do not feel:
  `thr` 150 moves 7.8% of rows by 2.6 cp on average and grows `bench 13` by
  84%. The bench column is a fingerprint, not a quality read, as rule 8
  says.
- **The taper's slope is not the taper's shape.** RAR-E58 and RAR-E59's
  phase slope in how large scores convert is not repaired by bending the
  taper: every bend loses. It stays calibration, for C.10's margins.
