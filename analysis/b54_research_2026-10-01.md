# B.5.4 — optimism, a research card

**Status: card written 2026-10-01; the switch is built, the game read is
registered (RAR-S92) and not yet played.** Leaf: PLAN B.5.4 (`R3`). Head:
`dev` at `d754577`, default build **11,171,726 / EBF 2.512**. Raw:
`tools/results/b5-20260930/b54/`.

## The question

Both donors lean every evaluation toward the root's running average score:
when the root's side is ahead, its positions are evaluated a little higher
and the opponent's a little lower, more so with more material on the board.
Stockfish: `optimism = 114·avg/(|avg| + 85)`, blended as
`nnue + (nnue·material + optimism·7675)/91000`; Reckless:
`113·avg/(|avg| + 201)`, blended as `(raw·(21032 + material) +
optimism·(1548 + material))/27015`. Does this help a search whose evaluation
is a classical one, fitted without it?

## Premise, checked

B.0 wrote "optimism: B.5 (producer) with B.2's consumer slot". The slot does
not exist: `core/correction.rs`'s `corrected_eval_parts` scales by material,
damps toward the fifty-move horizon and adds the correction, nothing else.
So the card is a hypothesis about a donor feature with no evidence in this
engine, and the rule for that is the cheapest game read, not an argument.

## What was built (`d754577`)

A switch `CoreOptimism` (default 0, behaviour-neutral: the default build
reproduces 11,171,726) and two coordinates, `CoreOptimismScale` 52 and
`CoreOptimismDiv` 92, Reckless's 113 and 201 at the measured 0.457 ratio
between the two evaluations' scales. The producer runs at the root at every
iteration's start, from the running average of the completed root scores
that the soft stop already keeps (zero until the first iteration completes);
the consumer is the corrected evaluation, which adds
`optimism·(1200 + material)/21000` for the side to move, the same two
weights as Reckless's (0.44 of the optimism at the start position, 0.06
with no pieces) on this material scale. Multi-PV searches leave it at zero,
so B.2.0.2's contract holds by construction. Per thread; reset per search.

## Evidence at zero games (recorded, not used to decide)

`bench 13` on the tune build: off 11,171,726; on **14,387,454** (+29% nodes
to the same depth); `CoreOptimismScale` 26 and 104 read 16.9 M and 12.1 M,
`CoreOptimismDiv` 46 and 184 read 10.7 M and 11.6 M, so the switch and both
coordinates are live. WAC at 100k: off 232, on 234. Per interior node with
the switch on: reverse futility at depth 1–3 −9.3%, razoring −21.5%, ProbCut
−10%, LDSE −11%, history pruning +20%, singular attempts +12%, aspiration
failures −25% (a steadier root score), estimate refinements +18%. Optimism
moves the estimate every margin reads, which is why B.2 to B.4's fitted
margins cannot be assumed to be centred for it. B.5.3 and B.5.5 showed that
none of these numbers predicts Elo; they are here so the game read can be
explained afterwards, not to rank anything.

## Hypotheses

- **H1:** the donors' gain transfers: +3 ± 10 Elo at `3+0.03`.
- **H2:** a classical evaluation fitted without optimism is biased by it
  and the fitted margins are de-centred: −5 or worse.
- **H3:** undecidable at 2,000 games, as draw-score randomisation was.

## Interaction map

| Producer | Consumer | Shared signal |
|---|---|---|
| root average score | every corrected evaluation | the estimate `est` and every margin B.2–B.4 fitted on it; the quiescence stand pat; the correction tables train on the residual to a biased eval (Reckless accepts this) |
| optimism | the TT | the raw eval stored is unbiased; the bias is applied at read, as the donors do |
| optimism | aspiration | a steadier root score (fails −25%) narrows the loop's work; B.5.5 showed the loop is at its optimum without it |
| optimism | helper threads | each thread computes its own from its own average; no shared state |

## The read (RAR-S92) and its rule

One 2,000-game `match-fixed` on one tune build (`rarog-b54-tune.exe`),
`CoreOptimism=1` as side A against the defaults, RAR-S80's rule: at or above
+5 Elo with the lower bound above −5 the card ends `READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION`
and a `[0,3]` SPRT of the switch baked on (PGO) against the head is
registered, with the two coordinates as B.6 candidates; between −5 and +5 it
closes `NO_CHANGE` at this budget and the switch stays 0 for B.8; at or
below −5 it closes `NO_CHANGE`.

## Prediction (frozen 2026-10-01, before any game)

**+1 ± 10 Elo**; adopted 0.2; at or below −5 0.25 (H2 has a real chance on
a classical evaluation).
