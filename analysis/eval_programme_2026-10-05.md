## 2. The question, and what could explain the gap

**Question.** With the search frozen, which changes to the evaluation's
representation gain playing strength, in what order, and how much of the
recorded same-search gap is there to gain?

| | Hypothesis | What it predicts | Status after today |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | **Structure** (PLAN's premise): Stockfish conditions the same concepts more strongly, family by family | donor families add outcome information outside Rarog's span | supported for king safety; weak for threats, passers, pawns; absent for mobility, pieces, material, space |
| H2 | **Calibration and data**: the surface is adequate, its fit or its labels are not | Rarog's fitted score predicts its own outcomes at least as well as an unfitted donor | **contradicted in sign**: Stockfish alone is 2.17% better on Rarog's own distribution. A share is calibration all the same: a monotone rescaling of Rarog's score recovers 0.97%, and the slope against outcome differs by phase |
| H3 | **Instrument**: much of the 329 is throughput, a stale evaluation and a search fitted to the other evaluation | the equal-node gap on the current evaluation is well under 329 | open; RAR-O05 |
| H4 | **Endgame knowledge and scaling** | the donor's edge sits in the total, in endgames, not in regular families | supported: +7.33% below phase 32 against +1.36% for the families there; but 1.2 of the 3.6 points are positions of six men or fewer |

Search effects that can masquerade as evaluation effects, in both
directions. Stockfish's search is fitted to Stockfish's evaluation, so the
oracle understates what Rarog's evaluation is worth in a search fitted to
it. Rarog's B.9 search is fitted to Rarog's evaluation: its cp-valued
margins sit on this scale, its correction history learns the evaluation's
systematic error, and it rescales the raw evaluation by material
(`correction.rs:176–184`). An evaluation change therefore meets a headwind
in its own gate (RAR-E12: `bench 13` +12.3% from one fitted table) and can
be partly duplicated by correction history. Phase C's gates measure
evaluation gain minus that mismatch until C.10.

Tooling and instrument effects on record: the FFI throughput cost (RAR-O01,
RAR-O03); adjudication, which moved one pair by 74 Elo between RAR-O01 and
RAR-O02; the `passer` cohort that once selected 127,777 of 127,778 positions
(RAR-E09); and, new today, the lazy path (section 12).

## 4. The same-search figure as an instrument: RAR-O05

Three things separate RAR-O02's 328.6 from "what the evaluation is worth":

- **Evaluation revision.** The hybrid ran the 2.3.2 evaluation. RAR-E06,
  RAR-E08 and RAR-E12 have added about 40 Elo since, measured in Rarog's
  search (RAR-O03's registration makes the same deduction).
- **Throughput.** Control 2.3 Mnps, hybrid 1.5 (RAR-O02); 1.64 with the
  2.4.0 evaluation (RAR-O03); 2.31 against 1.39 on one middlegame position
  in today's wire check. That is 0.5 to 0.7 doublings. RAR-S98 prices
  1% NPS at about 2 Elo on this engine at `3+0.03`; taken as an upper guide
  for another search, and about half that at a conventional 70 Elo a
  doubling, the handicap is of the order of 35 to 85 Elo. Part of
  it is the adapter (an instrument artefact), part is Rarog's evaluation
  costing more per call (real, and Phase C's speed requirement).
- **Sample.** About 205 games in the pair.

Not separable by any arm of this instrument: how much of the gap is
Stockfish's search being fitted to its own evaluation's scale and shape.

**RAR-O05** (registered, wire-checked, maintainer-run): the oracle package
on both sides, `Use Rarog HCE` false against true, 1,000 games at 150,000
nodes a move and 1,000 at `3+0.03`. Frozen prediction for Stockfish's
evaluation over Rarog's: **+215** at equal nodes (80% band 160 to 265),
**+285** at equal time (240 to 330). Frozen use: the equal-node figure
becomes Phase C's meter baseline; at 150 or more the programme's premise
stands; under 100 the family clusters are re-scoped with the maintainer
before C.3 is built. What it cannot say: the equal-node gap is inside
Stockfish's search, and how much of it Rarog's search can use is answered
only by C's own gates.

## 5. The donor-direction screen: RAR-E17 and RAR-E18

**Method.** A fitted evaluation's features are uncorrelated with its own
outcome residual; that is the fit's first-order condition, and it is why
RAR-E09 found nothing. A second evaluator's term family is a direction
outside that span. `tools/diag/donor_residual.py` scores each position
three times (Rarog's evaluation and Stockfish `9587eeeb`'s total through the
oracle binary, and Stockfish 11's per-term trace), then compares held-out
logistic models of the game outcome: Rarog's score alone against Rarog's
score plus the donor's total or one donor family. Two folds, totals clipped
at 2,000 cp, a shuffled family as the null control (it reads 0.000%).
Corpus: `hce-v3-tb/validation.csv`, 194,444 quiet positions from 8,000-node
self-play of the RAR-E08 head, labels Syzygy-corrected at six men or fewer.

**Readings** (gain in percent of Rarog's held-out squared error, ± one
standard error; every model refitted inside its cohort, RAR-E18):

| Cohort (rows) | Stockfish alone | + Stockfish total | + all families | + king | + winnability | Rarog rescaled |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| all (194,444) | +2.17 ± 0.16 | +3.59 ± 0.09 | +1.22 ± 0.05 | +0.66 ± 0.04 | +0.12 ± 0.01 | +0.97 ± 0.05 |
| phase ≥ 96 (43,017) | +1.89 | +2.19 | +1.95 | +0.71 | +0.03 | +0.07 |
| phase 32–95 (50,797) | +2.55 | +3.25 | +2.24 | **+1.58** | +0.03 | +0.12 |
| phase < 32 (100,630) | +5.77 | **+7.33** | +1.36 | +0.09 | +0.91 | +2.75 |
| men ≥ 7 (160,796) | +1.43 | +2.73 | +1.62 | +0.76 | +0.02 | +0.46 |
| men ≤ 6 (33,648) | +29.46 | +29.69 | +7.70 | +0.01 | +6.84 | +15.12 |
| \|score\| ≤ 500 (139,678) | +2.19 | +3.24 | +2.00 | +0.80 | +0.09 | +0.24 |

The other families, over all rows (RAR-E17): threats +0.07, passed +0.06,
pawns +0.04, space +0.02, mobility +0.01, pieces +0.00, material and
piece-square tables −0.02. Their best cohorts: space +0.15 at phase ≥ 96,
mobility +0.11 at phase 32–95, threats +0.20 and passed +0.16 without
queens.

**What it says.**

- King safety is the one regular family with a large donor-direction
  residual, and it is where it should be: middlegames with queens.
- The opening band's families together reach +1.95% where king alone gives
  +0.71%, with no other single family above +0.15%. The remainder is joint:
  it appears only when the families enter together.
- Below phase 32 the total adds 7.3% and the families 1.4%. In squared-error
  units the 3.6% over all rows is about 2.6 points from seven men or more
  and 1.2 from six or fewer. At six men or fewer, half of the donor's edge
  is the size it gives a win (Rarog scores a won KR-K at 4 to 7 pawns
  against a label of 1.0); the rest is exact knowledge. Above six men the
  total still adds about 1.1 points beyond the families and beyond
  rescaling. The winnability term itself adds almost nothing there, so the
  candidate is the scale factor, which the trace does not print.
- Stockfish's total alone beats Rarog's fitted score on Rarog's own games in
  every cohort. Rarog's slope against outcome also differs by phase
  (RAR-E17's phase-slope model: +1.07% at phase ≥ 96, −0.75% below 32, from
  one global fit).

**Correction, 2026-10-06 (RAR-E22).** The per-family trace above is
Stockfish 11's while the total is `9587eeeb`'s. With `9587eeeb`'s own
families (`tools/diag/donor_terms_9587.py`), `rarog+all_families` reads
+1.85% over all rows and **+2.73% at seven men or more, equal to
`rarog+stockfish`**: the "about 1.1 points beyond the families" in the
third bullet was the version mixture, not scaling knowledge, and the scale
factor measured directly as a direction adds +0.09% there (RAR-E21). The
king reading and the six-men-or-fewer reading stand. The table and the
bullets are preserved as measured.

**Limits, all stated before the runs.** The layer is static outcome
prediction: it ranks questions and refutes; it is not Elo and accepts
nothing. The positions are ones Rarog's play reached, and the labels are
what the pre-B search realised at 8,000 nodes. The per-family trace is
Stockfish 11's while the total is `9587eeeb`'s. Standard errors treat rows
as independent; rows from one game share a label. Rarog's score is the
*played* evaluation, lazy path included.

## 7. Shared inputs and interactions

**Computed today.** Attack maps (`attacked_by[2][6]`, `attacked[2]`,
`attacked2[2]`, `attacks_from_sq[2][64]`) are built once, at 1649–1697
inside `eval_piece_activity`, and read by mobility, the bishop
long-diagonal term, threats, king safety (`KsMaps`, 1106) and hanging
pieces. The pawn cache (16,384 entries) stores the key, net mg/eg, both
passed sets and both pawn-attack sets. The evaluation cache (32,768
entries) stores the final value. There is no material table: phase,
imbalance counts and every recogniser's exact-material test are recomputed
on each cache miss.

**Recomputed.** Enemy attacks on passer stop and path squares
(`is_attacked_by_with_occ`, 1581 and 1603, before the maps exist); rook
attacks at 1829 and bishop attacks at 2836; queen rays at 2190–2191; two
different king zones (1726 and 2405); the three-file flank mask three times
(2465, 2512, 2579); passed pawns again in `count_passed_pawns` (3400).

**Plan.** C.1 creates the single producer (`eval/attacks.rs`) with exactly
today's contents and consumers. What later clusters add goes through it or
the pawn cache, and each addition states its per-node cost before it is
built (PLAN's speed requirement):

| Input | Producer | Consumers | Added by |
|---|---|---|---|
| attack maps, double attacks, per-square attacks | `eval/attacks.rs` | mobility, threats, king, hanging, passers' path safety | C.1 (as today); passers read it instead of recomputing when C.6 opens |
| mobility area | `eval/attacks.rs` | mobility, and any term that needs "safe squares" | C.1 (as today) |
| king ring, attackers count and weight, attacks on king-adjacent squares | `eval/attacks.rs`, filled in the piece loop | king safety | C.3, if C.0.4's card selects them |
| shelter and storm by king square and castling rights | pawn cache | king safety | C.3, if selected |
| passer count, blocked-pawn count, pawn-attack span | pawn cache | winnability, scale, outposts, space | C.5.2 first |
| material signature → phase, imbalance, recogniser dispatch | a material table | `evaluate`, `scale_endgame`, C.5's dispatcher | C.5.1 decides; it removes per-miss recomputation and gives the recognisers one key |

**Order and partial sums** (the contract C.1 must keep): cache probe;
material, piece-square and phase; `eval_pawns`; `eval_passed_pawn_advance`;
the lazy gate, which reads the tapered running sum (1322); then either
mop-up alone or `eval_piece_activity`, mop-up, imbalance; tempo; taper;
`scale_endgame`. Three reads of a running sum: the lazy gate, the mop-up
(`(mg+eg)/2`, 2223, before imbalance and tempo) and the initiative term
(the sign of the running eg, 2908, before mop-up and imbalance).

**Evaluation to search.** Every cp-valued margin (reverse futility,
razoring, futility, null-move, ProbCut, SEE pruning, aspiration) sits on
the current scale; correction history learns the current evaluation's
error; `CoreEvalMaterialScale` rescales the raw evaluation; the search
applies rule-50 damping; the TT stores the static evaluation; `LazyMargin`
is a search parameter that changes the evaluation function. Each cluster's
record therefore carries its `bench 13` node and EBF change beside its
gate, and a candidate whose tree grows by several percent is reported as
"evaluation gain minus search mismatch", not as the family's value. The
mismatch is retuned once, at C.10.

## 12. Defects and observations in the current evaluation

Confirmed by reading the source:

1. **The lazy path omits imbalance and the bishop pair** (`evaluate`,
   1326–1334). Above the gate the engine evaluates material, piece-square
   tables, pawns, passer advance and mop-up only. When the shortcut was
   written imbalance was seeded at zero; RAR-E06 and RAR-E12 fitted it to
   piece-value-sized amounts (`imbalance_ours`' queen row reads 3, 107, −98,
   −63, −130, −97). The `texel` build disables the shortcut, so every fit
   describes a function the engine does not play above the gate, and the
   played function steps by up to several hundred cp across it. Owner:
   C.0.3.
2. **The unstoppable-passer test is one tempo generous** (2107–2109). It
   requires `king_steps > pawn_steps − (0 if the defender is to move, else
   1)`; the rule of the square needs `king_steps > pawn_steps + (1 if the
   defender is to move, else 0)`. With a pawn on a7, the defending king on
   b7 and the pawn's side to move, the term fires and Kxa8 follows the
   promotion. The fitted weight has adapted to the term as defined, so the
   repair is a definition change plus a refit. Owner: C.6; cheapest check: a
   tablebase-labelled set of pawn endings.

Read in the same pass and **not verified** (each is a question for its
owner, not a finding):

3. The generic mop-up switches on as a step of 28 to 82 cp of eg at
   `|(mg+eg)/2| > 200` in any material, middlegames included (2262–2267).
4. `eval_initiative` takes its sign before mop-up and imbalance and can
   only push eg away from zero (2908).
5. The evaluation cache requires an equal halfmove clock though the
   evaluation no longer reads the clock (1254); this only lowers the hit
   rate.
6. Thirty imbalance slots are never read (the upper triangles), six cancel
   (the `imbalance_theirs` diagonal), and `pawn_connected[0,1,7]` cannot
   fire.
7. The bishop pair is priced three times (`bishop_pair_*`,
   `imbalance_ours[0]`, `bishop_pair_pawn_*`).
8. `rook_trapped` needs the king on e1/e8 and cannot fire in the usual
   castled-king pattern (1825–1827); its weights are 0.
9. Storm is not limited to pawns ahead of the king (2587–2599); backward
   and isolated penalties stack (1516).
