# D.2 audit, 2026-11-02: the four-thread card's evidence re-read

An independent re-read of PLAN D.2, its experiment records (RAR-M74 to
RAR-M80, RAR-P46 to RAR-P53, RAR-R29 to RAR-R33), the Basilisk cross-check's
placements and the raw runs behind them, asked for by the maintainer when the
leaf stopped progressing. Nothing was built and no game was played; one
engine output check of a few milliseconds confirmed the `info` line format.
The scripts and logs are in `analysis/artifacts/d2-audit/` (ignored):
`pgn_side_depth.py` (`4f08cfec…`), `replay_fidelity_nonmate.py`
(`d297c9dc…`), `pgn_side_depth.log` (`76021f84…`),
`replay_fidelity_nonmate.log` (`f944bf02…`).

## 2. RAR-M78's verdict falls with its confounded leg

RAR-M79 showed the same configuration at R 3.44 where RAR-M78's leg ran at
2.48, and recorded the leg as confounded; the audit agrees. What follows was
not drawn: the deficit verdict rested on that leg alone, so **"Rarog
deficient" is not established**, and the card's research question is open
again rather than answered.

Frozen prediction for a clean rerun of the threads leg at R about 3.4, from
the odds leg's 101.8 Elo a doubling: the four-thread side gains
ln(3.44 / 2.48) / ln 2 = 0.47 doublings over the confounded leg, so **G4
+122 ± 12, D 1.2, X 2.3**, level with Reckless (2.30) and inside Stockfish's
error (2.48). G4 at about +85 or below reads deficient against Reckless
again (the registered rule's upper bound), and then the lead of section 4 is
the engine's own target. Between those the read is unresolved and a second
night decides.

## 3. The host state is the blocker, and it is unidentified

What is established:

- Four-thread processes alone lose about a third of their speed, flat for
  hours, and no one-thread side ever did: RAR-M74 (2.60 against B.9's 3.72
  on another day), RAR-P46 (5 of 12 passes, 100 ms searches), RAR-M78 (a
  whole 1,000-game leg at 2.48, flat from the first game), RAR-M79 `solo`
  (2.69, fifty minutes after `on` read 3.44). Healthy: 3.44 to 3.53 in games
  (RAR-M79), 3.57 to 3.66 pinned alone (RAR-R29, RAR-P52), 3.78 alone in the
  slot (RAR-R31).
- The head's processes only, so far. B.9 read 3.28 and 3.72 on two days, and
  Reckless and Stockfish 3.45 to 3.47 in one session each; none has been
  seen in a slow session, but none has been exposed to one either.
- RAR-M79's `off` leg ran in the healthy state, so it cannot clear large
  pages for the slow one (the record and D.8 said it did; corrected).
- In the slow state the main thread loses about twice the depth its NPS loss
  predicts: RAR-M78 1.05 plies against about 0.6 expected (0.35 from the
  speed, 0.15 from X, 0.09 from the own time), `solo` 0.93 against about 0.5.
  A uniform slowdown of every thread would not do that; the main thread is
  hit harder than the average, or intermittently.
- The host runs a custom power plan ("ChrisTitus - Ultimate Power Plan",
  minimum and maximum processor state 100%); its core-parking and boost
  settings are at their defaults in the registry, which the audit did not
  resolve to values.

Candidates that predict R near 2.5 and a one-thread side untouched: two
threads sharing a physical core (the slot is eight logical CPUs on four
cores; a parked core or a scheduler state packs the threads), and a parked or
down-clocked core under the four-thread process. Neither has been observed;
every read so far measured R and inferred.

The instrument is passive and costs nothing: `typeperf` at one-second
intervals over the slot's logical CPUs (`\Processor Information(n,m)\%
Processor Time`, `Parking Status`, `% of Maximum Frequency`, `Processor
Frequency`; all exist on this host) and `\Thread(rarog*)\% Processor Time`,
written to a CSV beside the run's manifest, from now on beside every
four-thread leg. The next slow episode then names its mechanism instead of
costing another leg. With it, each leg's per-side in-game R becomes its
validity check: a four-thread Rarog block under 3.0 (Reckless under 3.2) is
recorded and not read.

## 4. The engine-side lead that stands

- Four threads reach a fixed depth with about four times one thread's nodes
  and more slowly than one (RAR-R29, X 0.87 at the head, 0.96 at B.9); the
  helpers' entries reach the main thread (+5.6 points of hits) and do not
  cut its tree (RAR-R33, M 1.09). In games Reckless's and Stockfish's
  four-thread sides reach their one-thread depth at about 1.3 to 1.6 times
  its nodes (section 1's node bins), so their helpers feed the main thread's
  depth and Rarog's do not.
- It predates Phase C, so it is not the C head's regression, and it is
  priced only by the rerun of section 2: Lazy SMP's gain can be width alone,
  and only games say what Rarog's width is worth.
- The mate-score behaviour at four threads (a stop at 7 to 10 plies where
  one thread iterates on) is unexamined; it is H5's domain and decided
  positions, so it waits.

## 5. Reasoning errors, and where they are corrected

1. The runner's depth figures were taken as search depth (RAR-M78, RAR-R29,
   RAR-R30, RAR-R31, the card). Corrected in each record's audit note and in
   PLAN D.2.
2. RAR-M78's leg was taken as a property of its configuration; RAR-M79
   priced that at 0.08 and the host said otherwise. Already recorded.
3. RAR-M79's `off` leg was read as clearing large pages (D.8's order
   paragraph). Corrected in RAR-M79 and D.8.
4. The deficit verdict was not withdrawn when its only leg was. Corrected in
   PLAN D.2 and GUIDE.
5. The zero-game reads themselves (RAR-P52, RAR-P53, RAR-R29, RAR-R30,
   RAR-R32, RAR-R33) are sound; each was registered, predicted and read by
   its rule. RAR-R31's registered readings stand; its fidelity figure is
   re-read above.

## 6. Recommendation

1. **RAR-M80 as registered, with the sampler beside it.** An added passive
   observation; its legs, games, seed and thresholds do not change. Its
   `solo` condition is the one nearest a rating list, so it is worth its 80
   minutes, and a slow episode under the sampler is worth more than its R.
2. **RAR-M81, the maintainer's night:** RAR-M78's two threads legs played
   again, Rarog's and Reckless's, in alternating blocks of 250 games (the
   same binaries, Hash 256, `10+0.1`, three games at a time, the odds legs
   reused), the sampler running, each block's per-side R its validity check,
   read as RAR-M78 with section 2's prediction frozen. One night.
3. **By its outcome.** Not deficient: D.2's four-thread target closes
   `NO_CHANGE`, the host-state note goes to E.2's four-thread arm and every
   rated four-thread read (RAR-M74's 3,433.7 at 4T was likely read in the
   slow state), and scaling beyond four threads stays with G.1. Deficient:
   RAR-R33's next read (the depth and bound of the main thread's hits
   against what its node needs) is the card's first engine question, with
   helper depth staggering and the shared histories (H27) as the competing
   mechanisms, one candidate for the leaf's 4T SPRT.
4. **No engine change before the night read.** Meanwhile the agent works
   the two correctness holds (D.5 item 15, D.4 item 19) and D.3, in the
   register's order.

## 8. Where D.2 stands

Of the card's hypotheses: H1 held small and closed (`e057d84` kept), H2
refuted, H4 a designed behaviour priced only by games, H6 refuted, H7
reshaped into the host state above, H5 unmotivated until the rerun, H3 the
standing lead. The leaf is one night from its decision: either it closes
`NO_CHANGE` for four threads, or it has, for the first time, a measured
target and a mechanism to read. The two weeks since RAR-P46 bought the
instruments (`e057d84`, the time-to-depth and table-hit harnesses, the
replay) and the knowledge that in-game four-thread speed on this host is
not a constant; they did not buy a candidate, and should not have.
