## 1. Verdict

**Four registered floors fail: pooled NPS, WAC at 100k nodes, oracle
agreement at 300k, and the canaries.** Branching and WAC at 400k pass their
floors; branching is inside its window. PLAN B.2.2's rule for a floor failure
is one component ablation, then a re-plan. No ablation order was ever
registered, so the ablation was run as one unordered sweep of all eight bits
(section 4), which leaves no order to choose after seeing the results.
The re-plan and the paired run's place in it are the maintainer's decision
(section 6).

## 4. One ablation sweep

`b2core,ablate,tune` build (non-PGO; node-limited screens do not depend on
speed), one bit at a time. At mask 0 it reproduces the PGO candidate on
all 300 WAC positions at 100k (move, depth and node count), and on WAC.001's
depth record; mask 128 benches 25,183,900 as B.2.1 recorded.

| Arm | WAC 100k | Agreement 300k | Canaries |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| B.1 head | 200 | 40 | 67 |
| candidate | 204 | 35 | 75 |
| − razoring (1) | **217** | 39 | **104** |
| − reverse futility (2) | 198 | 35 | 77 |
| − null move (4) | 206 | 37 | 73 |
| − ProbCut (8) | 213 | **40** | 78 |
| − IIR and hindsight (16) | 205 | 36 | 77 |
| − move-loop pruning (32) | 160 | 34 | 73 |
| − singular (64) | 210 | 39 | 70 |
| − LMR (128) | 204 | 37 | 77 |

**Razoring is the outlier.** Removing it alone raises the canaries from 75
to 104 and WAC from 204 to 217, above the floor, and brings agreement to 39.
ProbCut and singular are next and far smaller on the canaries. Every other
bit leaves the canaries within ±4. This locates where the re-plan should look
first; it is not evidence that removing razoring gains strength (PLAN rule 8),
and at fixed nodes an ablated arm spends its nodes elsewhere, so a bit's row
mixes the mechanism's error with its node savings.

At a fixed depth the candidate searches **more interior nodes and fewer

adds threat and check-info work to each (the B.2.1 review's section 4), so
part of the NPS loss is node mix rather than per-node cost; how much is the
profile's question.

## 6. Speed against quality, and what is owed

The candidate reaches the bench's nominal depth sooner: `bench 13` takes
4,706,910 / 2,183,674 = 2.16 s against 7,601,220 / 3,199,166 = 2.38 s for
B.1 (0.91x, from the interleaved pooled medians). Nominal depth is not equal
work across the arms (section 2.3 of the B.0 analysis). An equal-time proxy on
WAC runs the candidate at 0.683 of each budget: **190 at 68,300 nodes against
B.1's 200 at 100k; 235 at 273,200 against B.1's 237 at 400k.** On this
evidence the better node choice does not yet pay for the per-node cost on
tactics; only the paired run at a clock measures it in games.

P1 (−10 ± 30 Elo unfitted) was made assuming P5's 0.93–0.97x. At 0.683x the
registered paired run's floor (−40, "below is a defect") can no longer
separate a defect from the speed loss: the project's planning constant (about
2 Elo per 1% NPS at STC, stated for identical trees, which these are not)
would put that loss alone at tens of Elo. The run is still the registered measurement and is
prepared (`tools/test_engines/rarog-b22core-pext-pgo.exe` and
`rarog-b22base-pext-pgo.exe`, both built at `02d2c09`, bench-verified,
hash-bound; a four-game smoke of the exact command played normally with
affinity). Also owed: the ETW profile of the candidate the review asked for
before it (`tools/profile/rarog.exe` staged: `b2core`, PEXT, `debug=2`, bench
4,706,910; the script needs an elevated shell).

**Recommendation.** Treat B.2 as returned for a re-plan, starting with
razoring's seeds and shape on this evaluation (section 4), then ProbCut and
singular. Profile before touching speed, so B.7's ticket names measured
costs. The paired run is cheap and answers the speed-against-quality question
directly. If it is played now, read it with the confound above, not against
the −40 defect floor alone. The screens say not to spend B.2.3's SPSA on this
arm as it stands: four floors failed, and a tune would be fitting around
razoring's canary losses and the NPS gap.
