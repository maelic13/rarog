# B.2.2.3 — curvature sweep and the P6 profile on the `b2core` arm

**Status: registration frozen 2026-09-15 before any sweep point was run;
results appended below the line.** Leaf: PLAN B.2.2.3 (class `V`). Ledger:
RAR-S73 (B.2.3's condition and prediction P6). Subject: the `b2core` arm
B.2.2.2 left, engine `308abe9` plus the diag-only instrument `f975eae`,
bench 13 **4,706,910 / EBF 2.391**, the five categorical switches at their
defaults. Condition: analysis/search_programme_2026-09-13.md §9, and PLAN
rule 4 (SPSA is conditional).

## Recipe

- **Binary:** a `b2core,tune` release build (PEXT, not PGO). Node-limited and
  depth-limited counts do not depend on speed. Its bench at the defaults must
  read 4,706,910 before any point is used.
- **Points:** each of the five coordinates at 0.5x, 0.75x, 1x, 1.5x and 2x of
  its default, the others at their defaults, set by UCI option, rounded half
  away from zero. The 1x point is the defaults, shared by all five.

  | Coordinate | Default | 0.5x | 0.75x | 1.5x | 2x | Range |
  |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
  | `CoreRfpLinear` | 50 | 25 | 38 | 75 | 100 | 0..=200 |
  | `CoreLmpSquare` | 1351 | 676 | 1013 | 2027 | 2702 | 256..=4096 |
  | `CoreFpBase` | 150 | 75 | 113 | 225 | 300 | −100..=500 |
  | `CoreLmrQuiet` | 2432 | 1216 | 1824 | 3648 | 4864 | 0..=6144 |
  | `CoreCorrUpdateSlope` | 148 | 74 | 111 | 222 | 296 | 32..=512 |

  Every point is inside its range, so the option setter clamps nothing.
- **Per point:** `bench 13` nodes, and WAC solved at 100,000 nodes by
  `tools/diag/fixed_budget_probe.py nodes 100000 src/wac.epd` with the point's
  option flag (`label=exe|Name=Value`; the probe stops on an option the engine
  rejects, and records the options in its JSON).
- **Classification, per coordinate, on the WAC column `w` over the five
  multipliers:**
  1. *flat* when `max(w) − min(w) ≤ 2`;
  2. otherwise *curved* when the maximum of `w` occurs only at interior
     multipliers (0.75x, 1x or 1.5x), and at neither 0.5x nor 2x;
  3. otherwise *monotone*, with the maximum reached at an edge.

  The bench column is reported beside it as the tree surface. It does not
  enter the classification.
- **Rule (PLAN B.2.2.3):** flat or monotone on all five coordinates sends B.2
  to B.2.4 at the defaults; any curved coordinate sends it to B.2.3 over the
  registered coordinate set, less the five categorical switches.
- **P6:** `bench 13` with `CoreCorrWeightCont2=0 CoreCorrWeightCont4=0`
  against the defaults. P6 (analysis §12) predicted a change of **< 3%**; its
  falsifier is a larger change, meaning a correction consumer is acting as a
  pruning signal. The continuation-correction admissions by remaining depth
  come from `bench 13` on a `b2core,diag` build at stride 1
  (`tools/diag/bench_counters.py`, counters `corr_cont{2,4}_admitted_*`).
  They are reported as shares by depth bucket against Manta's "concentrated
  at low remaining depth".
