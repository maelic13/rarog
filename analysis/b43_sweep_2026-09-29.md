- **Live wires:** the 1× point must reproduce the arm's defaults exactly
  (10,226,874 nodes, WAC 236, depth-14 4.026×), and at least one other point
  of every coordinate must move `bench 13`; a coordinate whose five points
  read one bench is dead, not flat, and is reported as such.
- **Classification, per coordinate, on the WAC column `w` over the five points
  (b223's rule, unchanged):**
  1. *flat* when `max(w) − min(w) ≤ 2`;
  2. otherwise *curved* when the maximum of `w` occurs only at interior points
     (0.75×, 1× or 1.5×), and at neither end;
  3. otherwise *monotone*, with the maximum reached at an end.

  The bench and depth-14 columns are reported beside it as the tree surface;
  they do not enter the classification.
- **Rule (the packet, *B.4.2 record*):** flat or monotone on all seven closes
  the cluster `NO_CHANGE`, with the PV-bit repair re-registered alone; any
  curved coordinate sends B.4 to the twelve-coordinate SPSA (the eight `Qs*`
  plus `CoreRfpSquare`, `CoreRfpLinear`, `CoreRfpConstant`, `CoreRazorBase`),
  registered separately before any game.
- **Predictions, frozen 2026-09-29 in the packet and restated here
  unchanged:** (a) at least four of the seven coordinates curved (0.7);
  (b) at least one of `CoreRfpConstant` and `CoreRfpSquare` curved (0.6);
  (c) the depth-14 node ratio below 3.5× at some swept point of
  `CoreRfpConstant` or `CoreRfpSquare` with WAC at 100k ≥ 232 (0.5).

**What it does not decide.** Fixed-node WAC, bench nodes and the depth-14
ratio are not Elo. A curved coordinate shows that the surface has an interior
the SPSA could find, not that the SPSA will gain.

**Wires.** The shared 1× point reproduces the arm: 10,226,874 nodes, WAC 236,
depth-14 4.026×. Each coordinate's default set by option reproduces
10,226,874, and every coordinate's other four points move `bench 13`, so none
is dead. Every probe and profile recorded its option (the summary asserts the
recorded option equals the point).

**An instrument defect, found by the 1× wire and repaired before any reading
was used.** The first depth-14 leg read 2.70× at the 1× point against the
registered 4.026×. The engine source is the arm's; the difference was the
`diag` feature, whose build ends each search with `info string diag nodes N`
(the interior-node counter), which `tools/branching_profile.ps1`'s node
pattern took as the search's count. The tool now reads search lines only
(`983795f`); after the fix the diag, plain and PGO builds read the same
nodes, and the whole depth-14 leg was re-run (the mis-read files are kept as
`points/*/branching_diagmiscount.json`). Bench (read from its own header) and
WAC (judged by the best move) never used that parser; the classification
reads WAC only, so it is unchanged.

**Classification by the frozen rule:** curved — `QsSeeClampHi`, `QsDeltaMargin`, `QsStandPatLerp`;
monotone — `QsSeeMargin`, `QsFutilityMargin`, `CoreRfpSquare`, `CoreRfpConstant`; none flat, none dead.
`QsSeeClampHi` peaks at 159 (239), `QsDeltaMargin` at 300 (239),
`QsStandPatLerp` at its default 583 (236, with both ends at 230 and 227).
`CoreRfpConstant` reaches its maximum at −72 (242), `CoreRfpSquare` ties at
144 and 288 (236), both counting as an end maximum.

**Decision by the rule:** three coordinates are curved, so B.4 proceeds to the
twelve-coordinate SPSA (RAR-S86).

**Calibration of the frozen predictions:** (a) at least four of seven curved
(0.7): **missed**, three; (b) at least one reverse-futility coordinate curved
(0.6): **missed**, both monotone, with the WAC maximum at `CoreRfpConstant`'s
more negative end; (c) depth-14 ratio below 3.5× at a swept reverse-futility
point with WAC ≥ 232 (0.5): **hit**, `CoreRfpConstant` −27 at 2.72× with WAC
235 and `CoreRfpSquare` 108 at 3.28× with 233, which supports the ablation's
reading that the interpolation's depth-14 cost runs through B.2's reverse
futility and is a fit question.
