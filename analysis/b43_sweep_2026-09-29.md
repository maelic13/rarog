## Recipe

- **Binary:** one `cargo build --release --features diag,tune,b4quiet` at
  `a716366`+ (engine source as `b734caa`), archived as
  `tools/results/b43-sweep/rarog-b43-sweep.exe`, sha256
  `20E3EE42144632C9431D07DBDBDDDC0BAF4865388C385407EDFED545B14F2CB0`,
  `bench 13` **10,226,874 / EBF 2.519** at the defaults, equal to the arm's
  fingerprint. Node- and depth-limited counts do not depend on speed, and a
  `diag` build reproduces its plain build's nodes.
- **Points:** seven coordinates, one moved at a time by UCI option, the others
  at their defaults; the 1× point is the defaults, one set of runs shared by
  all seven. Multiples rounded half away from zero; every point is inside its
  declared range, so the setter clamps nothing. `QsCutoffLerp` is not swept.

  | Coordinate | Default | Range | 0.5× | 0.75× | 1× | 1.5× | 2× |
  |---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
  | `QsSeeMargin` | 265 | 0..=600 | 133 | 199 | 265 | 398 | 530 |
  | `QsSeeClampHi` | 212 | 0..=600 | 106 | 159 | 212 | 318 | 424 |
  | `QsFutilityMargin` | 150 | 0..=400 | 75 | 113 | 150 | 225 | 300 |
  | `QsDeltaMargin` | 200 | 0..=800 | 100 | 150 | 200 | 300 | 400 |
  | `CoreRfpSquare` | 144 | 0..=320 | 72 | 108 | 144 | 216 | 288 |
  | `QsStandPatLerp` | 583 | 0..=1024 | 0 | 292 | 583 | 804 | 1024 |
  | `CoreRfpConstant` | −36 | −100..=100 | −18 | −27 | −36 | −54 | −72 |

  `QsStandPatLerp`'s five points are the registered 0, 292, 583, 804, 1024;
  `CoreRfpConstant`'s are its default times 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, as
  registered.
- **Per point:**
  - `bench 13` nodes: `tools/diag/bench_counters.py --stride 1 --set Name=Value`,
    its header's node count;
  - WAC solved at 100,000 nodes: `tools/diag/fixed_budget_probe.py nodes 100000
    src/wac.epd` with `label=exe|Name=Value` (the probe stops on an option the
    engine does not advertise);
  - the depth-14 node ratio against the oracle: `tools/branching_profile.ps1
    -MinDepth 4 -MaxDepth 14 -Hash 64 -Options Name=Value` on
    `tools/diag/phase4_suite_v1.epd`, its depth-14 cumulative nodes over the
    oracle's (`tools/results/b0-20260913/branching_oracle_sf9587_rarog_hce.json`,
    4,788,062; the arm reads 4.026×, the head 2.859×).
  Every number comes from a script over these outputs (`tools/results/b43-sweep/`).
- **Live wires:** the 1× point must reproduce the arm's defaults exactly
  (10,226,874 nodes, WAC 236, depth-14 4.026×), and at least one other point
  of every coordinate must move `bench 13`; a coordinate whose five points
  read one bench is dead, not flat, and is reported as such.
- **Classification, per coordinate, on the WAC column `w` over the five points
  (b223's rule, unchanged):**
  1. *flat* when `max(w) − min(w) ≤ 2`;
  2. otherwise *curved* when the maximum of `w` occurs only at interior points
     (0.75×, 1× or 1.5×), and at neither end;
  3. otherwise *monotone*, with the maximum reached at an end.

  The bench and depth-14 columns are reported beside it as the tree surface;
  they do not enter the classification.
- **Rule (the packet, *B.4.2 record*):** flat or monotone on all seven closes
  the cluster `NO_CHANGE`, with the PV-bit repair re-registered alone; any
  curved coordinate sends B.4 to the twelve-coordinate SPSA (the eight `Qs*`
  plus `CoreRfpSquare`, `CoreRfpLinear`, `CoreRfpConstant`, `CoreRazorBase`),
  registered separately before any game.
- **Predictions, frozen 2026-09-29 in the packet and restated here
  unchanged:** (a) at least four of the seven coordinates curved (0.7);
  (b) at least one of `CoreRfpConstant` and `CoreRfpSquare` curved (0.6);
  (c) the depth-14 node ratio below 3.5× at some swept point of
  `CoreRfpConstant` or `CoreRfpSquare` with WAC at 100k ≥ 232 (0.5).

**What it does not decide.** Fixed-node WAC, bench nodes and the depth-14
ratio are not Elo. A curved coordinate shows that the surface has an interior
the SPSA could find, not that the SPSA will gain.
