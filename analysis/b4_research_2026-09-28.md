## Decision needed

Which of the card's seven donor-shaped quiescence mechanisms (TT cutoff,
corrected stand pat, fail-high interpolation, LMP at three moves, SEE pruning
by margin, TT write on exit, evasions only in check) still earn a place on
this head, in what form, with what seeds; whether first-ply quiet checks are
owed to B.2's canaries; what the RAR-M19 piece-value scale card decides; and
what B.4.2 must show before the fit and the gate.

## Known evidence

Units: "interior" is the `nodes` counter at the node's counting site (after
the depth-0 dispatch), "qnodes" the quiescence entries after the draw check;
`bench` counts moves made (12,897,901), so the two do not sum to it.

Rarog's quiescence is now **43% smaller per interior node than the
oracle's**, while the oracle reaches five more plies at 300k nodes (median
19 against 14) and solves more of WAC at 100k (242 against 232). The excess
is the interior tree (3.5× at depth 8, 2.86× at depth 14), which B.4 does
not own. So B.4 is not a size cluster: a mechanism that shrinks the
quiescence further has no evidence behind it, and the donors' quiescence is
*bigger* than ours. What B.4 can own is the **quality of what the quiescence
hands back** to the interior (bounds, estimates, the PV bit, ordering
evidence) and the **fit of its margins**, which were last fitted in Phase
7.2 on a different search and were in none of B.2's or B.3's surfaces.

### Manta's measurement of the first-ply-check premise

PLAN's card carries Manta's MAN-S36 finding as a dependency: count-based
skipping, unverified null probes and zero-depth probes assume a quiet mate
threat stays visible one ply later, and a captures-only quiescence made
Manta blind to WAC.001's mate in two. Manta's own follow-up (`PLAN.md`,
"The table, WAC.001 at depth 5" and the third review resolution) measured
the repair: **first-ply quiet checks (component F) alone are
behaviour-correct on the canary and do not rescue either breaking
component**. The traced causes were a late-move count that skipped `Qh7#`
unmade at the *interior* node, and a reverse-futility cut on an unfitted
king-danger evaluation. Rarog's B.2 shipped its move-loop pruning with the
canary rule and its own quiet-check exemptions (futility and the count rule
in quiescence already exempt a checking capture; the interior LMP and
futility exempt direct checks), and the head passes 27 of 37 quiet-check
canaries. Generating checks at the first quiescence ply is therefore **not
owed** by the dependency, would cost nodes where our quiescence is already
the smaller one, and Manta's measurement says it does not buy the safety it
was supposed to. The obligation is carried by the canary rule instead.

## Unknowns

- U1 How much of the interior's `est` quality depends on the quiescence's
  raw fail-soft lower bounds. 21% of refinements come from depth-0 entries;
  the donors interpolate every quiescence fail-high toward beta before
  storing and returning it, so their interior never reads a raw stand-pat
  overshoot as a bound. Whether B.2's fitted margins (RFP 3.13M cuts, 36.7%
  of interior nodes) have absorbed our overshoot is unknown until an arm
  with interpolation is measured against the canaries and the paired run.
- U2 Whether the dead count rule and the threshold's treatment of checking
  captures and recaptures are why the head over-prunes relative to the
  oracle. `QsSeeClampHi = 0` gives +6 WAC at 100k, which says demanding a
  winning capture is wrong often enough to matter; the donors exempt
  checking moves and recaptures (Stockfish) or divide the gap by eight
  (Reckless).
- U3 Whether stopping quiet evasions once a non-losing score exists (both
  donors) loses a mate canary. In-check qnodes are 13.6% of qnodes and try
  57% of their evasions; the saving is bounded, the canary risk is the
  question.
- U4 Whether training noisy history from quiescence cutoffs (Reckless, flat
  +100) helps or dilutes B.2's depth-scaled producer; 423,737 quiescence
  cutoffs against 1,127,091 interior capture cutoffs per bench is a 38%
  volume increase of untyped bonuses.

## Hypotheses

- **H1 (leading):** the quiescence's *outputs* are the defect, not its size:
  raw fail-soft fail-highs stored as bounds and read as `est` by 3% of
  interior nodes, the PV bit dropped on depth-0 stores, and a pruning shape
  (no check or recapture exemption from the SEE threshold, a dead count
  rule, a unit-sensitive delta margin) fitted to a search two clusters ago.
  Interpolation, PV-bit preservation, the donor exemptions and a re-fit of
  the margin surface together measure **+8 ± 8 Elo fitted**.
- **H2 (competing):** B.2 and B.3 were fitted on today's quiescence outputs
  and have absorbed them; changing the bounds' magnitude de-centres their
  margins and the cluster reads +0 ± 5 fitted until B.6's joint fit.
  Discriminated by the paired run with the surface unfitted (H2 predicts it
  at or below zero) and by the gate.
- **H3 (competing, mechanism-local):** the strength is in the pruning shape
  and the fit alone (U2); interpolation and the PV bit are neutral at STC.
  Discriminated by B.4.2's bit ablation of the `b4quiet` components on the
  fixed-node screens and, if the paired run is small and positive, by a
  categorical run.
- **H4 (canary):** evasion pruning loses at least one of the 91 canaries.
  Discriminated at zero games; if true the component is off by default.

**The reverse-futility crossover, quiescence edition.** RFP cuts 36.7% of
interior nodes on `est ≥ beta + margin`. When `est` is a quiescence Lower
bound (237,458 per bench), it is the *raw* stand pat or cutoff score of a
child that failed high at a depth-0 window; the donors never let that value
into the table unmixed. If H2 is right, B.2's RFP margin has grown to cover
the overshoot and interpolation will read as a small loss until B.6; the
paired run with the surface unfitted is where that shows first, and it is
why P1 is wider than B.3's.
