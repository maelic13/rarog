# HCE audit, 2026-10-15

A read of every module under `src/eval/` on the C.8 head (`fcc19a2`, engine
`75c3db5`, 14,126,183 / EBF 2.557), with two mechanical sweeps over the
194,444 held-out rows of `hce-v6-tb/validation.csv`. It answers the
maintainer's question whether the classical evaluation hides a defect or a
large untried lever before C.9 to C.12, and it is the evidence behind PLAN
C.13. Evidence: `analysis/artifacts/c13audit/` (the sweep test, the
frequency script, their logs, `source.txt`).

## Method

- Every term read against its stated contract and, where it follows the
  donor's shape, against the donor's definition (mechanism only; no value
  crossed).
- **Colour-symmetry sweep:** `evaluate(fen) == evaluate(mirror(fen))` over
  all 194,444 rows, with the mirror of `tests/eval_invariants.rs` (colour
  flip, vertical flip, side to move, castling, en passant). 0.8 s in release.
- **Frequency count** (`hce_audit_freq.py`, python-chess) of the cases the
  definitional findings concern, per side and row.

## Findings

### Bug: connected rooks are colour-asymmetric with three or more rooks

`eval_piece_activity` pairs the lowest and highest rook squares
(`lsb`/`msb`). With three rooks a vertical flip picks a different pair, so
the bonus (9 mg, 47 eg) appears on one colour only. The sweep found **4 of
194,444 rows** asymmetric, all with three black rooks, by 31 to 32 cp; with
`rook_connected_mg/eg` zeroed through the `tune` build's parameter file the
sweep is clean (0 of 194,444), so this is the only asymmetry the corpus
reaches. Ten side-rows in the corpus have three rooks. Repair: any aligned
pair with nothing between; bench expected unchanged (verify), the sweep as
the targeted check.

### Definitions that miss their common case (measured, not yet valued)

| Term | Engine's definition | Measured | Donor's shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| Passed pawn | no enemy pawn in the three files ahead; an own pawn ahead on the file is ignored | **6,832 of 207,961 passers (3.3 %)** have an own pawn ahead and score as full passers | the bonus is halved when a pawn stands on the stop square or the pawn would not be passed after a push |
| Trapped rook | king on its home square, all castling rights gone, rook in a home corner, mobility ≤ 3 | fires **173** times; the donor's condition **8,603** | rook mobility ≤ 3 and the king on the home rank between the rook and the centre, more without castling rights |
| Outposts | relative rank ≥ 5, no upper bound | rank-4 outposts, excluded, are the most common: **26,472**, against 22,250 on rank 5, 6,418 on 6, 1,242 on 7 and 197 on 8 | ranks 4 to 6 |
| Initiative | `sign(eg) · (pawns + king-file distance + both flanks)`, one weight (fitted 1) | the form has no intercept and no clamp, so it cannot say that a simple position is drawish; the weight is inert | an intercept of −110, passers, outflanking, infiltration, almost-unwinnable, and `max(complexity, −|eg|)` so the sign never flips |

The trapped-rook and initiative weights are what a fit gives a feature that
rarely fires or cannot express its idea; none of the four is a crash or a
sign error. Their value is unknown: RAR-E57 read the donor's pieces rows at
under +0.06 %, so the outpost and trapped-rook repairs are expected small;
the passer and initiative repairs have no static read yet. C.13.2 reads them
before anything is built.

### Inert terms still computed every evaluation

Fitted to zero in every half: `space_weight`, `space_piece_mg`,
`space_behind_piece_mg`, `rook_trapped_*`, `pawn_lever_*` (frozen),
`passed_candidate_*`, `pawn_isolated_*`; near zero: `hanging_*` (0, 1, 1).
Zero is a legitimate fit outcome; the cost is speed and parameter noise.
Removing a zero-weight term is behaviour-neutral (the fingerprint proves
it), so this belongs to C.12, with C.13.2's inventory of firing rates and
mean |contribution| per term as its list.

### Observations with no action

- The king-danger inputs reproduce the donor's 500-position fixture exactly
  (`king_danger_inputs_reproduce_the_donor_fixture`); shelter and storm
  reproduce theirs. The one deliberate omission is the donor's king-flank
  attack terms, which RAR-E21 measured as not carrying the residual.
- The KRP-KR, KRP-KB, KQ-KP, KR-KP, KBP wrong-corner and KPK recognisers
  match their references line by line; C.5 measured them in play.
- The lazy gate is part of the fitted function (the texel trace sees it), the
  mop-up and the endgame scale enter the tuner's deltas, the pawn and
  shelter caches are keyed by everything they read.
- Material: knight 392 → 261, bishop 420 → 317 and queen 1,140 → 952 fall
  from middlegame to endgame, the opposite of the donor's piece values. Not a
  bug: mobility tables carry endgame value (knight up to 86, rook 115, queen
  125 eg), and the sum is what the fit sees. Worth knowing when reading
  RAR-E58's Material row.
- The evaluation cache is keyed by the halfmove clock the evaluation does not
  read: lost hits only (C.12).
- The symmetry test's ten seeds did not reach the three-rook case; a sweep
  over a frozen sample of corpus rows will (C.13.1).

## What the review says about the question asked

The code is sound where it is reached: one small asymmetry, four
definitions that miss their common case, a handful of inert terms. Nothing
here is a hidden 50 Elo. The large untried levers are not in the terms but
in the fitting target (pure outcomes; RAR-E04's blended-label result was
judged by a static loss that cannot judge a target change, and its retry
trigger fired with Phase C's representation) and in instruments that read
interactions and the evaluation's own search residual; PLAN C.13 holds
them.
