# C.13.5 — definition cards: material × men and mobility × men

Research cards for C.13.5's two candidates, written at `R2` (maintainer
decision 2026-10-15 to escalate the cards from `I1`). They state each
candidate's evidence, mechanism, competing explanations, interactions,
invariants, the cheapest test that can kill it, and the build that follows
only if it survives. Nothing here is built. Evidence: RAR-E63, RAR-E64 and
RAR-E65 (`analysis/artifacts/c133/`, `c134cal/`, `c134/`).

## Decision needed

Whether either candidate is `READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION`. If so, in what form
it enters C.13.5's single build, refit and gate. If neither survives its
kill test, C.13.5 closes `NO_CHANGE`.

## Known evidence

- **RAR-E63** reads the residual of Rarog's played score with depth-2
  boosted trees per pair of inputs, against same-capacity single-input
  trees, on the 144,537 non-lazy held-out rows of `hce-v6-tb`.
  - Two pairs pass 0.10% at 4 game-clustered SE above a 20-replicate
    permutation null (+0.024%) and the phase-slope guard: **material × men**
    (+0.151 ± 0.023%) and **mobility × men** (+0.103 ± 0.017%).
  - Imbalance × phase failed the guard (C.7's calibration).
  - The inputs are tapered family contributions, oriented by the score's
    sign. "Material" is `mg_val`/`eg_val` alone; the piece-square tables
    are their own family.
- **RAR-E64:** with score × phase, score × men and score × pawns slopes all
  in the base, both survive: material × men +0.122 ± 0.021% (5.8 SE),
  mobility × men +0.102 ± 0.017% (6.0 SE, 0.002 points over the line). A
  monotone calibration of the score by the men or pawns left does not
  explain them.
- **RAR-E63's descriptive table** (post hoc, mean oriented residual in
  outcome points by quartile and men band):
  - *Material:* at 25–32 men the largest material edges overclaim
    (−4.27, 1,438 rows). At 12 men or fewer the third quartile overclaims
    (−1.94) and the top quartile underclaims (+1.86).
  - *Mobility:* more mobility is worth more than its price at 25–32 men
    (−3.39 → −1.59 from the lowest quartile to the highest) and less at
    12 men or fewer (+0.30 → −0.92).
- **RAR-E65** compares the static score with the head's own 8,000-node
  search on the same rows. In 11 material-signature buckets (9.6% of rows,
  mostly endgame imbalances) the search departs from the static score by
  100 to 160 cp: less impressed by a queen or rook up, more by a minor or
  rook with pawns. Those corrections barely predict outcomes (+0.046%), so
  they look like transient material, an exchange or promotion in progress
  in a row the quiet filter kept.
- **RAR-E59** (C.7): on the played score, the phase slope among decided
  scores was carried mostly by game length (plies to the game's end).
- Men counts every piece and pawn, kings included. Training-row quartiles
  (`hce-v6-tb/train.csv`, 3,500,000 rows): 9, 16, 22. On the held-out
  non-lazy rows men correlates 0.933 with the pawn count and 0.905 with the
  engine's phase.

## What the engine computes today

- **Phase:** piece-based (`PHASE_W`: N = B = 1, R = 2, Q = 4, capped at 24).
  It ignores pawns, so men carries the pawn count, which the taper does not.
- **Material:** `mg_val`/`eg_val` per piece and the piece-square tables,
  tapered, before the lazy gate.
- **Imbalance:** SF's quadratic form over (bishop pair, pawns, knights,
  bishops, rooks, queens): `imbalance_ours` and `imbalance_theirs`, products
  of counts, phase-independent, after the gate. Any product of a material
  edge with a count of pieces or pawns is a quadratic form in the counts,
  so **the imbalance can already express every bilinear material × men
  effect, and the fit prices it.** A new term earns its place only if its
  form is not a polynomial in the counts.
- **Mobility:** per piece, the count of attacked squares in the mobility
  area, scored through one table per piece type and phase half (`mob_*`),
  after the gate. The tables' mg values are summed per side into
  `mobility_mg`, which king danger reads as the mobility lead
  (`kd_mobility`).
- **Closedness:** rammed pawns × knights or rooks, mg only. It is the one
  existing pawn-structure-dependent price on piece activity.

## Hypotheses

- **H-mat (term):** the value of a material edge depends on the men left in
  a way the taper (piece-based) and the quadratic imbalance (bilinear) cannot
  express. Large edges overclaim in crowded positions and underclaim in
  sparse ones.
- **H-mob (term):** mobility's value depends on the men left (mostly
  pawns) beyond its tables' taper. It is worth more in crowded positions and
  less in sparse ones.
- **H-len (calibration):** both are game length. With many men, more plies
  remain, so any static edge is less decisive (RAR-E59's mechanism). A score
  × men slope does not capture it, because the effect is in which edge, not
  in the score's size.
- **H-trans (transient):** both are transient material and activity. Rows
  with an exchange or promotion in progress (RAR-E65's large departures)
  carry big static edges and big mobility swings that the game does not keep.
- **H-imb (already representable):** the effect is bilinear, and the
  imbalance would carry it with different fitted values. The trees found it
  only because they are nonlinear. This is a fitting question, not a term.

## Interaction map

| Mechanism | Shares with material × men | Shares with mobility × men |
|---|---|---|
| Phase taper | the material values' mg/eg split | the mobility tables' mg/eg split |
| Imbalance | counts, post-gate, the same material edge | — |
| Endgame scale and C.5.2's rule | they scale the whole score in specific endings, after the sum | the same |
| Lazy gate (`LazyMargin`, C.10) | a pre-gate term would move the gate's decisions; the term goes after the gate | already post-gate |
| Mop-up | reads `(mg+eg)/2` after piece activity; a post-gate material term shifts it | the same |
| King danger | — | must not see the new term (`mobility_mg` stays table-only) |
| Closedness, threats (restricted) | — | other prices of activity by structure |
| RAR-E65's transient material | the same buckets | mobility swings in the same positions |

## Card 1 — material × men

- **Mechanism:** a material edge's outcome value relative to its tapered
  price varies with the men left, not monotonically, and not as a product of
  counts.
- **Evidence it is active:** RAR-E63 and RAR-E64 above; nothing else.
- **Form, if it survives:** a band-wise material-edge correction.
  - `Δ` is the material edge in conventional pawns, White minus Black:
    P 1, N 3, B 3, R 5, Q 9.
  - Men bands from the training quartiles: B0 ≤ 9, B1 10–16, B2 17–22,
    B3 ≥ 23.
  - The contribution is `Δ · w_b` for the row's band, mg and eg. B2 is the
    reference and carries no weight; the four bands together would be
    almost collinear with `mg_val`/`eg_val`.
  - That is six weights (`material_men_b0/b1/b3`, mg and eg), linear in the
    trace, seeded 0 (behaviour-neutral, fingerprint held), fitted in C.13.5's
    refit, clamped to [−30, 30] cp per pawn of edge.
  - It is evaluated after the lazy gate, beside the imbalance, so the gate's
    decisions do not change.
  - A step function of men is not a polynomial in the counts, so H-imb
    cannot be the whole story for this form.
- **Invariants:**
  - Colour symmetry (the standing sweep).
  - Exact texel reconstruction and an activation test with one position per
    band.
  - The evaluation cache stays exact (a pure function of the position).
  - The term never runs on a lazy row.
  - Seeds 0 reproduce 10,521,141 / EBF 2.521.
- **Falsifier, before building (RAR-E66 below):** under the game-length base
  or on the search-settled rows, the pair falls under the line.
- **Falsifier, after the refit:** RAR-E63's pair, re-read on the refit
  head's held-out rows, must fall under the line, so the term absorbed it.
  If it does not, the form missed the mechanism, and the cluster's gate
  decides the rest.

## Card 2 — mobility × men

- **Mechanism:** mobility's outcome value varies with the men left (mostly
  pawns) beyond its tables' phase taper: worth more in crowded positions,
  less in sparse ones.
- **Evidence it is active:** RAR-E63 and RAR-E64. Its margin over the line
  is 0.002 points, the weaker of the two.
- **Form, if it survives:** a band-wise mobility slope.
  - `M` is the side's total mobility count (the same squares the tables
    count, over knights, bishops, rooks and queens), White minus Black.
  - The contribution is `M · v_b` for bands B0, B1 and B3 (B2 the
    reference), mg and eg: six weights (`mobility_men_b0/b1/b3`), seeded 0,
    clamped to [−10, 10] cp per square.
  - It is computed in the mobility loop, after the gate, and **not** added
    to `mobility_mg`, so king danger's input is unchanged.
  - A per-piece slope (24 weights) is the alternative. It is not proposed
    first: the evidence is one family-level pair, and 24 sparse weights
    would invite the fit to chase noise.
- **Invariants:**
  - Those of card 1.
  - `king_danger_inputs_reproduce_the_donor_fixture` passes unchanged.
- **Falsifiers:** as card 1. Its thin margin makes RAR-E66 the likelier
  place for it to fail.

## Cheapest discriminating test (RAR-E66, to be registered before any number)

This is RAR-E63's instrument unchanged, on the two pairs only, on the
non-lazy rows, at RAR-E64's deciding base (spline plus the phase, men and
pawns slopes), with two additions read separately:

1. **H-len:** add `r · z`, with `z` the cohort-standardised
   `log1p(plies to the game's end)`, to the base. Each row's ply and game
   length come from `c132/provenance.csv`'s columns, which come from the
   source PGN.
2. **H-trans:** restrict to the **search-settled rows**, those whose
   RAR-E65 calibrated residual satisfies |S − g(E)| ≤ 100 cp (the search
   agrees with the static score within a pawn), with RAR-E64's deciding
   base.

- **A pair survives** when it passes 0.10%, 4 game-clustered SE and
  RAR-E63's null threshold in both readings.
- **Rows:** about 1 minute of compute on RAR-E63's and RAR-E65's
  artifacts. On the subset the power is lower, and the registration states
  the subset's size before the read.
- **Stop rule:**
  - A pair that falls under the line in either reading closes `NO_CHANGE`:
    game length or transient material, owned by C.13.6's target or not
    fixable by a term.
  - Neither surviving closes C.13.5 `NO_CHANGE` (C.13.2 and C.13.4 admitted
    nothing).
  - H-imb is not tested statically: a refit with the new term absent is
    what the bilinear imbalance already has. The post-refit falsifier above
    covers it.

## Decision (2026-10-15)

Neither card is `READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION` yet. Each has a measured effect
in this engine, its competing explanations, its interactions, its
invariants and a falsifier, but the two cheapest explanations that would
make a term useless (game length, transient material) are untested. RAR-E66
tests them, and a survivor becomes ready with the handoff below. I recommend
against building either before RAR-E66: an unexcluded calibration has
already cost this phase reads (C.7). If RAR-E66 clears both, the expected
value is modest. The two pairs together are about 0.22% of the held-out
loss, the same order as C.6's passer residual (+0.204 in its band), which
gave +6.4 Elo with a refit. One to four Elo is a fair prior, accepted only
by the gate.

## Implementation handoff (C.13.5, conditional on RAR-E66; frozen here)

For each surviving card:
1. **Engine:** the term exactly as its card's *Form*, seeded 0.
   - New fields: `material_men_b0/b1/b3_mg/eg`, or
     `mobility_men_b0/b1/b3_mg/eg`.
   - The band edges are constants in the source with a one-sentence comment
     naming the training quartiles.
   - Check: the fingerprint reproduces 10,521,141 / EBF 2.521 (seeds 0),
     plus fmt, clippy at zero warnings, debug, release and texel tests.
   - New tests: activation per band, reconstruction, the colour sweep and
     the king-danger fixture.
   - Engine commit apart.
2. **Tooling:** the tuner's group lists and clamps, and manifest v7 from
   `fit_manifest.py` differing from v6 by exactly the new rows (all free).
   Tooling commit apart.
3. **Cluster:** one joint refit of the whole surface on `hce-v6-tb` by
   `fit_complete.ps1` (the maintainer's command), the static screens, the
   post-refit falsifier (RAR-E63's pairs re-read on the candidate), the tree
   read, gate 1 `[0,3]` at `3+0.03` with RAR-M10's cap at the expected
   value, the margin block if gate 1 fails or a screen flags, and the
   `10+0.1` read. All of it is registered before any game, per PROCESS's
   evaluation-change procedure.
