# D.7.8.5 — the interrupted iteration on the kernel: the screen (2026-11-19)

PLAN D.7.8.5's frozen screen, at zero games: B.5's instrument
(`analysis/b5_research_2026-09-30.md`, *An interrupted iteration discards
almost nothing*) on the compact kernel. **Result: `NO_CHANGE`.** At
`3000+30`, 0 of 300 searches discard a move when a stop falls inside an
iteration, under the frozen 2% rule.

## Instrument

- **Counters** (diag build only; the default build has no code for them):
  at a stop inside a single-PV iteration, `root_stop_mid_iteration`;
  `root_stop_partial_new_best` when the interrupted window's line names a
  move other than the fallback (the root resets its line at each window
  and sets it only when a fully searched move raises alpha);
  `root_stop_after_fail_high_new` when an earlier window of that iteration
  failed high on a move other than the fallback; `root_stop_discards` when
  either holds. These are B.5's definitions (`429e25f`), carried onto the
  kernel's root driver.
- **Driver:** B.5's `b5_interrupt.py` with the counter list updated, in
  `analysis/artifacts/d78-night/` (SHA-256 `3a1a8ca7…`): 300 positions from
  the UHO book at an even stride, `ucinewgame` before each, `go wtime W
  btime W winc 30 binc 30`, Hash 64, one thread, for W = 3000, 1000 and
  300 ms.
- **Binary:** the diag build of `927063c` (D.7.8.4's engine and the
  counters), `rarog-d785-diag.exe` (SHA-256 `4770d1b7…`); `bench
  13` 3,320,355 / EBF 2.317 on it and on the default build, the
  fingerprint.
- **Live wire:** under node budgets of 3,000, 20,000 and 77,777 on 30
  positions (`live_nodes.py`), all 90 searches stopped inside an iteration
  and 2 discarded a move, so the counters report a stop inside an
  iteration when one happens.
- **Host:** idle (3% CPU, no engine or harness process) before the run.

The classic kernel read 1, 14 and 22 stops inside an iteration at the same
clocks (B.5). The kernel's iterations are several narrow windows each, but
its soft stop still ends almost every search between iterations. A
fail-high past the soft target, which already plays the move that failed
high, ended 11 to 12 searches at each clock.

## Reading

The frozen rule: at 2% or more of the searches at `3+0.03` discarding a
new best move or a failed-high move, the donor's rule is built; under it,
`NO_CHANGE` with the counts. 0% is under it: **`NO_CHANGE`**. The donor's
use of the interrupted iteration, with its forgotten-mate and aborted-loss
guards, would act on no search at `3+0.03` and on 1.3% at a tenth of that
clock. RAR-S129's (P) match is not re-run: the rule it would have
unclouded is not built.

**Retry trigger:** a time manager that ends searches inside iterations more
often (D.7.9's clock work), or a longer control in a rated read, raising
the share at its clock to 2%; the counters stay for that read.
