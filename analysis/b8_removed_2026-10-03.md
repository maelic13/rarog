## 1. The B.1 search (the legacy path)

- **What it did.** The search Rarog shipped as 2.4.0, split into modules by
  B.1. It had its own node kernel (`negamax`, `quiescence`), its own staged
  move picker, history tables and correction history, and its own LMR,
  futility, razoring and SEE-pruning rules. It had no donor form: it was Rarog's own design, tuned by SPSA
  through Phases 4–10. `--no-default-features` compiled it, and it reported
  `2.5.0-dev+legacy`.
- **Where it lived.**
  - `src/search/node.rs`, `movepick.rs`, `correction.rs` and `history.rs`
    (3,323 lines), chosen by `#[cfg_attr(feature = "b2core", path = ...)]`
    in `src/search/mod.rs`;
  - every `cfg(not(feature = "b2core"))` block in `eval.rs`, `lib.rs`,
    `stack.rs`, `threads.rs`, `thread.rs`, `tt.rs`, `board.rs` and
    `search_options.rs`;
  - `tests/evaluator.rs`'s legacy arm;
  - CI's `--no-default-features` clippy, test and bench legs;
  - the legacy columns of the cross-platform node check.
- **How decided.** B.2.4a accepted the unfitted selectivity core against it
  at +65.09 ± 23.26 Elo (432 games), and B.2.4b accepted the fitted core
  against the unfitted one at +138.60 ± 30.66 (248 games). The flip made the
  core the default on 2026-09-20 (`a47e85b`). Its last fingerprint is
  **7,590,542 / EBF 2.473** (7,601,220 / 2.474 at 2.4.0; the three shared
  quiescence thresholds took their fitted values at `5a5c150`).
- **Removed with it,** because only the B.1 search read them:
  - the TT's four-bit age (16 generations, divisor 4) and its
    same-position non-exact keep guard; the core's five-bit age and depth
    keep rule are now the only ones;
  - the evaluator's own rule-50 damping (`eval * min(clock, 100) / 199`),
    which the core applies in the search;
  - the stack's `cont_row_base` and the B.1 `push_move`;
  - the per-thread LMR jitter (`next_jitter`, a xorshift64 state seeded per
    thread, adding ±64/1024 ply to the reduction on every thread when more
    than one searches; Reckless's diversification shape).
- **Retry in another form.** Nothing to retry: the core replaced it as a
  unit. Its numbers live in RAR-S73–S77 and HISTORY. A comparison against
  the old search needs the 2.4.0 release binary, not a rebuild. The LMR
  jitter is the one helper-thread diversity device the core lacks; D.2
  decides the helper policy fresh.
- **Status:** removed in `84de712`. The core's modules moved from
  `src/search/core/` to `src/search/`, keeping their module names.
  Fingerprint after the commit: 11,171,726 / EBF 2.512, exact.

## 2. The arm features `b2core`, `b3proof`, `b4quiet`

Each was a compile-time umbrella for one candidate cluster. Each became a
default feature when its gate accepted it. The code each feature gated
becomes the only search; the off branch of each is deleted.

- **`b2core`.** Its off branch is the B.1 search (entry 1). The feature and
  its `path` attributes go with it.
- **`b3proof`.** Its off branch is the selectivity core's proof searches as
  B.2 shipped them:
  - a single null move with no verification region;
  - ProbCut without the margin-scaled verification;
  - the binary singular extension (+1, +2 off a PV line, −1 or multi-cut),
    with the shared `SingularTtDepthMargin` 3;
  - no LDSE and no LMR singular term;
  - the B.2 IIR policy.

  RAR-S84 accepted the cluster at +50.5 ± 10.9 Elo, H1 at 804 pairs, and it
  became the default in `f53ca7d`. The cluster's design and donor forms are
  in `analysis/b3_research_2026-09-23.md`.
- **`b4quiet`.** Its off branch is the quiescence before cluster 3:
  - no stored PV bit on depth-0 stores;
  - no fail-high interpolation toward beta;
  - the SEE threshold clamped below by `QsSeeClampLo` as well as above;
  - no donor count rule and no evasion pruning.

  RAR-S88 accepted the cluster at +4.4 ± 2.9 Elo, H1 at 10,705 pairs, and
  it became the default in `5a5c150`. Design:
  `analysis/b4_research_2026-09-28.md`.
- **Where they lived.**
  - `Cargo.toml` `[features]`;
  - 71, 103 and 34 `cfg` sites in `src` and `tests`;
  - the version suffixes `+b3proof` and `+b4quiet`. The default build now
    reports plain `2.5.0-dev`;
  - CI's `--features b3proof` and `b4quiet` clippy and test legs, and the
    b3proof bench leg;
  - the 32-subset feature matrix;
  - `build_test.ps1` and `nps_build_pool.ps1`'s `-Features` notes;
  - the `Core*`-needs-`b2core` guard in `harness_common.ps1`;
  - `tools/spsa_configs/README.md`'s per-arm build lines.
- **How decided.** By the three gates above.
- **Retry in another form.** The off branches are earlier searches, not
  ideas. Restore a mechanism from `10d0e83`, and only behind a new
  registered gate.
- **Status:** `b2core` removed in `84de712` with entry 1; `b3proof` and
  `b4quiet` folded in `07f22ed` (ten `not(...)` items deleted, among them
  the B.2 null-move and ProbCut block of 253 lines, the binary singular
  extension, and the B.4 quiescence's fixed delta and futility margins,
  count rule and clamped SEE threshold). The version string is now the
  package version alone. CI's per-arm clippy, test and bench legs and the
  `b3proof,tune,ablate` build went with them; the feature matrix covers the
  four features left (`tune`, `diag`, `ablate`, `texel`), 16 subsets.
  Fingerprint after the commit: 11,171,726 / EBF 2.512, exact.

## 3. Dead parameters

A coordinate is dead when no live code reads it. The proof is a reader
search outside `params.rs` with the deleted code excluded
(`tools/results/b8-cleanup-20261003/param_readers.py`), re-run after classes
1 and 2. Removing a coordinate removes its `tune` UCI option, its
`Default`, its clamp and its checks.

- **The B.1 search's coordinates** (`SearchParams`, read only by the legacy
  search; 33 found before class 1):
  - **pruning:** `FutilityBase`, `FutilityNotImproving`, `RazoringCoeff`,
    `LmpNotImproving`, `LmpCountBase`, `QuietHistPruneCoeff`,
    `SeePruningCoeff`, `SeePruningMax`, `FpCoeff`;
  - **move ordering:** `CheckBonusSafe`;
  - **LMR:** `LmrTtPvAdj`, `LmrExactBound`, `LmrShallowTt`, `LmrHistDiv`,
    `LmrRootRelief`;
  - **history shape:** `HistBonusMul`, `HistBonusSub`, `HistBonusMax`,
    `HistMalusMul`, `HistMalusSub`, `HistMalusMax`, `ExactBonusPct`,
    `CaptureMalusPct`, `SurpriseBonusPct`;
  - **correction:** `CorrCaptureWeightPct`, `CorrRfpScale`, `CorrFutScale`,
    `CorrLmrScale`, `CorrWeightPawn`, `CorrWeightMinor`, `CorrWeightOwnNp`,
    `CorrWeightTheirNp`, `CorrWeightCont`.

  What they did: the B.1 search's pruning margins, LMR adjusters, history
  bonus and malus shape, and its correction blend. Their values and bake
  histories are in the `params.rs` comments at `10d0e83`. How decided:
  dead, no live reader since the core replaced the B.1 search. Retry: none
  as such; the core's own `Core*` coordinates cover each family.
- **`QsSeeClampLo`.** The lower clamp of the quiescence SEE threshold. Under
  `b4quiet` the threshold is `alpha - stand_pat - QsSeeMargin` with only the
  upper clamp. Since `alpha >= stand_pat` there, the lower clamp can never
  act (`analysis/b4_research_2026-09-28.md`, *Carried forward*). Read only
  by the B.1 search and by the core's `not(b4quiet)` branch. Retry: none; a
  lower clamp is meaningless while the threshold cannot fall below
  `-QsSeeMargin`.
- **Read only by the B.2 branches class 2 deleted** (found by the re-run
  after `07f22ed`), eight more:
  - **null move:** `NullMoveDepthCoeff`, `NullMoveImprovingBonus` (the B.2
    null-move gate: `eval >= beta - coeff * depth - bonus * improving`);
  - **ProbCut:** `ProbCutMargin`, `ProbCutMoveCapBase`,
    `ProbCutMoveCapCutBonus` (the B.2 ProbCut margin and move cap; the
    proof cluster's `CoreProbcut*` replace them);
  - **singular extension:** `SingularBetaMult`, `SingularDoubleMargin` (the
    binary extension's singular beta and double-extension margin; the
    proof cluster's `CoreSing*` replace them);
  - `QsSeeClampLo`, listed above.

  41 `SearchParams` coordinates in all; the other 25 have live readers.
- **Not dead, kept:** `AspMaxFails` and the aspiration growth coordinates
  (B.5.3 was rejected, so they stay live), and every coordinate in a live
  SPSA surface.
- **Status:** planned (class 3).

## 6. The dead PVS guard

- **What it did.** At a PV node's re-search, `if mv == tt_move && ev.depth >
  1 { new_depth = new_depth.max(1) }`. It is the donor's guard for a TT move
  whose negative singular extension (−3 there) drove `new_depth` below one.
- **Where it lived.** `src/search/core/node.rs`, the PV re-search branch.
- **How decided.** B.2.1's review (`analysis/b21_review_2026-09-14.md`)
  found it cannot fire:
  - the TT move is always the first move, which takes the full-window
    branch, not the re-search;
  - under `b3proof` the negative extension is clamped to keep at least one
    ply.

  Proved again here by a counter build over bench before deletion (class
  6's record).
- **Retry.** Needed only if a later picker can put the TT move after another
  move, or a negative extension can drive `new_depth` below one.
- **Status:** planned (class 6).
