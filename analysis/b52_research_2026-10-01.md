**Three mechanisms, not two.** H1 holds with an amendment. (1) The root is
cut to the single DTZ-preferred move. (2) The single-legal-move shortcut
(`search/mod.rs`, `legal_moves.len() == 1`) reads that cut set as a forced
move and ends every `go depth`/clock search after depth 2. That is why KQvK
and KRvK report an evaluation (`cp 635`) instead of a tablebase win. (3) The
in-search WDL probe returns at once at a child reached by a zeroing move. All
three record positions' preferred moves are zeroing (`e5e6`, `a7a8q`,
`f3f4`), so their PV ends at the root move. Removing (1) and (3) gives full
searched PVs and ponder moves with no extension, so the competing explanation
(mate-distance pruning in the tablebase band truncating the PV) is refuted on
these positions.

**The display, measured.** `cp 31743` is `TB_WIN_SCORE − 1`, printed raw. A
second raw value leaks too: KRvK in arm 1 printed `cp 31871`, which is the
TT's rule-50 downgrade of a mate score (`MATE_SCORE − MAX_PLY − 1`).

### A contract defect the reads exposed: the tablebase band is empty

- `is_win(s)` is `s >= TB_WIN_SCORE` (31,744), but a probed tablebase win is
  `TB_WIN_SCORE − ply`, which is below it for every ply ≥ 1. So no
  tablebase value the search ever produces counts as decisive. The pruning
  guards, the correction-training gate (`|score| < TB_WIN_SCORE`) and the
  thread vote's decisive rank (`threads.rs`) all treat it as an ordinary
  large score.
- `score_to_tt`/`score_from_tt` adjust only the mate band (≥ 31,872).
  Tablebase values are stored without ply adjustment, so their distance is
  path-relative, and no rule-50 downgrade applies to them.
- The rule-50 downgrade of a mate returns 31,871, which `is_win` counts as
  decisive. Stockfish downgrades to the highest *non*-decisive value.

Stockfish's layout, in Rarog's numbers: tablebase base
`TB_VALUE = MATE_SCORE − MAX_PLY − 1` (31,871), probe value
`TB_VALUE − ply` (31,744–31,871 for plies 0–127, so exactly the band
`[TB_WIN_SCORE, MATE_SCORE − MAX_PLY)` the helpers already define), TT
adjustment over the whole decisive band, and downgrade to
`±(TB_WIN_SCORE − 1)`. The two TT changes cannot be separated: once the
whole band is ply-adjusted, a stored 31,871 plus a ply would read back as a
mate. Measured: the downgrade target alone reproduces the bench exactly
(11,171,726 / EBF 2.512), so the bench never reaches it.

### A clock interaction the reads exposed

Today a tablebase root costs 0–4 ms (cut plus shortcut). Without the cut it
searches like any position, and here Rarog has a won-endgame time sink that
exists without tablebases.

- **Without tablebases**, on the head under `60000+600`: rec1 32,305 ms and
  KRvK 11,251 ms. Both are exactly the computed hard maximum (32,304 and
  11,250 ms, `scripts/tm_limits.py`, `time.rs`'s own formula). In rec1 the
  last `info` came at 3,744 ms, after a depth-32 aspiration cascade of eight
  fail-highs (555 → 1,043), and the next re-search ran until the hard limit.
  KRvK stalls at `cp 31871 lowerbound` from 682 ms.
- **With the root cut removed and probing off (arm 3)**, the same sink
  reaches tablebase roots: rec3 30,330 ms (maximum 30,329) and rec1
  8,358 ms. With the cut removed and probing on (arm 1), rec1–3 took
  1.6–3.5 s and KRvK hit its 11,250 ms maximum.
- **Stockfish 19** at the same clock, same tables, one thread: rec1
  6,409 ms (PV 30), rec3 5,553 ms (PV 31), KQvK `mate 8` in 676 ms, KRvK
  `mate 16` in 991 ms, KQvK at rule-50 92 `cp 28`.
- **Capping the hard limit at the optimum at a DTZ root** (arm 7):
  4,701/4,601/4,413 ms for rec1–3 and 1,637 ms for KRvK, each exactly the
  computed optimum and within Stockfish's range. The PV stays full at fixed
  depth. Under the clock the final line can be one move long (an
  interrupted fail-high line); the ponder move still came from the TT, and
  the extension fills the line.
- **The downgrade-target change does not cure the stall**: KRvK then shows
  `cp 2987` instead of `cp 31871`, still at 11,250 ms.

The general sink is outside B.5.2. It is recorded as a research input under
D.1, its owner: an iteration that outlives the optimum is cut only at the
hard limit. A fixed-depth `go depth 22` on these positions ran past ten
minutes before it was stopped (which position is not recorded). B.5.2
contains the exposure at tablebase roots with the cap above.

## Stockfish's parts against Rarog's contracts

### P1. Root ranking and the root move set — `READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION`

- **Rank in Rust, not with Fathom's root ranking.** Fathom's
  `root_probe_dtz` ranks every clean win 1,000, so KQvK kept 19 of 21 moves
  where Stockfish, which forces DTZ ranking when DTZ equals distance to mate,
  keeps the fastest. `tb_probe_root_impl`'s per-move results carry each
  move's WDL and DTZ from the root's view (signed DTZ = +DTZ for a win or
  cursed win, −DTZ for a loss or blessed loss, 0 for a draw; a mating move
  is 1; a zeroing move carries ±1/±101). The prototype rebuilt Stockfish's
  ranks from them and walked to mate in KQvK in 15 plies, Stockfish's
  `mate 8`.
- **Rank formula** (Stockfish's): win `v > 0`:
  `MAX − (rankDTZ ? v : 0)` while `v + cnt50 ≤ 99` and the position has not
  repeated, else `MAX/2 − (v + cnt50)`; loss mirrored with `−v·2 + cnt50 < 100`;
  draw 0. `rankDTZ` is set for the PV extension and forced when DTZ is DTM
  (pawnless, three men, or four with minors only).
- **Rarog's board supplies what Fathom cannot:** a root move into a true
  threefold or a rule-50 draw ranks as DTZ 0. The arbiter's rule applies
  here, not the search's aggressive twofold. The "has repeated" test is
  Stockfish's (any repetition since the last zeroing move); Rarog's
  `has_repeated_position()` tests only the current position, so the
  implementation adds the rule-50-window form.
- **The root set is the whole best-ranked group,** never one move. B.2.0.2's
  contract stands as written: MultiPV is clamped to that root set.
- **The single-move shortcut keys on the legal-move count,** not on the
  filtered root set.
- **Under time management at a DTZ-ranked root, the hard limit is the
  optimum.** This is a Rarog deviation, justified by the measured sink.
- The existing skip under `go nodes` stays.

### P2. In-search WDL probing — `READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION`

- **Off for the whole search** when the root was DTZ-ranked, or when it was
  WDL-ranked and not winning (Stockfish's rule). The flag is set before
  helpers start and carried to them: helpers run their own
  `reset_search_state`, which would otherwise re-enable probing.
- **Bound-correct elsewhere:**
    - a win is a lower bound, a loss an upper bound, and a draw (cursed and
      blessed results included under the rule-50 option) exact;
    - the node returns only on an exact result or a cutoff;
    - the TT store uses that bound at `min(MAX_PLY − 1, depth + 6)` with no
      move, replacing today's always-exact store;
    - at a PV node a lower bound raises alpha and the best score, and an
      upper bound caps the node's result.
- **Unchanged:** the zeroing-move condition under `Syzygy50MoveRule` and
  Rarog's probe-depth and probe-limit handling. They are equivalent at the
  defaults (`SyzygyProbeLimit 7` above the 6-man set, probe depth 1, so
  quiescence is never probed).

### P3. The tablebase band and the TT — `READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION`

- Probe values `TB_VALUE − ply`.
- `score_to_tt`/`score_from_tt` adjust every `|v| ≥ TB_WIN_SCORE`.
- A mate whose distance exceeds `100 − r50`, or a tablebase value with
  `TB_VALUE − |v| > 100 − r50`, reads back as `±(TB_WIN_SCORE − 1)`.
- `is_win`, `is_loss`, `is_decisive`, the correction gate and the thread vote
  then see tablebase values as decisive without any edit of their own.

Without tablebases the only behaviour that changes is the rule-50 downgrade
of mate scores, which the bench never reaches (fingerprint exact). This part
is a correctness repair that P2's bounds and P4's display both need.

### P4. The displayed score — `READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION`

UCI output only; internal values never change.

- **Mate band:** `mate N` as now.
- **Tablebase band:** `cp ±(20000 − (TB_VALUE − |v|))`.
- **Ordinary score:** `cp v` as now.
- **At a tablebase root, unless the search found a mate,** each line shows
  its move's display score:
    - a clean win `cp 20000`;
    - a cursed win `max(3, r − (MAX/2 − 200)) · 100 / 200` (1–49 cp; Stockfish
      prints `cp 28` for KQvK at 92);
    - a draw `cp 0`;
    - losses mirrored.

The `cp` scale of ordinary scores stays D.3's card; this part owns only the
tablebase band.

### P5. The PV extension — `READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION`, with a Rarog cost rule

**Semantics are Stockfish's.**
- It runs for a decisive score that is not a mate, at a tablebase root, or
  when an in-search probe produced the score.
- **Step 1** keeps the searched PV while each move keeps the best rank
  (`rankDTZ` off) and no threefold or rule-50 draw appears, and cuts the PV
  at the first failure.
- **Step 2** extends with `rankDTZ` on. Ties go to the move that leaves the
  opponent fewer replies (−1 per reply, −100 per capture). It stops at mate,
  at a draw, or when DTZ is missing.
- A draw found on the way displays `cp 0`.

**Measured cost** (prototype, `runs/ext_cost_run1.log`; full walk to mate):

| Position | Plies to mate | Cold | Warm |
|---|---|---|---|
| KQvK | 15 | 1.3 ms | 1.3 ms |
| KRvK | 27 | 1.6 ms | 1.6 ms |
| rec1 | 19 | 19.8 ms | 5.6 ms |
| rec2 | 71 | 9.7 ms | 6.8 ms |
| rec3 | 47 | 27.4 ms | 7.5 ms |

The worst single ply took 9.4 ms cold (table loading) and 1.2 ms warm.

**The box.** Stockfish's box is `2 · MultiPV · elapsed ≥ Move Overhead`:
5 ms at the default 10 ms and MultiPV 1. Three-man lines finish inside it;
six-man lines are cut, and Stockfish then prints its "requires more time"
string. One Fathom call probes a whole ply, so the box can overrun by one
ply: up to about 9 ms cold on this host.

**Rarog's cost rule.** Stockfish runs the extension at every completed
iteration, up to 5 ms each on the clock. Under time management Rarog runs it
once, on the line reported with `bestmove`. By then the helpers are joined,
so the probe runs alone. The box is Stockfish's: `Move Overhead / (2 ·
MultiPV)` per line.

**Timing cases:**
- In a time scramble, where `time.rs`'s `2 · overhead` reserve binds (below
  about 52 overheads of clock), the extension is skipped and the ponder move
  falls back to the TT.
- Without time management (`go infinite`, `depth`, `nodes`, ponder) it runs
  at every iteration's output, unbounded, as Stockfish's does.

### P6. The ponder move — `READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION`

Take `pv[1]` of the reported, possibly extended, line. Fall back to the TT
when the line has one move (today's `ponder_from_tt`). The extension runs
before `bestmove` is printed, so B.3.5's contract (exactly one `bestmove` per
`go`, whatever arrives) is untouched. A `stop` during the box changes
nothing, because the box is already bounded.

### P7. Thread safety of a root DTZ probe beside helper WDL probes — established; `NO_CHANGE` (no lock)

**From the source.** Fathom's probe entry points keep their state on the
stack or in caller buffers. Table loading is double-checked
(`ready[type]` atomics, `tbMutex`). MSVC compiles the file as C++17
(`std::mutex`, `std::atomic`) and other targets as C11 (`stdatomic.h`,
pthread). Global tables are written only by `tb_init`, which runs from
`setoption` between searches. The header's "NOT thread safe" on
`tb_probe_root` describes an older Fathom.

**Measured** (prototype, Windows/MSVC only): four threads probing WDL over
747 positions in six tables, while the main thread repeated DTZ walks to
mate. Five runs started with cold tables, so loading was concurrent, and one
started warm.
- About 53 million probes per run, every one checked against that thread's
  first answer for the position.
- All 2,988 first answers checked against a serial reference.
- 680–690 walks per run, all identical.
- Zero mismatches in every run.
- **Live wire:** a planted wrong reference answer was caught (4
  mismatches, exit 101).

Linux and macOS (the C11 branch) are covered by the source reading, not by
this run.

### Kept as they are — `NO_CHANGE`

- **Cursed-win and blessed-loss scores in search:** Stockfish's ±2; Rarog
  keeps 0. No measured reason to change, and B.5.1 found no gain in
  perturbing draw scores.
- **Stockfish's probe-depth refinement** (tables below the limit probed at
  any depth): equivalent at the defaults, see P2.

## Interaction map

- **Root ranking → root set → MultiPV:** clamp as B.2.0.2. Line 1 chooses
  within the best group by search.
- **Root set → single-move shortcut:** keyed on legal moves.
- **Root set → time:** cap at the optimum at a DTZ root.
- **In-search probe → TT:** bound, depth + 6, no move. Feeds TT cutoffs, the
  ponder fallback and the PV.
- **Tablebase band → `is_win` consumers:**
    - pruning guards;
    - ProbCut's clamp;
    - the correction-training gate;
    - the thread vote;
    - the aspiration window (decisive scores);
    - the UCI display.
- **TT downgrade → rule-50 horizon:** mate and tablebase alike.
- **Extension → PV → ponder move → `bestmove`:** B.3.5. It runs after the
  helpers are joined under a clock.
- **Probe options:**
    - `SyzygyProbeLimit` caps the in-search cardinality only; root ranking
      needs the root to fit the tables.
    - `SyzygyProbeDepth` is unchanged.
    - `Syzygy50MoveRule` selects the cursed-win rank band and the display
      bound.

## Prospective prediction (frozen 2026-10-01, before any gate game)

- **Without tablebases:** identical bench; strength change 0. The rule-50
  mate downgrade is the only non-tablebase path.
- **With tablebases on the standard book:** 0 ± 3 Elo. Roots in the tables

## Falsifiers and stop conditions

- **A won tablebase root not converted, or a time loss with tables
  configured:** an implementation defect. Stop, fix, re-run the checks.
- **The extension's "requires more time" notice at most tablebase roots of
  3-man fixtures:** the box is mis-sized.
- **A non-tablebase bench change:** P3 reached the bench, and the change is
  no longer behaviour-neutral without tables. Stop and re-derive.

### A premise the stop rule refuted: the band is reachable without tables

The handoff moved every value in the tablebase band by ply in the table. With
that, bench 13 read **11,189,746 / EBF 2.513**.

**The cause, traced by a debug-build backtrace**
(`impl/runs/band_leak_backtrace_debug_bench.log`): ProbCut's entry guard is
`!is_win(beta)`, one-sided, so ProbCut runs at a losing beta near a mate
score. Its `probcut_beta` (for example −31,853) lies inside the band. The
fail-hard quiescence then stores its window bound, alpha = 31,853 at ply 12.

Ordinary window arithmetic therefore fills the band without any table, and
the record's claim that only the rule-50 downgrade changes behaviour without
tables was false: it had been measured for the downgrade alone. By the stop
rule the change was not adopted.

**What landed (`84b1f74`):**
- probes return `TB_VALUE − ply`, so every tablebase result is decisive;
- a mate beyond the rule-50 horizon reads back as `TB_WIN_SCORE − 1`, not
  31,871;
- the table stores band values as it always did.

**What stays open:**
- a tablebase value's distance in the table is path-relative;
- a tablebase value gets no rule-50 downgrade, exactly as before.

Separating tablebase values from window bounds needs a behaviour change
without tables (a two-sided ProbCut guard, or a fail-soft quiescence store),
so it is a card for B.6 or B.8's review with its own measurement, not part
of this leaf.
