# D.7.9 — clock use on the new kernel: the screen, the horizon, the fit's surface (2026-11-20)

PLAN D.7.9's research, at zero games. **Result:** the screen is done; the
low-clock horizon's field read is **not registered** (`NO_CHANGE`, low
expected value); the clock's constants go to the finishing fit as thirteen
coordinates, `TmHorizonPerSec` held at 50, with their own activation read.
Found on the way: the kernel spends about 11% less a move than the classic
while its clock is above a second, the same total, and its band under
500 ms is half as populated. Evidence layer: clock use from game records and
a formula. None of it is Elo.

## Use

- Item 1, the screen: whether the kernel changed where its clock goes, and
  the exposure of the horizon's domain, which a field read's card must
  state (AGENTS, *Gating*).
- Item 2: whether the horizon's field read is worth its hours.
- Item 3: which clock coordinates the fit's surface takes, and how their
  activation is read.

## Instrument

All in `analysis/artifacts/d79-clock-2026-11-20/` (ignored); file reads
only, no engine run and no timing on the host.

- `clock_screen.py` (SHA-256 `3cfdb5d0…`): a run directory's
  `games.jsonl` and `games.pgn`. Each game's PGN is cut by the journal's
  offset and length and must match its SHA-256; each side's move comments
  must number the journal's search count, and its longest and shortest
  `t=` must equal the journal's charged maximum and minimum. Every check
  passed on every run below (2,000 games each, 20,000 for RAR-M87's field
  leg; `t=` is the charged time floored to the millisecond on all
  4 × 2,000 checked extremes a run, so a rebuilt clock reads up to 1 ms a
  move high). Budget = 3,000 + 30 × own moves (RAR-O08's measure).
  "Undecided" is a non-mate `|s| < 150`, RAR-M87's band.
- **The instrument reproduces the recorded figures:** on RAR-M87's field
  leg it gives the head's and 2.6.0's end clocks as 616 and 379 ms (means)
  and 567 and 263 (medians), RAR-M87's own numbers.
- `h_by_clock.py` (`05d01842…`): `h=` over 20 ms by the clock the engine
  was sent, with the same checks.
- `horizon_by_control.py` (`5f13ee89…`): `compute_runtime_limits`
  (`src/search/time.rs:112-252`) transcribed for sudden death, Move
  Overhead 10 ms; it reproduces RAR-M87's ratios (0.84, 0.65, 0.43 at
  750, 500 and 300 ms) and settling clocks (416–575 ms; 154 for fifty
  moves at every clock).
- Outputs: one `.txt` a run beside the scripts.

By game length (kernel, S132): sides of 40 moves or fewer spend 60% and
end near 1.6 s; from 61 moves on they spend 89–91% and end near 500–575 ms.
The unspent share falls with the move number to about 0.09 of the budget
by move 80 and stays there: the clock settles where the optimum meets the
increment.

**Finding 1: the total did not move.** The kernel spends what the classic
spent head to head (85–86%) and ends with the same clock (about 620 ms);
the end clock is the horizon's equilibrium, not the kernel's. RAR-O08's
figures, against a field: the classic 87.1–88.9%, Reckless's time manager
94.9–95.1%.

**Finding 2: the kernel spends less early.** In the same games (RAR-S126)
it spends 0.622 of the base in its first twenty moves against the
classic's 0.696 (−10.6%) and 83.8 ms a move against 96.0 while its clock
is above a second (−12.7%); the fitted kernel spends 0.59–0.61. The time
is not lost: it is still on the clock at move 40 (about 900–1,040 ms
against 600–660) and is spent in the middle and late game. The donor's
time manager spends 0.84–0.87 of the base in those moves (RAR-O08). The
cause is not separated. Candidates, each a reading of the soft target's
factors (`soft_stop`, `time.rs:329-357`):
- the stability factor counts completed iterations since the best move
  changed, and the kernel completes about five more a move (depth 18.2
  against 13.3 at the same clock, S132 and S125), so the factor reaches its
  floor sooner. It is Reckless's own factor at Reckless's constants
  (`pv_stability`, Reckless `search.rs:256`), on the donor's search shape;
- the overrun past the target shrinks with the branching factor (EBF 2.32
  against 2.48), the stop being checked between iterations (RAR-R15 read
  time moving as the formula's ratio to the power 0.66);
- the falling slope reads the kernel's score, which the fitted wrapper
  shrank to 0.50–0.82 of the evaluation's (the audit's A3), so a fall buys
  less time;
- the effort factor, if the best move's node share is more concentrated.

Whether the shift costs strength was not read and is not read here: see
item 3.

**Finding 3: the horizon's domain halved.** Undecided moves under 500 ms
are 4.5–6.8% of the kernel's undecided moves against 11.6–13.9% for the
classic head to head and 18.8% for the classic against the field; the
sides that play one, 14–18% against 24–36%.

**Finding 4: `h=` is not a hold.** The manifests show the kernel's `h=`
p99 at 37–45 ms against the classic's 12–13, about 2,400 moves a run over
the 20 ms margin against 860. `h=` is the charged time less the time in the
engine's last `info` line (CLI 0.5.0,
`crates/colosseum-engine/src/pgn.rs:44-47`; RAR-R30). On the
kernel it runs over 20 ms on 12.3–15.1% of the moves sent under 300 ms of
clock, 2.7% at 300–500 ms and under 1% above, against 1.1%, 0.8% and
under 0.7% for the classic (S126, S132). The hold proper, the charged time
less the process's CPU time (the journal's `held_ns`, a game's maximum),
is the same for both: median 0.7–0.8 ms, p99 6–9 ms. The excess is search
time after the last completed iteration at low clocks, the stop inside an
iteration D.7.8.5 counted (24 of 300 searches at `300+30`), which prints
no line. Consequences: a hold or forfeit-margin read on the kernel uses
the journal's `held_ns`, not `h=`; D.7.8.5's trigger is not fired (its
measure, a discarded new best, was 1.33% of searches at `300+30`, and 7–8%
of the kernel's moves are sent under 300 ms). PROCESS's description of
`h=` is corrected in the same change.

## Item 2: the low-clock horizon — not registered

**I recommend against the horizon's field read now.** This is low expected
value, not a refutation.

- **The direct read exists and is null.** RAR-R17 played the horizon at 100
  against 53 a second, the rest equal, 2,000 games at `3+0.03`: −0.9 ± 8.0
  Elo for 100, with the late time a move equal in both arms. The field gap
  is RAR-M87's post-hoc split by a variable the arms themselves move
  (which games run low), which chooses and does not accept.
- **It acts only at `3+0.03`.** At `10+0.1`, `60+0.6` and CCRL's Blitz
  `2'+1"`, Stockfish's cap on the optimum (`0.19404 × clock / time left`)
  binds before the horizon does: the optimum at 999 ms is unchanged (ratio
  1.000) and the clock settles at the same point with or without it (at
  `10+0.1`, 515 / 858 / 1,765 ms at multipliers 1.0 / 0.6 / 0.4). CCRL's
  40/15 sends `movestogo`, whose branch never reads the horizon
  (`time.rs:155-160`). A gain it bought would be a gain at the test control
  only.
- **It is the donor's current rule.** Stockfish sets the horizon to
  `clock_ms × 0.05` below one second in sudden death (local clone
  of 2026-11-14, `timeman.cpp:107-110`), which is 50 a second.
- **On the kernel its domain halved** (finding 3).
- **Cost.** RAR-M87's field leg played 20,000 games in 3.6 hours for a
  difference between two heads with an SE of 3.5 Elo. A ±5 Elo interval on
  the horizon's difference needs about 38,000 games, about 7 hours, and the
  `10+0.1` harm read of 2,000 games about another hour.

*Retry trigger:* a direct read of the horizon (not a split) wholly below
zero, or a rated control with an increment under about 50 ms entering
acceptance. Until then `TmHorizonPerSec` stays 50.

## Item 3: the fit's surface

- **Thirteen coordinates join D.7.11.1's surface**, at their present seeds:
  `TmOptScale`,
  `TmFallBase`, `TmFallSlope`, `TmFallPrevSlope`, `TmInstabBase`,
  `TmInstabSlope`, `TmEffortHigh`, `TmEffortLow`, `TmEffortFloor`,
  `TmStabBase`, `TmStabSlope`, `TmStabFloor`, `TmFailHighTarget`.
  `TmFailHighTarget`'s range stays below its switch-off (10,000,000).
- **`TmHorizonPerSec` is held at 50, out of the surface.** It acts only at
  `3+0.03` (item 2), so a fit at that control would fit it to a regime no
  rated control plays. It is the forfeit guard: RAR-R17's arm at 100 a
  second sent 22% of its games under 100 ms of clock, none at 53. The fit
  stops on a forfeit, which on Colosseum is `--max-time-losses 0` (a tune
  otherwise allows 0.5% of its games, at least three; CLI 0.5.0,
  `crates/colosseum-cli/src/composition/spsa.rs:213-220`),
  so an arm that spends the reserve would put the run at risk for a
  coordinate with no measured strength.
- **Their activation read is the clock's, not the bench's.** RAR-S128's
  rule drops what changes no bench decision. `bench 13` is depth-limited
  and reads no clock (`params.rs`, *Time-management dynamic multipliers*),
  so that rule would drop all thirteen. Their read is
  `tools/diag/tm_activation.py` on a `diag` build: 300 book positions at
  `3000+30`, a fresh game each, minutes on an idle host. A factor at one end
  of its range on more than 90% of iterations is inert, and its
  coordinates leave the surface (RAR-R15 (b), RAR-S47's lesson). The same
  read says which factor carries finding 2.
- **Correction.** D.7.9's text said these constants were fitted to the
  classic kernel. They were never fitted on Rarog. They are the donors'
  seeds: the base allocation and most factors from Stockfish, the stability
  factor Reckless's. D.1.5 kept the clock pieces at their seeds
  (RAR-R28), and RAR-R15's block 1 (`TmOptScale` 11,687 among its centres)
  was never baked. D.7.11 is their first fit.

**Finding 2 needs no read of its own.** The fit carries `TmOptScale`
(its present range 0.2–2.0) and the factors, so it can restore the early spend if its
gradient says so. A 2,000-game read could not resolve the few Elo a shift
of this size might be worth. The prior is weakly for spending more early:
RAR-R15's block 1, on the classic, raised the time a move ×1.21 in plies
0–19 (RAR-R16's wire check), and its gate ended unresolved (RAR-R16,
+0.6 ± 5.8 Elo at 2,345 pairs); both donors front-load. The form behind it, the donor's time manager
(Reckless's soft bound with its node-share extender and five factors), is
not a candidate now. Its clock share of the donor's lead was predicted at
+9 [−9, +27] and never measured (RAR-O09's matches (2) and (3), prepared).
*Trigger:* the finishing fit raising `TmOptScale` by 15% or more registers
the donor's time manager as a form candidate in D.7's review, with those
two matches on the kernel as its measurement.

## Addendum, the same day: the donors compared, and the decision

Asked whether the clock should be re-implemented, the three time managers
were compared from source (Stockfish's local clone of 2026-11-14, `timeman.cpp`,
`search.cpp:581-635`; Reckless at `0756957`, `time.rs`, `search.rs:245-262`).
Rarog's is a hybrid: Stockfish's budget in an older form, without its
per-control scale (`originalTimeAdjust`, 0.76 at `3+0.03` to 1.30 at
`2'+1"`), its cut when behind on the clock or `increaseDepth`;
Stockfish-style falling, instability and effort terms; Reckless's
stability factor; Rarog's fail-high stop. D.1 deferred each missing
Stockfish piece with a trigger (`analysis/d12_tm_research_2026-10-22.md`).

D.1's `increaseDepth` trigger, 3% of moves at three times the optimum or
more, read on the kernel's games (`over_optimum.py`, the optimum from
`horizon_by_control.py`'s checked transcription, the same game checks):
RAR-S131 and S132 6.1–6.2% of moves (3.9–4.0% with the clock above a
second), 30% of all time past the optimum; the classic after D.1 (RAR-S125)
9.2–9.3% (8.2%), 39%. 2.6.0, which set the trigger, read 3.2–3.4% with 27%
of its time past the optimum (`tm_profile.txt`), so the trigger sits near
the level any extension produces; it reads fired, which is weak evidence by
itself.

Reckless's budget and all five factors were tuned together with its search
parameters in two SPSA sessions: 160,000 games at `40+0.4` (its pull
request 956, 2026-05-01; +3.3 Elo at `8+0.08`, +4.3 at `40+0.4`) and
200,000 at six threads (pull request 1072, 2026-06-06; +3.6), both moving
every time constant. **Maintainer decision 2026-11-20:** one donor's time
manager, not a hybrid, Reckless's, with Rarog's guards kept: PLAN D.7.9.1,
before D.7.10.
