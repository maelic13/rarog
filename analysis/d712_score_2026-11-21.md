# D.7.12: the reported score calibrated, and win-draw-loss output (2026-11-21)

PLAN D.7.12. Output only: no search or time decision reads the printed
score, and `bench 13` stays 3,390,842 / EBF 2.317.

## Why

`score cp` printed the kernel's internal score unchanged. Before the fits
the classic kernel won half its games at about +120 with most material on
the board and at +244 in endings, and the fitted wrapper then scaled the
kernel's scores to 0.50 to 0.82 of that, so the next release would print
lower numbers than 2.7.0 for the same position. The convention adopted is
Stockfish's: +100 is the score at which the side wins half its games.

## What the donors do (read 2026-11-21)

- **Reckless** (`8d7d1dd`, `src/types/score.rs`, `src/thread.rs`): an
  ordinary score prints as `100 · x / a(m)`, `a` a cubic in material / 58,
  material counted pawn 1, minor 3, rook 5, queen 9 and clamped to
  [16, 78]; tablebase and mate scores keep their own forms. It has no
  win-draw-loss output: none of its UCI options shows one and nothing
  prints `wdl`. How its constants were fitted is not visible in the local
  clone (shallow history).
- **Stockfish** (`1e7e6bde`, `src/uci.cpp`): the same `100 · x / a(m)`,
  material clamped to [17, 78], with a second cubic `b(m)`: win
  `1 / (1 + exp((a − x) / b))`, loss the same at `−x`, draw the rest, in
  permille. With `UCI_ShowWDL` (a check option, off by default) every
  `info` line carries `wdl W D L` after the score and its bound. Its
  constants come from its own games (`official-stockfish/WDL_model`).

Rarog takes Stockfish's form and option, with its own constants, fitted on
its own games; no donor value crosses.

## The fit

`python tools/wdl_fit.py tools/results/rar-s135-read2000-2000/games.pgn
--engine d711at2000` (the defaults `--max-abs 4000 --min-count 300`): the
2,000 games of RAR-S135's read at the stop, the side `d711at2000`, which
plays theta-2000, the seeds baked by D.7.11.8 (`16f93cb`). Of its 146,653
moves, 97,741 positions are fitted: 6,389 mate scores and 2,117 scores at
or beyond 4,000 (the clamped band a downgraded mate prints in) are left
out, and a few positions under 17 material. Every material count from 17
to 78 has at least 300 positions.

| | m = 20 | 40 | 58 | 78 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `a(m)`, the internal score of an even chance | 135.6 | 89.9 | 83.7 | 82.8 |
| `b(m)` | 37.5 | 38.1 | 44.6 | 68.6 |

Constants (cube first): `WDL_A = [-145.41690682, 467.7359744,
-495.45731177, 256.82923484]`, `WDL_B = [43.96743966, -59.72730625,
26.90952593, 33.4824991]`; mean log loss 0.5452. The cubic follows each
material count's own fit within its noise (about ±10 in `a` on counts of
about 1,000 positions; 148.1 against 151.8 at 17, 83.7 against 84.6 at 53).

predicted win rate):

The even chance falls at +100. The largest misfit is next to zero: the
band [0, +25) holds 13,603 positions, many scored exactly 0 by a draw, and
draws there run 0.908 against 0.863 predicted; the model has no term for a
proven draw.

**Limits:** one time control (`3+0.03`), UHO book openings, 97,741
positions against Stockfish's millions; enough for the even chance to sit
at +100 within a few percent, not a fine calibration of the tails. Refit
after any later fit of the search or the evaluation (Phase F, F.6), with
the same tool; `--check-rust src/uci/info.rs` fails until the source holds
the new constants.
