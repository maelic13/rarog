# C.0.4 — the first evaluation unit: which part of king safety carries the residual, why Rarog's inputs read zero, what scaling adds, and the magnitude contract

`dev` at `f072495` (RAR-E21's registration), 2026-10-06; engine source
unchanged since `ee02ed1` apart from the behaviour-neutral commits PLAN D.3
lists (fingerprint 11,171,726 / EBF 2.512). Research leaf, class `R3`. **No
engine source changed.** Raw results are in
`tools/results/king-subterms-20261006/` (ignored storage; files named below);
the instrument is `tools/diag/king_subterms.py` with
`tools/diag/patches/sf9587_king_subterms.patch`, `tools/diag/ks_weight_scan.py`
and `tools/diag/king_inputs_rarog.py`, each with its tests.

## Decision needed

RAR-E17 read the donor's king term as the one regular family with a residual
(+0.66% of Rarog's held-out outcome loss, +1.58% in the middlegame band) and
RAR-E18 left the donor's excess above six men to the scale factor, unmeasured.
C.3 is the first unit of Phase C: king safety with winnability and scaling
(C.5.1, C.5.2) and the shared inputs both need, one fit, one margin block, one
gate. Before it can be implemented the card owes: which sub-term carries the
residual; why `ks_weak_ring`, `ks_flank_attack` and `ks_shelter_storm` were
fitted to zero; whether the scale factor or the complexity term adds anything
above six men and in which material; the shared inputs and their cost; how the
unit keeps the score distribution the frozen search was fitted to; and the
frozen prediction, falsifier and handoff.

- The per-node cost of the donor-shaped inputs in Rarog's structure: estimated

| | Hypothesis | Standing after RAR-E21 |

| H1a | The danger map carries most of the king residual | **supported**: 92% alone, 72% by leave-one-out; the map's nonlinearity is more than half of it |

## Interaction map

Shared inputs → king term → static evaluation → the cp-valued margins
(reverse futility, razoring, futility, null move, ProbCut, SEE, delta,
aspiration), correction history keyed by pawn and piece structure, the TT's
stored evaluation, `CoreEvalMaterialScale`. The lazy gate reads the running
material + piece-square + pawn sum: the king term runs only below it, so a
king change moves scores in the ≤ 600 cp bands, which is where the margins
are densest and where RAR-E13's +12.3% tree came from. Inside the
evaluation: the mobility difference makes the king term a consumer of the
mobility producer (order: pieces and mobility before king, as today);
shelter/storm needs the pawn structure and the king square (the pawn cache
holds the first, the entry must learn the second); the attackers, king-adjacent
attacks and weak ring come from the attack maps the producer builds. The
mop-up and its two const assertions are untouched by the unit; the unit's
cap keeps its own term far below the decisive band. Fitting: the index
components are inside a square, so the linear trace cannot fit them; the
re-evaluation pass fits them (`--tune-kingsafety`'s method) and the linear
trace fits the two map scales, the shelter tables and the pawnless flank.

## Prospective prediction (frozen here; the C.3 registration copies it before the first game)

1. Static screen on the fitted candidate: the donor's king family gains under
   **+0.20%** against the candidate's played score over all rows (probability
   0.7) and under +0.50% in the middlegame band (0.7). If it stays above
   +0.40% over all rows the implementation did not capture the donor's
   information and C.3 returns to `RESEARCH` before any game.
2. Magnitude: against the head on validation, the candidate's mean shift
   toward the score's sign stays under **20 cp** in each band ≤ 600 and the
   share of rows above the lazy gate moves by under 2 points (probability
   0.6). A larger shift flags the candidate for the margin block before gate
   1 (PROCESS step 1), it does not reject it.
3. Tree read: the depth-12 tree over about 70 game positions grows by **+3%
   to +12%**, more in middlegames (probability 0.7 that it grows); `bench 13`
   moves between −2% and +10%.
4. Speed: pooled-PGO NPS within **±1%** of the head (probability 0.7); the
   second pass over enemy pieces goes away and the new inputs are a few
   bitboard operations each.
5. Gate 1, `[0,3]` at `3+0.03`, search unchanged: **+4 Elo**, 80% band
   [−8, +15]; probability of passing 0.35. The RAR-E19/E20 coupling is the
   reason for the low pass probability: the change moves scores in exactly
   the bands the margins were fitted on.
6. Margin block: the coordinates that compare the static evaluation with a
   bound in middlegames move most: `CoreRfp*`, `CoreRazor*`, `CoreFp*`,
   `QsFutilityMargin`, `QsDeltaMargin` by 5–15% away from the head, mostly
   upward; `AspirationDelta` up; `CoreCorrUpdate*` and the SEE constants
   under 5%. Gate 2 after the block: probability of passing 0.55; expected
   +6 Elo, 80% band [−4, +16].
7. The `10+0.1` read of an accepted unit: non-negative (probability 0.75).
   Most likely failure: gate 1 and gate 2 both fail inside the harm bound,
   which rule 6 counts as a rejected cluster and PROCESS step 6 may re-gate
   once at `10+0.1` if the cost is nodes.

## Falsifiers and stop conditions

- Prediction 1's +0.40% line: the implementation, not the design, failed;
  back to `RESEARCH` without games.
- Both gates fail: a worse function for this engine; the unit is rejected,
  its record states the tree read, the static screens and the margin block's
  movement; the retry trigger is C.10's joint tune or a changed corpus
  (C.8).
- A tree read above +20% with gate 1 failing outside the harm bound: stop
  before the margin block and reconsider the cap.
- The cluster shape's caps and bounds are fixed at registration and never
  changed after games.

## Decision

1. **C.3's king part: `READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION`.** The unit builds the
   donor's danger index in the donor's shape and scale with a smooth map,
   replaces Rarog's 40-bucket table and its inputs, and rebuilds shelter and
   storm by file and rank with the castling destination in the pawn cache.
   The flank terms, the king-to-pawn distance and the material table are
   out.
2. **C.5.2's generic scaling: `NO_CHANGE` for the first unit**, by RAR-E21's
   frozen rule (the scale factor under +0.30% at seven men or more, the
   complexity term under +0.15% there). The unit is king safety alone; the
   name "with winnability and scaling" is kept on the step and resolved as
   "measured, not built".
3. **C.5.1** keeps the material cut. Its first zero-game step was run the
   same day as RAR-E22: with one Stockfish version the families carry all of
   the donor's gain above six men, so the step that remains is the pure
   opposite-bishop cohort as a candidate for refitting Rarog's existing
   `opposite_bishop_scale` constants, read on the drawn-cohort instrument
   before any game.
4. **The 1.1 points above six men** were RAR-E17's version mixture
   (RAR-E22); no obligation remains.

## Implementation handoff (C.3, king part)

**Goal.** Replace Rarog's king-danger model and its linear shelter and storm
terms with the donor's mechanisms in Rarog's structure, so that the king
family's donor-direction residual falls to the noise, without moving the
score distribution the frozen search was fitted to.

**Semantics, per side (the side whose king is evaluated is "us").**

- *King ring:* the king's attacks and square with the king clamped to files
  B–G and ranks 2–7, minus squares defended by two of our pawns (the donor's
  ring; Rarog's forward-shifted zone is retired).
- *Accumulated in the attack loop* (producer, C.1's `eval/attacks.rs`, which
  the unit extends; C.1 itself adds nothing): per enemy piece whose attacks
  touch our ring, `attackers_count += 1`, `attackers_weight += W[piece]`
  (seeds N 81, B 52, R 44, Q 10), `king_attacks += popcount(attacks ∩ our
  king's attacks)`; the enemy pawn attacks on the ring count once into
  `attackers_count` at initialisation.
- *Weak squares:* enemy-attacked squares that we attack at most once and only
  by our king or queen; `weak_ring = popcount(ring ∩ weak)`.
- *Safe checks by piece type* from the squares a rook, bishop, queen or knight
  could check from (sliders with our queen removed from the occupancy),
  attacked by that enemy piece type, safe (not enemy-occupied, not attacked by
  us unless weak and attacked twice by them); queen checks exclude rook-check
  squares and squares our queen attacks; bishop checks exclude queen-check
  squares; single and multiple checks weighted separately (seeds 792/1283,
  645/967, 1084/1897, 772/1119); the non-safe remainder counts as unsafe
  checks.
- *Pinned blockers:* our pieces pinned to our king (count); the board has no
  pin set today, so the unit computes them from two slider x-rays per side
  (cost below); this is the one input that may be dropped on the NPS read.
- *Index* `kd = attackers_count·attackers_weight + 185·weak_ring +
  148·unsafe + 98·blockers + 69·king_attacks + mobility_mg(them) −
  mobility_mg(us) − 873·[no enemy queen] − 100·[our knight defends a square
  next to our king] − 6·shelter_mg/8 + 37 + safe_checks`, all seeds the
  donor's and all fitted by the re-evaluation pass; **flank attack² and flank
  defence are not built.** Seeds are quoted in the donor's internal units
  (`PawnValueEg` 206 = 100 cp): the index keeps its integer units, and the
  implementation converts the map scales, the shelter tables and the
  pawnless flank to Rarog's centipawn scale by 100/206 before the refit
  decides them.
- *Map:* when `kd > 100`, `mg −= ks_map_mg · kd²/4096` and `eg −= ks_map_eg ·
  kd/16`, with `ks_map_mg`, `ks_map_eg` linear coefficients (seed 1, 1)
  traced for Texel; the index is clamped at **`KS_INDEX_CAP = 1600`** before
  the map (a structure constant, not fitted: 303 cp mg, 49 cp eg at the seed
  scales; the donor's p90 per king is 1,246). The cap is the magnitude
  contract's instrument; its value changes only by a recorded decision with a
  new magnitude read.
- *Shelter and storm* in the pawn cache: per side, the donor's
  `evaluate_shelter` (our frontmost unopposed pawn and their frontmost pawn
  on the three files around the king clamped to B–G; shelter strength by
  edge distance × our rank, unblocked storm by edge distance × their rank,
  blocked storm by their rank into mg and eg; a constant seed), taken as the
  maximum by mg over the current square and the castling destinations we
  can still reach. Stored in the entry with the king square and castling
  rights it was computed for, recomputed when they differ (about one call in
  five, by the donor's measurement). The four `shelter_*` and two `storm_*`
  linear terms and `ks_shelter_storm` are removed. The shelter mg also feeds
  the index (the feedback term).
- *Pawnless flank:* kept as a linear term outside the index, mg and eg
  (seeds 17, 95), replacing `ks_pawnless_flank` inside it.
- *Removed:* `king_safety_table[40]`, `king_safety_unit_*`, `ks_weak_ring`,
  `ks_safe_check_*`, `ks_queen_relief`, `ks_flank_attack`,
  `ks_pawnless_flank`, `ks_shelter_storm`, `shelter_missing_*`,
  `shelter_dist*`, `storm_*`. `eval_king_centrality_danger` stays.

**Why local evidence says it should work here.** The danger map carries
92% of the only large family residual on this engine's own positions; its
two most informative components are the ones Rarog cannot express on its
bucket scale; the map's nonlinearity is more than half of the information;
and the three inputs' zeros were a resolution limit, not an absence of
signal.

**Producer, state, consumers.**

| input | producer | state | consumers | per-node cost on an evaluation-cache miss (estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| king ring, attackers count and weight, king-adjacent attacks | `eval/attacks.rs`, filled in the attack loop | per-evaluation locals | king | two ANDs and a popcount per piece; replaces today's second pass over enemy pieces (four piece-type loops per side) — net at or below zero |
| weak ring, safe and unsafe checks | king, from the maps | locals | king | today's two slider lookups from the king square per side, plus the exclusion ANDs and popcounts: about +10 ns per side |
| pinned blockers | king, two slider x-rays per side | locals | king | about +20–30 ns per side; dropped if the pooled NPS read attributes more than 0.5% to it |
| mobility mg per side | the mobility consumer (pieces) | two `i32` kept per side instead of the summed difference | king | free |
| shelter and storm | pawn cache | per entry and side: king square (`u8`), castling rights (`u8`), shelter mg and eg (`i16`, `i16`): 12 bytes per entry, 196 KB over 16,384 entries | king | two compares on a hit; on recompute three files × (two frontmost-pawn finds and two table reads) × up to three king squares: about 40–120 ns |
| the map | king | — | `mg`, `eg` | one multiply, one shift, one compare |

Expected total: inside ±1% NPS, read by PROCESS's pooled-PGO method; the
budget is 1.5% (about 3 Elo at RAR-S98's rate), beyond which the blockers go
first and the castling alternatives second.

## Magnitude contract

The unit keeps the score distribution by |score| band where the frozen
search expects it. Instrument: the full-vs-played method on
`hce-v3-tb/validation.csv` (the candidate's played score from the `texel`
build's `--dump-scores` with the lazy gate honoured, against RAR-E17's
`scores.csv` with the damping undone). Read before any game: per band (0–100,
101–300, 301–600, 601–1200, > 1200), the mean shift toward the score's sign,
the mean |score|, the p90 of |score|, and the share of rows above the lazy
gate. Flag rule (frozen): a mean shift toward the sign above 20 cp in any
band ≤ 600, or more than 2 points of change in the share above the gate,
flags the candidate for the margin block before gate 1. Structural bound:
`KS_INDEX_CAP` with the const assertion. The seeds put the donor's king
term at roughly twice Rarog's in the 101–600 bands; the refit on the played
function decides the scales, and the read says what they did.
