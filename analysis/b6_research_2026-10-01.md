### The journals: how much gradient each tune was still carrying (new)

`journal_read.py`. An SPSA step is `(a_k / c_k) · difference_k · δ_ik` with
`δ_ik` a fair random sign, so without a gradient a coordinate's trajectory
is a random-sign walk with the observed step sizes, and
`z = net travel / sqrt(sum of squared steps)` has variance 1 whatever the
schedule. A run's signal is `mean(z²)`; its null comes from re-drawing every
step's sign 2,000 times. Wires asserted on every run: the journal is
complete, every step equals the journal's own schedule times the
difference (0 steps off in all four runs), and the last centres round to
the tuned values. The full-step movers and their sizes equal the ledger's.

| Read | Coordinates | `mean(z²)` | Null 95% | p | `\|z\| ≥ 2` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| RAR-S78, whole run | 82 | 1.62 | 1.27 | 0.0005 | 10 |
| RAR-S78, first half | 82 | 1.48 | 1.28 | 0.004 | 9 |
| RAR-S78, second half | 82 | 0.99 | 1.27 | 0.52 | 4 |
| RAR-S78, second half, the 74 no later tune touched | 74 | 1.02 | 1.28 | 0.43 | 4 |
| RAR-S78, last 2,000 iterations, the same 74 | 74 | 0.94 | 1.30 | 0.63 | 3 |
| RAR-S83 block 1 | 36 | 1.12 | 1.42 | 0.28 | 2 |
| RAR-S83 block 2 | 36 | 0.99 | 1.38 | 0.51 | 0 |
| RAR-S86 block 1 | 14 | 1.46 | 1.65 | 0.11 | 1 |
| RAR-S86 block 1 without `QsFutilityMargin` | 13 | 0.68 | — | — | 0 |

- RAR-S78's gradient was spent in its first 2,500 iterations. Its second
  half, and the last half of every later block, is indistinguishable from a
  walk with no gradient.
- `QsFutilityMargin` is the one coordinate with a gradient at the end of
  its tune: `z` +3.4 over the block, +2.4 in its second half (150 → 178,
  and 189 in the partial second block).
- Of the eight RAR-S78 coordinates that a later tune re-fitted after a
  cluster landed, one reads `|z| ≥ 2`: `CoreLmrQuiet` in B.3.3's first block
  (+2.57, then +1.43 in the second; 1,421 → 1,932 over both). The other two
  LMR bases and the five reverse-futility and razoring coordinates in
  B.4.3 do not (largest +1.24).

**The instrument's limit.** The read sees an effect the size of RAR-S78's
(its excess over the null is 0.62 against a threshold near 0.28) and would
miss one about half that size. RAR-S77 is the measured warning: a tail in
which no coordinate moved a full step was worth +4.43 ± 2.90 Elo. So the
journals rule out another RAR-S78 in plain sight and say nothing about a
gain of a few Elo.

## Unknowns

- What a fresh-schedule block on the joint surface would gain. Nothing
  short of the block measures it.
- The Elo value of a unit of `mean(z²)`: one calibration point (RAR-S78)
  is not a scale.

## Hypotheses

- **H1, settled at this resolution.** Each cluster's own tune stopped by
  its rule, the B.2 surface spent its gradient in RAR-S78's first half, and
  a restart random-walks 120 coordinates for a gain inside the gate's dead
  zone.
- **H2, coupling left behind.** As in RAR-S78, coordinates that barely
  moved carry a gradient that appears only under a fresh schedule, now
  enlarged by two clusters changing the tree; a restart gains +5 Elo or
  more.
- **H3, one coordinate.** `QsFutilityMargin` and little else.

The journals fit H1 and H3 and cannot exclude a small H2.

## Interaction map

- **C.10** re-fits the search's cp-valued margins after the new evaluation;
  `QsFutilityMargin`, reverse futility, futility, razoring and the SEE
  margins are among them, so a fit of those now is spent twice.
- **A new evaluation moves every node population**, not only cp margins:
  the rate table above shows two search clusters alone moved futility
  pruning 42% and the correction residual 16%. The non-cp families (LMR,
  histories, corrections) will be misfit after C in the way they were
  before RAR-S75.
- **B.7.2** needs an exact fingerprint per change; a bake between its
  changes re-declares the fingerprint. **B.9** freezes the search head for
  C.
- **D.1** owns the time management multipliers, fitted for the 2.x search;
  they are not search coordinates and join no search tune.

## Prospective prediction

Frozen in `tools/results/b6-20261001/FROZEN.md` before the first run of the
journal read (file times 16:28:04 and 16:28:25); it should have been
registered in the ledger first and was not.

**Rule:** the first block is justified by the journals when (1) the second
half of RAR-S78 reads above its null's 95% point on the coordinates no later
tune touched, or (2) at least three of the RAR-S78 coordinates re-fitted
after a cluster landed read `|z| ≥ 2` in the block that re-fitted them.
Neither: `NO_CHANGE` unless other evidence is named.

**Read:** (1) 1.02 against 1.28, not met. (2) one of eight, not met. The
frozen text says nine shared coordinates; `CoreNmpBase` was never in
RAR-S78's surface, so there are eight.

**Calibration.**
- RAR-S78 whole run above 2.0 (0.8): missed, 1.62. Magnitude: I expected a
  +13 Elo tune to stand further from noise than it does.
- RAR-S78 second half above its null (0.6): missed. Mechanism: the gradient
  was spent by the half; the full-step count at the end reflects travel
  made early.
- B.3.3 block 1 above its null (0.6): missed, 1.12; block 2 not (0.6): hit.
- `QsFutilityMargin` `z ≥ 3` (0.7): hit, 3.41; the block without it inside
  its null (0.6): hit, 0.68.
- At least three shared coordinates (0.4): no, one.
- Instrument: I froze the rule before checking the read's power. RAR-S77
  already showed that movement is not an Elo proxy, so the rule's
  "neither" branch is weaker than its wording.

## Falsifiers and stop conditions

For the recommendation below, the triggers that reopen it:
- C.10 is reached: the joint re-fit runs there (see *Decision*).
- A registered game read shows a direction (RAR-S80's rule, +5 Elo or more
  with the lower bound above −5) on any carried coordinate: it becomes a
  seed and its neighbours a registered surface.
- B.9's checkpoint attributes a same-evaluation deficit to selectivity
  that a named family of coordinates controls.

## Decision

**Recommended: `NO_CHANGE`. Do not run the joint tune now.**

- No instrument shows a misfit. The journals read noise at the end of
  every tune; the one coordinate with a gradient is a cp margin C.10
  re-fits; cluster-to-neighbour re-centring was measured twice and moved
  one coordinate of eight.
- PROCESS's go/no-go asks for local evidence that consumers are misfit and
  cancels a tune whose plausible gain is inside the gate's dead zone. My
  estimate of a restart's gain is +3 ± 3 Elo: a judgment from the series
  above (+13.1, then nothing resolvable beyond one coordinate), not a
  measurement. About a third of that range is below what `[0,3]` can bank.
- The cost if it is run: 12.5 hours per block, up to three; then a `[0,3]`
  gate that needs about 36,000 games (7 hours) at a true +3 Elo and about
  70,000 (14 hours) at +2.
- The same hours buy more after the evaluation programme, when every
  population has moved and the whole surface is misfit at once. **Input to
  C.10:** select its surface by the firing-rate check against the B-era
  head, not by "cp-valued" alone, so the non-cp families whose consumers
  moved 10% or more are re-fitted in the same tune.

What the recommendation gives up: the expected few Elo between now and
C.10, on a search that B.9 freezes for the whole of C.

**Maintainer decision, 2026-10-01:** skip now; the joint search SPSA is a
numbered step after the classical evaluation, where the optimum is
expected to have moved. PLAN C.10 is rewritten as that step, with the
surface rule, the seeds and the carried items above. `CoreIirMinDepth`'s
reads are undecided.

**The carried items:**
- `QsFutilityMargin`: to C.10 with its measured direction (rising from
  178).
- `QsCountLimit`, `CoreQsNoisyHistory`: stay as they are; no read at
  2,000 games resolves an effect this small.
- `CoreNmpVerifyDepth`, `NmpMinNonPawnPieces`, `CoreEvalRule50Damping`:
  rarely reached at `3+0.03`; no gradient to fit there.
- `LazyMargin`: an evaluation margin; C owns it.
- `ProbCutSeeGapScale`: joins C.10's surface.
- `CoreIirMinDepth`: a discrete threshold on a mechanism that fires at 15%
  of interior nodes; raising it to 10 multiplies `bench 13` ninefold. It has
  never been read by games on this search. A pair of 2,000-game reads (3
  and 6 against 4, about 40 minutes) is the cheap test; my prior for a
  direction is about 0.1, so it is offered, not recommended.
- `CoreLmpSquare`: its range floor (256) is widened before any tune that
  includes it.
