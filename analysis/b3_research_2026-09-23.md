# B.3 — proof searches and extensions on the fitted B.2 head

**Status: research packet for PLAN B.3, 2026-09-23; the implementation
contract for B.3.1 and the registered numbers for B.3.2 (RAR-S79).** B.0's
handoff (`search_programme_2026-09-13.md` §3.4, §3.5, §13.3) froze the
mechanisms on 2026-09-13 against the B.1 head. Since then B.2 rebuilt the
node around them, RAR-S75 fitted 82 constants (+139 Elo over the seeds), and
the head is 7,185,678 / EBF 2.444. This packet re-bases every activation
number on that head, re-reads the donors, and fixes what B.3.1 builds.

in what form, with what seeds, and what B.3.2 must show before the SPSA and

## Unknowns

1. Whether the cut-node-only NMP population loses the all-node cutoffs Rarog
   takes today (the head converts 22% of attempts; the oracle 83%).
2. Whether ProbCut's deeper marginal verification costs more than the
   confirmations it removes (98.2% survive today).
3. Whether the `4·d` singular margin is Rarog's optimum or a relic of the
   pre-B.2 tree; the donor's `~0.5·d` would double the attempts that extend.
4. Whether IIR at 12.3% of interior nodes is paying for itself on the fitted
   tree, where Reckless has none.
5. Whether adding the LMR singular-margin term disturbs RAR-S75's fitted LMR
   constants (it was a zero slot when they were fitted).

## Hypotheses

- **H1 (B.0's, kept):** the donor's proof-search shapes — NMP at cut nodes
  with a verification region and a bound shortcut, ProbCut with margin-scaled
  verification depth and a lerp return, singular with graded extensions,
  soft multi-cut, −3 negative extension and LDSE — recover a coherent share
  of the remaining selectivity deficit. Prediction: +8 ± 8 Elo fitted.
- **H2 (competing):** the head's gains came from the move-loop cluster and
  these four mechanisms are already near their optimum in Rarog's form; the
  cluster measures +0 ± 5 fitted. Discriminated by the gate; partially by
  the unfitted paired run (H2 predicts it at or below zero).
- **H3 (competing, mechanism-local):** the strength is in ProbCut's
  verification and NMP's population alone; singular's graded extensions are
  neutral at STC. Discriminated by B.3.2's bit ablation on the fixed-node
  screens and, if the paired run is positive but small, by a categorical
  2,000-game run with bit 6 off.

**The reverse-futility crossover (checked 2026-09-23).** RFP runs before
NMP and returns at every non-`tt_pv` node with `est ≥ beta + margin`, and
B.2's fitted margin is `135·d²/16 + 17·d − 26` (not improving, no
correction, threatened): 101 at depth 3, 177 at 4, 270 at 5, 380 at 6, 650
at 8. NMP's population under the donor rule is the band
`beta + m ≤ est < beta + margin_RFP`; with the seed `m = 150 − 4d` that band
is **empty at depth 3** (138 > 101), 43 units wide at depth 4 and open from
depth 5. The donor is built the same way (Reckless RFP 313 against NMP 292
at depth 5, RFP 433 against 283 at 6), so NMP is a deep-node device in both
engines; at `tt_pv` nodes, where RFP is off, NMP runs alone with its +50.
Consequences: NMP attempts fall far more than the first P3 said; T1's
expected yield is small (the head's bit-2 ablation was already near-inert)
and its value is the verification region and the `cutoff_count` consumer;
`CoreNmpBase` is in the curvature sweep for exactly this. Rarog's own
population evidence (RAR-S57/S58 at `!tt_pv`, any node type) outranks the
donor's cut-node shape for both NMP and ProbCut, so each population is a
categorical with Rarog's as the default. The new LMR margin term lands on
LMR bases RAR-S75 fitted without it, so `CoreLmrQuiet`, `CoreLmrNoisy` and
`CoreLmrCutNode` join the B.3 surface. **Forward:** ProbCut's qsearch
verification and LDSE read today's quiescence; B.4 re-reads `probcut_qpass`
when it changes the quiescence.

Feedback loops: multi-cut and ProbCut returns are not stored as exact scores
(donor: ProbCut stores Lower, multi-cut stores nothing), so they change which
nodes ever get an exact entry; the LMR margin term trains history through the
re-search rule. B.3.2 reports activation *and* cutoff composition, as B.2.2
did.

## Cheapest discriminating tests

In order, each on the fixed-node screens before any game:

1. Off-arm identity: `--no-default-features`-style flag off reproduces
   7,185,678 / EBF 2.444 and every 40 per-position line.
2. Stride-1 counters on the candidate: the P3–P5 predictions below are the
   first thing that can fail, and they fail cheaply (a wiring defect shows
   as a conversion that did not move).
3. The bit ablation sweep (bits 2, 3, 4, 6) on WAC at 100k and the canaries:
   locates a defect to a mechanism.
4. Categoricals as baseline/A/B/A+B on the fixed-node screens plus
   time-to-depth: `CoreNmpNodes` {Rarog `!tt_pv` any type, cut nodes},
   `CoreProbcutNodes` {Rarog `!tt_pv` any type, Reckless cut nodes with the
   quiet-TT-move veto}, `CoreIirPolicy` {current, off, SF-form},
   `CoreProbcutTtServed` {off, on}, `SingularTtDepthMargin` {3, 2}. Zero games choose the default;
   one 2,000-game categorical run only where the screens disagree.
5. The 2,000-game unfitted paired run against the head (maintainer-run): the
   one screen that clears the cluster for its fit.

## Prospective prediction (frozen 2026-09-23, before any exposure)

- **P1** unfitted paired run, 2,000 games: **+5 ± 12 Elo**; P(≤ −40) 0.10.
- **P2** fitted, after the SPSA, the gate: **+8 ± 8 Elo** (about +12 nElo);
  under `[0,3]`: H1 0.60, cap 0.25, H0 0.15.
- **P3** NMP (amended the same day after the RFP crossover check, before

  more at cut nodes only; conversion rises from 22% to ≥ 35% (likely above
  50%: the band sits between two margins); verification failures rise above
  0.3% of verifications (the region makes them real). Falsifier: conversion
  below 28% means the margin did not land or its sign is wrong; attempts
  falling by less than 40% means RFP is not running first.
- **P4** ProbCut: at the default population attempts fall by 10–30% (the
  TT gate alone), at the Reckless population by ≥ 30%; cuts per attempt stay

  not wired.
- **P5** Singular: attempts per interior node within ±30% of 4.9%; the
  negative-extension share rises to ≥ 20% of attempts (the cut-node
  condition); multi-cut 10–20%; triple extensions < 5%.
- **P6** Branching stays in [1.60, 1.80]; WAC at 100k ≥ 215 unfitted; no

- **P7** Time-to-depth ≤ 1.05x of the head: the cluster adds no per-node

- **P8** IIR: `off` costs > 5% time-to-depth; the default stays `current` or

## Falsifiers and stop conditions

- P1 ≤ −40: a defect, back to `IMPLEMENTED`, the bit sweep names it.
- P3 or P4 falsified on the counters: wiring, fixed before any game.
- Gate H0 or cap: the cluster is rejected as a unit; PLAN rule 6 counts it;
  the ablation sweep record says which bits to carry to B.6.
- A floor failure never holds a candidate the paired run cleared (rule 8).

## Decision

**READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION, with the contract below.** B.0's mechanism
decisions stand; three things change on the new evidence: NMP's population
moves to cut nodes and its verification becomes a region (today's is inert);
ProbCut keeps Rarog's measured filter and cap and adopts the donor's
verification depth and return; the singular margin is seeded at Rarog's own
`4·d`, not the donor's `0.5·d`, with the sweep deciding whether it curves.
IIR becomes a categorical decided by zero-game screens, and so do the NMP
and ProbCut populations, defaulting to Rarog's measured `!tt_pv` any-type
population over the donors' cut-node one. T1's expected yield is small (see
the crossover note); it stays for the verification region and the
`cutoff_count` consumer, and the bit sweep will say. The gate uses the
default `[0,3]` bracket rather than B.0's `[0,5]`: the prior is moderate and
RAR-M10 shows `[0,5]` rejects a true +4 nElo the default would accept; no
game has been played, so the bracket is fixed here, before any.

## Forward compatibility: what later phases consume from B.3, and what B.3 must not fix in place

Read from PLAN B.4–B.9, C, D, F and G on 2026-09-23. The rule for the
programme is that a later cluster consumes B.3 through the same few
interfaces B.2 established, never by editing B.3's mechanisms around its own
needs; so B.3 is built so that those interfaces are the only coupling.

**What B.3 closes that B.2 left open on purpose.** Three B.2 producers get
their consumers here: `cutoff_count[ply+1]` in the NMP margin, ProbCut's TT
Lower store in singular admission (RAR-S31's population), and the singular
score in the LMR margin term. That is the sense in which the clusters are
meant to strengthen each other: B.2 chose its producers so that B.3 could
consume them without new state.

**Rules for the implementation (bind B.3.1):**

- R1 one `est`: every eval-unit comparison in T1–T3 reads `eval_for_pruning`;
  `static_eval` appears only in the ProbCut SEE-gap filter.
- R2 no fork: cfg-gated blocks inside `core/node.rs`; no second node, no
  duplicated helper; the diff is readable as "these blocks change".
- R3 no globals: `nmp_min_ply` and any new state live on the thread's data
  and are reset at search start; helpers verify independently.
- R4 the root is out: `NODE::ROOT` excludes every B.3 mechanism; MultiPV's
  identity at `MultiPV = 1` is asserted by the existing test.
- R5 names persist: counters keep their names where semantics are unchanged;
  new ones are listed in the contract; `nmp_cut` gains a depth split
  (`nmp_cut_d3_6`, `nmp_cut_d7_12`, `nmp_cut_d13_plus`) for B.4.
- R6 decisive scores: `is_win`, `is_loss`, `is_decisive` on `TB_WIN_SCORE`,
  as RFP does; no mechanism returns or stores a mate it did not prove.
- R7 depth order: IIR and hindsight adjust `depth` at entry; pruning reads the
  adjusted depth; singular, negative extension and LDSE act per move on it;
  the child depth is asserted ≥ 1 in debug builds.

## Implementation contract (B.3.1, class `I2`)

Feature `b3proof = ["b2core"]` (it requires the core; the legacy search
never compiles it), off by default until B.3.4 flips it; the off arm is
byte-for-byte the head's search (7,185,678 / EBF 2.444, 40/40 lines). Version
string `+b3proof` on the arm. Constants live in `CoreParams` under `Core*`
names as spins; categoricals are switches, never SPSA coordinates.

**T1 NMP** (`src/search/core/node.rs`, the block at the null-move guard):
- Population: `CoreNmpNodes` switch, 0 (default) = Rarog's measured
  population, `!tt_pv` at any node type; 1 = cut nodes only, the donors'.
  Below, `<population>` stands for whichever is selected.
- Entry: `<population> && !in_check && excluded.is_null() && !potential_singularity
  && ply >= td.nmp_min_ply && nmp_material_ok && !is_loss(beta)
  && !is_win(est)` and `est >= beta + max(2, m)`, with
  `m = CoreNmpBase − CoreNmpDepth·depth + CoreNmpTtPv·tt_pv
  − CoreNmpImprovement·improvement/1024 − CoreNmpCutoff·(cutoff_count[ply+1] < 2)`,
  seeds 150 / 4 / 50 / 43 / 10 (Reckless 337 / 9 / 110 / 94 / 21 × 0.457).
  No null move when the TT bound is Lower and the TT move captures a piece of
  at least knight value. `nmp_material_ok` stays Rarog's.
- Reduction, in 1024ths of a ply:
  `R = (CoreNmpRBase + CoreNmpRImproving·improving + CoreNmpRDepth·depth
  + CoreNmpREval·clamp(est − beta, 0, CoreNmpRClamp)/128) / 1024`,
  seeds 4407 / 917 / 265 / 1044 / 542 (the eval slope is 477 per Reckless
  unit, so 477 / 0.457 per Rarog unit; the clamp 1187 × 0.457).
- Bound shortcut: the null search runs against `tt_score` instead of `beta`
  when the TT score is valid, below beta, the bound is Lower and
  `depth − 2 <= tt_depth`; a fail-high against that bound counts.
- Verification: below `CoreNmpVerifyDepth` (16) or inside a region, a
  fail-high returns; otherwise `reduced = depth / 2` if the score is below
  beta (bound case) else `depth − R`, set `td.nmp_min_ply = ply + 3·reduced/4`,
  search `[beta − 1, beta]` at `reduced` with the null move disabled for every
  node at ply below `nmp_min_ply`, then clear it. Return the null score only
  if the verification holds. Never return an unproven mate (keep Rarog's
  clamp to beta).
- `nmp_min_ply` is per-thread state on `ThreadData`, zero at every search
  start; helpers own their own.
- A verification search runs at this ply and overwrites `stack[ply]`;
  restore `tt_pv` and `laterality` afterwards, as the singular search does.
- Score helpers on Rarog's own bounds: `is_win(s) = s >= TB_WIN_SCORE`,
  `is_loss(s) = s <= −TB_WIN_SCORE`, `is_decisive(s) = |s| >= TB_WIN_SCORE`,
  the convention RFP already uses.

**T2 ProbCut:**
- Population: `CoreProbcutNodes` switch, 0 (default) = Rarog's measured
  population (RAR-S57/S58), `!tt_pv` at any node type, no quiet-TT-move
  veto; 1 = Reckless's, cut nodes only with no ProbCut when the TT move is
  quiet.
- Entry: `<population> && !is_win(beta) && excluded.is_null() && !in_check`,
  TT gate on both populations: when a TT score is valid it must be
  `>= probcut_beta` and not decisive, otherwise `est >= beta`.
- `probcut_beta = beta + CoreProbcutBase − CoreProbcutImproving·improving`,
  seeds 180 (Rarog's fitted) / 39 (85 × 0.457).
- Keep the SEE-gap filter (`see_ge(mv, (probcut_beta − static_eval)·
  ProbCutSeeGapScale/100)`, floored at 0) and the searched-move cap, now one
  coordinate `CoreProbcutMoveCap` seeded 4 (today's 2 + 2 at cut nodes).
- Depth: `base = max(0, depth − 4 − improving)`; after a qsearch pass,
  `probcut_depth = clamp(base − (score − probcut_beta)/CoreProbcutDepthDiv, 0, base)`
  (seed 146 = 319 × 0.457); search at `probcut_depth` against
  `adjusted = min(INF, probcut_beta + CoreProbcutAdjust·(base − probcut_depth))`
  (seed 90 = 197 × 0.457); if it fails below `adjusted` while
  `probcut_beta < adjusted`, re-search at `base` against `probcut_beta`,
  otherwise `probcut_beta = adjusted`.
- On a cut: store Lower at `probcut_depth + 1` with the move; return the
  score if decisive, else `lerp(score, beta, CoreProbcutLerp/1024)` (seed 276
  = 0.2695).
- `CoreProbcutTtServed` (switch, default 0): when 1, before the block, a TT
  Lower bound at `tt_depth >= depth − 4` with `tt_score >= beta +
  CoreProbcutTtMargin` (seed 208 = 428 × 0.485) and neither decisive returns
  that margin beta.

**T3 Singular, LDSE and the LMR term:**
- `potential_singularity = depth >= 5 + tt_pv && tt_depth >= depth −
  SingularTtDepthMargin && bound != Upper && valid && !decisive`, computed
  once per node (NMP consumes it).
- Margin: `k = CoreSingularMargin` (seed 4, Rarog's fitted `4·d`);
  `margin = (Exact ? ceil(depth/4) : depth)·k + depth·k·(tt_pv && !PV)`;
  `singular_beta = tt_score − margin`, `singular_depth = (depth − 1)/2`,
  exclusion search at the node's own `cut_node`.
- Below `singular_beta`: `extension = 1 + (score < singular_beta − double)
  + (score < singular_beta − triple)`, `double = CoreSingDoublePv·PV
  + CoreSingDoubleNotTtPv·(PV && !tt_was_pv) − CoreSingDoubleQuiet·quiet_tt
