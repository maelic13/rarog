# B.5 — root, aspiration and iterative deepening on the cluster-3 head

**Status: research packet for PLAN B.5, 2026-09-30; the decision, the gate's
candidate and the predictions (RAR-S89).** Measured on the accepted head,
`dev` at `80996ba`, default build **11,171,726 / EBF 2.512**, with diag-only
counters added for this research (behaviour-neutral: the diag build
reproduces the fingerprint). Raw runs, recipes, temporary variants and their
diffs are in `tools/results/b5-20260930/` (`recipe/`, `asp/`, `cand/`,
`variants/`, `deep/`, `interrupt_head.json`, `clock_*.json`).

## Decision needed

Which of the card's root mechanisms (aspiration delta from eval and PV
stability, optimism, root move accounting, forgotten-mate and aborted-loss
guards, the PV table, a bound on deep-iteration line growth, root LMR relief)
has a measured defect or opportunity on this head, in what form, and what
the cheapest test that can kill it is.

## Known evidence

### The aspiration loop is where the root loses its time

Counters added today: nodes of every root window search from depth 4
(`asp_search_nodes`) and of those that failed and were searched again
(`asp_fail_low_nodes`, `asp_fail_high_nodes`). On the head:

| Workload | Root-window nodes | In windows that failed | Fails per aspirated iteration |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `bench 13` | 11,160,104 | **61.2%** (4.79 M fail-high, 2.04 M fail-low) | 788 / 400 = 1.97 |
| `bench 15` | 54,709,694 | **69.7%** (29.1 M fail-high, 9.0 M fail-low) | — |
| 300 book positions at a `3000+30` clock (mean depth 11.7) | — | **45.9%** | 3,604 / 3,500 |
| the same at a `1000+30` clock (mean depth 10.3) | — | 46.7% | 3,217 / 3,100 |

The window is `±AspirationDelta` (21) around the previous score; a failing
side widens by `×1.5 + 5` *re-centred on the original centre*, not on the
failing score, up to `AspMaxFails` (20) times. A score that moves 100 units
between iterations therefore needs four full-depth re-searches
(21, 36, 59, 94, 146). The constants were fitted on the 2.3 search
(RAR-S17's standalone re-centring read, −4.52 ± 4.80, was taken against
them); B.2 to B.4 changed how far scores move between iterations and nobody
re-read the loop.

**This is also the cause of a floor that failed in B.3.2 and B.4.2.** Nodes
at depth 14 on the phase-4 suite against the oracle: the head reads 4.18×;
with one-retry aspiration **1.97×**, with branching 4–14 at **1.730**
against the oracle's 1.736. The depth-14 excess that two clusters recorded
as a failed floor was the root loop, not their mechanisms. B.4.2's "b15 cost"
is the same thing: that pawn ending's score swings and the loop re-searches
it (`deep/`).

that side fully.

### Candidates compared, the simple form against the donors' (`cand/summary.json`)

Two temporary builds of the loop (diffs in `variants/`, each reverted):
`recentre` (a failing window re-centres on the failing score, both donors)
and `reduce` (as `recentre`, and a fail-high re-search is one ply shallower
per consecutive fail-high, both donors); and `midpoint` (a fail-low keeps
beta at the old window's midpoint instead of halving toward the new alpha).
Canaries are scored against the head's own 91 passes.

| Candidate | `bench 13` | `bench 15` | Depth-14 nodes vs oracle | WAC 100k / 400k | Canaries (lost) | Depth at 300k |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---:|
| head | 1.00 | 1.00 | 4.18× | 232 / 266 | 91 | — |
| **`AspMaxFails = 1`** | **0.55** | **0.28** | **1.97×** | **241 / 275** | 94 (WAC.133, .143) | **+0.76 ply** |
| `AspMaxFails = 2` | 0.70 | 0.38 | 2.85× | 234 / 270 | 90 (4) | +0.17 |
| `AspirationDelta = 70` | 0.64 | 0.41 | 3.25× | 233 / 265 | 95 (WAC.143) | +0.00 |
| `recentre` | 0.79 | 0.40 | 3.69× | 236 / 266 | 91 (2) | +0.19 |
| `reduce` | 0.56 | 0.27 | 2.00× | 229 / 268 | 87 (5) | +0.88 |
| `reduce`, delta 30 | 0.56 | 0.29 | 2.05× | 246 / 270 | 89 (4) | +0.71 |
| `midpoint` with one retry | 0.63 | 0.33 | 2.21× | 232 / 271 | 91 (3) | +0.17 |

The donors' form and the one-retry setting reach the same tree; one retry is
better at fixed nodes (nine more WAC positions at both budgets) and shifts
fewer canaries. The donors' shallower re-search reports a nominal depth it
did not fully search, which is where its canary shifts come from
(WAC.013, .160, .172 in every `reduce` variant). The "cleaner" midpoint form
is worse than the plain one. **Under one retry the window's width is flat**
(`AspirationDelta` 9 to 100: 0.55 to 0.62 of the head's bench, WAC 234 to
246), and so is the reduction formula's window coefficient
(`CoreLmrPvWindow` 0 to 1200: within 14% and no order), so there is nothing
to fit: the change is one categorical.

### Under game-like clocks the gain is smaller, and that is the honest size

300 book positions, clocks `3000+30` and `1000+30`, diag build, idle host:

| Candidate | Mean depth at `3000+30` | Nodes used | Mean depth at `1000+30` | Nodes used |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| head | 11.68 | 1.00 | 10.33 | 1.00 |
| `AspMaxFails = 1` | 12.00 (**+0.32**) | 0.91 | 10.59 (**+0.26**) | 0.92 |
| `reduce` | 12.05 (+0.37, nominal) | 0.91 | 10.79 (+0.46, nominal) | 0.97 |
| `recentre` | 11.77 (+0.09) | 0.91 | 10.45 (+0.12) | 0.95 |

A third of a ply and 9% fewer nodes per move at the depths a `3+0.03` game
reaches; the two- and threefold savings belong to depth 14 and beyond, which
a slower time control reaches. A failed window is not pure waste either: the
head finds WAC.143's `g6h6` at depth 6 and every candidate at depth 8,
because the head's re-searches at one nominal depth are extra search. Fixed
nodes (WAC, depth at 300k) and the clock read are the fair instruments, and
both favour one retry; games decide.

### An interrupted iteration discards almost nothing

The root falls back to the last completed iteration when the clock stops it
inside one. Counted at the stop (`interrupt_head.json`):

| Clock | Searches | Stopped inside an iteration | A new best move discarded | An earlier window had failed high on a new move |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `3000+30` | 300 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| `1000+30` | 300 | 14 (5%) | 1 (0.3%) | 2 (0.7%) |
| `300+30` | 300 | 22 (7%) | 1 (0.3%) | 6 (2.0%) |

The soft stop ends almost every search between iterations. Using the
interrupted iteration's result, and the donors' forgotten-mate and
aborted-loss guards that go with it, would act on well under 2% of searches
at a short clock. **No change**, with a retry trigger: if D.1's time
management raises the share of searches stopped inside an iteration above
10%, this is re-read.

### Deep-iteration line growth (the note from B.3)

On bench position 11 the head runs lines to selective depth 125 at depth 15
with 18 M nodes; with one retry the same depth takes 1.1 M at selective
depth 50, and lines reach 109 to 115 only at depths 18 and 19 (`deep/`).
The loop multiplies the growth; it does not create it. No depth a `3+0.03`
or `10+0.1` game reaches is affected after the change, so **no bound is
added**; the question returns only with evidence from a slower control.

### Not supported by evidence on this head

- **Optimism** (Reckless: `113·avg/(|avg| + 201)` in the corrected
  evaluation). A donor feature with no measurement here; the consumer slot
  B.0 assumed does not exist in `core/correction.rs`. It is a producer into
  every evaluation-unit margin B.2 to B.4 fitted, so it is filed as its own
  research card (B.5.4) with a switch experiment and a game read, not
  carried inside this gate.
- **Delta from eval and PV stability, the `avg²` term, average-centred
  windows**: refinements of a window whose width is flat under one retry.
  No change.
- **Root LMR relief**: confirmed retired; the core's reduction has no root
  term beyond the PV and window terms.
- **Root move records and ordering by the previous iteration**: not
  measured; no defect observed. Left as unknown.

## Hypotheses

- **H1 (leading):** the head spends about half of its game-time nodes
  re-searching aspiration windows, and opening a failed side at once
  recovers a third of a ply at `3+0.03`: **+8 ± 8 Elo**, more at slower
  controls.
- **H2 (competing):** the re-searches are effective extra search at the
  same nominal depth (the WAC.143 observation), so the recovered time buys
  little: +0 to +3.
- **H3 (competing):** one retry loses more from full-window re-searches
  near the root (no cutoffs from a bound) than it saves at short time
  controls: negative. The clock read (+0.3 ply with 9% fewer nodes) argues
  against it; the gate decides.

## Interaction map

| Producer | Consumer | Shared signal, and where it can duplicate or cancel |
|---|---|---|
| the root window (`alpha`, `beta`) | every PV node's window; `root_delta` in the reduction formula (`CoreLmrPv`, `CoreLmrPvWindow`) | an opened side makes `root_delta` large, so the window term's relief vanishes in that re-search; swept under one retry: flat, no refit owed |
| fail-high and fail-low root searches | the TT (every re-search reads what the failed one stored), history tables | fewer re-searches write fewer entries and fewer history updates; WAC at fixed nodes improves, so the net is positive at zero games |
| root score sequence | the soft-stop formula (falling eval, best-move changes, effort) | unchanged inputs; iterations end sooner, so the soft stop fires 9% earlier in nodes; D.1 owns any re-fit of the time multipliers |
| `bench 13` | the fingerprint, PGO training workload | the fingerprint moves to the candidate's (6,192,452); PGO is rebuilt as always |
| MultiPV's per-line windows | B.2.0.2's contract (identity at `MultiPV = 1`, line semantics above) | the same `Aspiration` type serves both loops; the contract's tests run on the candidate |
| SMP pool-seeded window centre | helper threads | unchanged; the helpers' windows open on the same rule |
| B.4's interpolation | root scores | quiescence fail-highs are already pulled toward beta, which shortens score jumps; measured on the head as it is |
| C, F (evaluation programmes) | score volatility between iterations | a smoother evaluation makes narrow windows cheaper; the setting is a categorical B.6 and C.10 may re-read |

## Cheapest discriminating test

The candidate is one default: `AspMaxFails` 20 → 1. It needs no
implementation, the zero-game reads are done, and a fixed match of the same
pair beside a gate is not allowed (PROCESS, one gate, one read). The
cheapest test that can kill H1 is therefore **the gate itself**.

## Prospective prediction (frozen 2026-09-30, before any game)

- **P1** the gate, `[0,3]` nElo: **+8 ± 8 Elo**; H1 accepted 0.65, cap 0.15,
  H0 0.20. If H1, in under 6,000 pairs 0.4.
- **P2** the candidate's zero-game reads reproduce exactly on the gate
  binary: `bench 13` **6,192,452**, WAC at 100k 241 (a wire check, not a
  prediction).
- My record on this programme overstates (B.4: +8 predicted, +4.4 read), so
  P1's centre is shaded from what +0.3 ply would suggest.

## Falsifiers and stop conditions

- The gate binary's `bench 13` is not 6,192,452: a bake defect, fixed
  before any game.
- H0 or the cap rejects the change: nothing is baked; the record keeps the
  zero-game evidence and the question returns at B.6 or at a slower time
  control (a `10+0.1` read is the retry trigger, since the saving grows with
  depth).
- Bounds, cap, book and adjudication never change after games are seen.

## Decision

**`READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION`, and the implementation is one value.** I
recommend gating `AspMaxFails = 1` now. I recommend **against** implementing
the donors' aspiration form, the interrupted-iteration guards and a
line-growth bound now: each was measured and none beats or adds to the
one-value change. Optimism is a separate card (B.5.4). If the gate accepts,
`AspGrowthPct`, `AspGrowthHighPct`, `AspGrowthAdd` become dead at the
default (no second failure ever widens) and go to B.8's cleanup with
`AspirationDelta`'s range left as it is.

## The gate rejected the change (2026-09-30)

**RAR-S89: H0 accepted at 1,991 pairs, −18.3 ± 6.9 Elo** (−28.6 ± 10.8
nElo; 979-1815-1188, [97, 569, 836, 424, 65]; 0 faults, both binaries at
their registered hashes, idle host). One-retry aspiration loses at
`3+0.03`. Nothing is baked.

**Calibration.** Predicted +8 ± 8 with H1 at 0.65; read −18.3: **missed in
sign**, outside the interval. The instrument failed, in the same way as in
B.4.3: every zero-game read pointed the other way (nodes to depth 0.55×,
WAC +9 at two budgets, +0.76 ply at 300k nodes, +0.3 ply under a clock), and
none of them is Elo. What the registered competing hypotheses said is what
happened: the re-searches the head performs when a score leaves its window
are useful search on exactly the positions where the best move is in doubt
(H2), and a fully opened side costs more than it saves at the depths this
control reaches (H3). The clock read held a warning I underweighted: the
candidate used 9% fewer nodes per move, so part of what it "saved" was
simply not searched.

**What follows from it.** The head's narrow, slowly widening window is not
a defect at `3+0.03`; the failed floor on depth-14 nodes against the oracle
(B.3.2, B.4.2) measured a tree shape, not strength, and the floor itself is
the wrong screen for this engine. The donors' aspiration form moves in the
direction the games rejected and is not built. Two questions remain and
both are answered only by games: where the current form's own coordinates
sit on the Elo surface (they were last fitted for the 2.3 search), and
whether the sign turns at a slower control, where the savings are several
times larger.

All three played by the maintainer, 2,000 games each, 0 faults, no abnormal
game, binaries at their registered hashes, host idle.

**The aspiration question closes `NO_CHANGE`.** The Elo surface falls
steeply toward wider or sooner-opened windows (−25 at twice the width, −18
with one retry at both time controls) and is flat toward narrower ones
(+2.1 ± 9.2 at 12). The current value sits at or next to the top; no
direction justifies a tune. The loss does not shrink at the slower control,
so it is not a trade against depth: on this search a narrow window around
the previous score is doing real work, most plausibly through the many
null-window and reduced searches that are scaled by the root window
(`root_delta` in the reduction formula), and the re-searches that follow a
failure.

**Calibration.** Delta 12 predicted +2 ± 10, read +2.1: hit. Delta 45
predicted −6 ± 10, read −25.4: sign right, magnitude missed by a factor of
four. One retry at `10+0.1` predicted −8 ± 10, read −18.3: sign right, at
the edge of the interval. Predictions made after one game result are
already far better than the ones made from node counts.

**B.5's cluster verdict: `NO_CHANGE`.** The root as it stands is kept. What
the research leaves behind: the diag counters for the loop and for
interrupted iterations; the inventory of coordinates never re-fitted (the
time management multipliers for D.1, six single coordinates for B.6, with
the aspiration loop now read and cleared); and two corrections to method,
both written into memory and into this packet: a zero-game read does not
rank playing candidates, and rule 8's depth-14-nodes floor measures tree
shape, not strength.
