# B.3.2 screens — the `b3proof` arm against the fitted head, before any game

**Status: B.3.2's zero-game half, 2026-09-23. The 2,000-game paired run
(section 8) is the maintainer's and has not been played.** The arm is B.3.1's
closed `b3proof` build: the T1–T3 contract with amendments 2–6 (per-line
extension budget, Rarog's multi-cut rule, `depth >= 4` candidates, the
lone-move `alpha` return), at `9bf6717`'s engine source. The gate binaries
were built at `1178ba0`, which changes no engine source. Registration: RAR-S79,
the packet `b3_research_2026-09-23.md` (*Registered screens*, amendment 6), and
the rules fixed before any measurement in
`tools/results/b32-20260923/preregistration.md`. Evidence:
`tools/results/b32-20260923/` (ignored).

## 1. Verdict

- **The paired run governs, and the screens hand it over** (PLAN rule 8). A
  floor failure never holds a candidate the paired run clears.
- **Three floors fail:**
  - nodes at depth 14 against the oracle, **3.11×** (floor ≤ 2.2×);
  - canaries, **four of the head's 87 lost** (89 pass, six fixed);
  - time-to-depth, **1.134×** (floor ≤ 1.10×), with pooled NPS −8.20%.
- **Every quality screen is at or above its target:**
  - WAC 100k **228** (target 226);
  - WAC 400k **268** (target 263);
  - oracle agreement **43/50** (target 43);
  - branching 1.789, inside its window.
- **The deep-iteration cost screen passes** on all five positions, at
  0.31×–2.26×.
- The arm finds more at a fixed node budget and spends more nodes and time
  per depth. The games decide which one wins at `3+0.03`.

**Two instrument findings, both resolved before any arm number was read:**

1. **The probe's depth read-out had drifted.** Since the UCI-info series
   (`8a76826`), single-PV searches print aspiration bound lines with a PV. In
   `nodes` mode `fixed_budget_probe.py` read the last such line as the depth
   reached. Today's head therefore read one ply deeper than the baseline on 21
   of 50 positions (same best moves).
   - Fixed in `288ccea` (`last_completed()`).
   - With the fix, the baseline's own binary (`rarog-b24b-core-pext-pgo`,
     `2A586F3B…`) and today's head both reproduce the recorded records
     exactly, 0 of 650 differing.
   - Only the depth and node read-outs were affected: solved counts,
     agreement and canaries were not.
2. **The committed `summarise_head.py` is stale.** It reads the depthpv-12
   anchor and cannot produce the recorded `canaries_d10` (87) or the
   depth-14 ratio (1.90×). Its d10 variant reproduces both exactly and is
   the rule used here (`summarise_ladder.py`).

## 5. Time-to-depth and pooled NPS

- **Pools:** `nps_build_pool.ps1 -Arch pext -Builds 4`, one per arm, each copy
  bench-verified at its fingerprint.
- **Measurement:** `nps_multibuild.ps1`, 10 cycles × 3 repeats, interleaved
  (PROCESS, *Toolchain and harness notes*).
- **Estimator validation:** the head's self pair (builds 1–2 against 3–4)
  reads +0.22%, 95% CI −0.09% to +0.45%.

| | Head pool | Arm pool |
|---|---:|---:|
| Pooled median NPS | 2,143,698 | 1,967,928 |
| Delta (95% bootstrap CI) | — | **−8.20%** (−8.55% .. −7.92%) |
| `bench 13` nodes | 7,185,678 | 7,479,114 (+4.08%) |
| **Time-to-depth** (nodes / pooled NPS, head = 1) | 1.00 | **1.134×** (≈ 1.130–1.138) |

The NPS loss is plausibly compositional rather than a per-node producer. The
arm's `bench 13` is 64.7% interior nodes against the head's 53.8% (the final
counter record). Interior nodes cost more than quiescence nodes, and the
arm's extra proof searches (verification, ProbCut, the exclusion search) are
interior. Not profiled here; B.7 owns speed.

`kbnk_positions_are_driven_to_mate`: 40 of 40 playouts identical move for

  finds more WAC positions and more canaries, but agrees with the oracle
  less.

## 11. Recommendation and what the review must decide

1. **Play the paired run first** (section 8). It governs. At ≥ +5 Elo the
   cluster proceeds to B.3.3; at ≤ −40 it is a defect and the bit sweep names
   it.
2. **Between −40 and +5, three floor failures carry the review:**
   - time-to-depth (1.134×), which is NPS −8.2% plus depth cost;
   - depth-14 nodes (3.11×);
   - four canaries lost.

   The bit sweep says the null move and ProbCut each cost WAC and canaries
   at fixed nodes while adding agreement. B.3.3's sweep and SPSA are the
   registered lever for the bars (`CoreSingDoubleBase` and
   `CoreSingTripleBase` among them).
3. **Order of the categorical runs:** `CoreSingularFloor=1` first. It is the
   only alternative that cuts nodes (−11.5%) and it gains the most WAC (+10),
   against the two floors that fail on cost. The other three follow only if
   the review wants them. Each is 2,000 games at `3+0.03` (B.2.2's took 22
   minutes at concurrency 14), so all four are about 8,000 games, roughly
   1.5 hours of maintainer compute.
4. **Tooling:** `fixed_budget_probe.py` is fixed (`288ccea`).
   `summarise_head.py` in the B.3 evidence folder should be replaced by its
   d10 form if that folder is ever re-summarised.
