# B.5.1 — the two cards from the Gyatso read

**Status: research record for PLAN B.5.1, 2026-09-30.** Card (a) closes
`NO_CHANGE` on a false premise; card (b) is registered as RAR-S91, one
2,000-game categorical read. Measured on the accepted head, `dev` at
`afead81`, default build **11,171,726 / EBF 2.512**. The engine commit
`edc69ad` adds the switch `CoreDrawJitter` (default 0) and two diag
counters; with the switch off the release build reproduces the fingerprint,

(stride-1 counters with the switch off and on, the corpus script and its

## Card (a): TT-cutoff history bonus — `NO_CHANGE`, the premise is false

The card said Rarog's TT-cutoff path writes no history. That is true only of
the legacy search (`src/search/node.rs`, which `--no-default-features` still
compiles). The default search has carried the bonus since B.2 (`295ced2`,
2026-09-14; the default build since 2026-09-20): at a non-PV TT cutoff with
a score at or above beta, a legal quiet TT move reached through one of the
parent's first four moves gets the quiet bonus
`min(190·depth − 81, CoreHistTtCutoffBonusCap)` and the continuation bonus
`min(96·depth − 73, 1206)` (`src/search/core/node.rs`, the TT-cutoff block).

- **Provenance.** This is Reckless's form (read at `31d9cd6`: the same
  condition and constants, cap 1,691). B.0 adopted it
  (`analysis/search_programme_2026-09-13.md`, *Early cutoff*), B.2.4a and
  B.2.4b's gates carried it, and B.2.3's and B.2.7's tunes fitted the cap:
  1,727 now, range 256–4,096, not at a rail.
- **Activation on the head** (`bench 13`, stride 1): 84,593 bonuses over
  7,468,694 interior nodes (1.1%), 27% of the 307,919 lower-bound TT
  cutoffs.
- **What remains is form.** Gyatso gives half the depth bonus at every
  quiet lower-bound cutoff. Stockfish (read at `59aae690`) gives an uncapped
  `131·depth` at every quiet TT-move cutoff with no condition on the parent,
  and instead penalises the parent's early quiet move (`moveCount < 5`,
  −2,210 on its continuation entry). No measurement here locates a defect in
  this producer. A different form of a producer that has passed a gate and
  been fitted twice is a donor difference, not evidence.

**Verdict: `NO_CHANGE`.** Retry when an ordering measurement locates a
defect in the quiet history's producers, or when B.6 finds
`CoreHistTtCutoffBonusCap` at a rail. The Gyatso note carries the correction.

## Card (b): draw-score randomisation

### The premise, verified

`negamax` (below the root) and `quiescence` in the core search return 0 when
`Board::can_declare_draw_in_search` holds: a rule-50 draw (mate excluded),
insufficient material, or any repetition within the scan bound (Rarog's
aggressive twofold, pre-root occurrences included). Stalemate and tablebase
draws return 0 through their own paths.

- **Reckless** (`31d9cd6`): `draw() = nodes % 5 − 2` at every draw in the
  main search and the quiescence, at the upcoming-repetition alpha raise, at
  `MAX_PLY` in check and at stalemate.
- **Stockfish** (`59aae690`): `VALUE_DRAW − 1 + (nodes & 2)`, so ±1, at
  `is_draw` in the main search and at the upcoming-repetition raise. The
  quiescence returns a fixed 0. Its comment gives the purpose: to avoid
  3-fold blindness.

### Evidence in this engine

- **Population in search** (`bench 13`, stride 1, new diag counters
  `draw_return` and `q_draw_return`): 25,862 draws below the root in the
  main search and 1,945 in the quiescence, 0.28% of visited nodes. The bench
  is mostly middlegames, so games reach more.
- **Population in games** (RAR-M63's gauntlet, 40,000 games of
  `2.5.0-dev+b3proof` against the pool, whose draw code is the head's;
  `draw_corpus.py`, one score per ply checked against the stored ply count
  in every game):
    - 19.5% of the games end by repetition (16.9%) or rule 50 (2.6%);
    - in 32.4% Rarog's root printed exactly 0 on three consecutive moves;
      those games then went 1,554 won, 9,842 drawn, 1,558 lost. A flat draw
      at the root costs nothing one-sided, so no blindness defect is
      measured;
    - repetition or rule-50 endings with at least +100 printed in the side's
      last ten moves: Rarog 1,219 of 7,815, its opponents 861. Engines do not
      share a score scale, so this comparison is recorded and not used.
- **The bench cannot screen it.** A deterministic bench samples one
  realisation: switch on, it reads 11,176,453 (+0.04%), which shows only
  that the wire is live.
- **The ledger gives no prior.** RAR-S67's stop note re-read RAR-S54, S62
  and S64: two of the three were directional de-selectivity, not noise, and
  symmetric reduction jitter was stopped at −5.35 ± 7.28 in 3,764 games. That
  refutes "noise helps" as a basis here. It does not transfer the other way
  either: this change moves only the value of a drawn leaf, by at most two
  units, not the shape of the tree. The prior rests on the donors alone.
  Stockfish accepted the change, and Reckless keeps it at twice the
  amplitude.

### Hypotheses

1. **Tie-breaking at zero windows (the donors').** With a fixed 0, every
   drawn line ties. In a zero window at 0 all of them fail the same way, so
   ordering, not search, picks among the moves that keep or avoid a
   repetition. With two units of variation, some drawn lines fail high and
   are searched again, so the search keeps probing alternatives near a
   repetition. Predicts a small gain concentrated in games that reach a
   repetition or a flat root.
2. **No effect beyond diversity.** The donors' self-play gain came from
   varied games and does not survive against a fixed opponent. Predicts 0.
3. **Harm through the TT.** Parent entries built from drawn leaves carry up
   to two units of path-dependent offset into exact entries and bounds at
   0-centred windows. Predicts a small loss.

Rarog's aggressive twofold makes the population larger than Stockfish's,
which counts a repetition inside the search only after the root.

### Interaction map

- **TT:** drawn leaves are not stored; their parents store the offset value.
  The rule-50 horizon guard on TT cutoffs is unchanged.
- **Correction:** a drawn node returns before training. A parent whose best
  line is a draw trains on a score two units from 0, which is small next to
  the update clamps.
- **History:** which moves fail high at a 0-centred window, and so which get
  the bonus, changes. That is hypothesis 1's mechanism itself.
- **Pruning margins, mate and tablebase bands:** two units are below every
  margin, and the bands are untouched.
- **Aspiration and time management:** the window (`AspirationDelta` 21) and the
  score-drop terms see at most a two-unit wobble in drawn positions.
- **MultiPV:** drawn lines' values vary within ±2. The contract's structure
  (identity at `MultiPV = 1`) is unaffected.
- **Determinism:** the offset derives from the thread's node count, so a
  single-threaded bench reproduces. Helpers disagree by design.

### Cheapest discriminating test and rule (RAR-S91)

The effect can only be read by games. The test is RAR-S80's categorical
read: one tune build on both sides, `CoreDrawJitter=1` as side A by option,
2,000 games under Colosseum `match-fixed` (`3+0.03`), about 21 minutes.

- **At least +5 Elo with the 95% lower bound above −5:** the card becomes
  `READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION`, and a `[0,3]` SPRT of the switch baked on
  (PGO) against the head is registered next. That gate decides.
- **Between −5 and +5:** `NO_CHANGE` at this budget. This means the evidence
  is insufficient, not that the idea is refuted. The switch stays at 0 until
  B.8 removes it.
- **At or below −5:** `NO_CHANGE`, closed off.

Effects are never summed with B.5.4's read.

### Prospective prediction (frozen 2026-09-30, before any game)

**+1 ± 10 Elo.** Adopted (≥ +5, lower bound above −5) with probability
0.2; at or below −5 with probability 0.1. The read cannot resolve a true
effect of the donors' likely size (a few Elo); what it can catch cheaply is
a large gain or a clear loss.

### Falsifiers and stop

- A read at or below −5 falsifies hypothesis 1 at this control. The
  amplitude is not tuned to rescue it.
- A read between −5 and +5 stops the investment. The retry trigger is a
  measured draw defect: for example, the outcome split after a flat-draw
  root turning one-sided, or conversion losses traced to repetitions.

## RAR-S91 read: undecidable, the switch stays off (2026-10-01)

`CoreDrawJitter=1` against the defaults on the registered tune binary
(`529E9525…`, both sides), 2,000 games at `3+0.03`, seed 20261014,
0 faults, idle host (`tools/results/b51-cat-drawjitter`): **−1.2 ± 9.4 Elo**
(−2.0 ± 15.2 nElo; 520-953-527, pentanomial [37, 237, 455, 238, 33]).

By the registered rule this is between −5 and +5, so **card (b) closes
`NO_CHANGE`** at this budget: the evidence is insufficient, not a
refutation. The switch stays 0 and B.8 removes it with the other
unconsumed switches. The two diag counters stay while this record names
them. Retry only on a measured draw defect (see *Falsifiers and stop*).

**Calibration:** predicted +1 ± 10 Elo, adoption 0.2; read −1.2, inside the
interval, on the branch the prediction expected. No assumption failed. The
read could not resolve an effect of the donors' size, and it was
registered knowing that.

**B.5.1 is closed: `NO_CHANGE` on both cards.**
