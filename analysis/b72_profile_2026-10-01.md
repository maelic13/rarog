## Located mechanisms

**Selection scans after quiets are skipped.** `pick_next` is 13.35% of samples
(innermost 10.12%, plus its swap copy 1.08%), almost all from
`MovePicker::next` in non-PV `negamax`. A counter build over `bench`
(`scripts/picker_counters.patch`; bench 11,171,726 unchanged) split the
scanned elements by stage:

| Stage | Elements scanned | Picks |
|---|---:|---:|
| full picker (root, in check) | 446,116 | 24,931 |
| good noisy | 9,451,931 | 4,197,856 |
| quiets, not skipping | 165,788,160 | 7,994,499 |
| skipping: survivor emitted | 15,788,810 | 729,958 |
| skipping: discarded, a survivor still left | 40,301,237 | 1,854,261 |
| **skipping: discarded, no survivor left** | **346,852,999** | **26,271,645** |
| total | 578,629,253 | 41,073,150 |

Once late-move or quiet-futility pruning sets `skip_quiets`, the picker keeps
selecting every remaining quiet, only to discard each one that is not a
direct check or a promotion. When none of those is left, **60% of all scanned
elements and 64% of all picks** produce nothing.

Also counted: 50,100,226 quiets scored, 8,724,457 emitted. `skip_quiets` is
never set when the quiets are generated (0 of all generations), so the scoring
has no exact lazy path.

**Per-node copies of the scored move list.** `vcruntime140.dll` takes 5.08%
of samples, about 2,500–2,700 per cohort. That is near-constant because every
cohort searches the same node count. From xperf's caller table, pooled:
`MovePicker::staged` 2.18%, `score_tactical_moves` 0.61%, `score_moves` 0.13%.
The rest, `clear_history` 0.40% and the TT clear 0.35%, is the driver's
`ucinewgame` per root. The disassembly has six `memcpy` calls of exactly
**4,104 bytes**, the size of `ScoredMoveList` (256 × 16-byte entries plus the
length). They sit in `score_moves`, `score_tactical_moves`,
`MovePicker::staged` and three `negamax` monomorphs: the list is built and
returned by value. AGENTS' per-node rule forbids this.

## Candidates (rule: located mechanism, estimate, ceiling ≥ +0.5%)

1. **Quiet-stage early exit.** Count the survivors (direct checks,
   non-capturing promotions) when the quiets are appended. Decrement on every
   survivor picked. Under `skip_quiets` with none left, go straight to the
   bad noisy moves.
   - **Exact by construction:** the abandoned loop only reorders the quiet
     partition, which nothing reads afterwards. It ends in the same `next()`
     call with the same stage and the same returned move.
   - **Removes** about 60% of `pick_next`'s work, about 8.0% of samples.
     Ceiling about +8.7% NPS.
   - **Prediction:** +4% to +9% pooled-PGO NPS, fingerprint exact.
2. **Build the scored list in place.** Fill the list through `&mut` in the
   picker's own storage instead of returning it by value; the six 4,104-byte
   copies go.
   - **Exact:** the same contents.
   - **Removes** up to 2.92% of samples. Ceiling about +3.0%.
   - **Prediction:** +1% to +3%, fingerprint exact.
   - The `_alloca_probe` 0.35% (stack probing for frames over 4 KB) may
     shrink with it.
3. **Conditional: the selection scan's layout.** Scores apart from the
   16-byte entries, so the scan is contiguous and vectorisable, with the same
   first-maximum rule.
   - After candidate 1 the residual is about 5.3% of samples. At `s` = 1.25
     the ceiling is about 1.1%, but the local speedup is unmeasured.
   - A re-profile after 1 and 2 decides whether it goes forward.

Not candidates:
- **Quiet scoring** (6.5%): no exact lazy path, see above.
- **The TT probe** (6.2%): it is already prefetched after make, and no further
  mechanism is located.
- **Evaluation** (21.2%): Phase C re-implements the evaluation families, so
  speed work there would be discarded.
- **Evaluation correction** (3.9%): spread across lookups, with no single
  mechanism at 2.5%.
- **The root node's `Vec` allocations** listed in PLAN: invisible. No
  allocator frames appear among the samples, so the item stays below the
  floor and is dropped.

## Decision

B.7.2 is `READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION` for candidates 1 and 2:
- **One engine commit each**, each with a targeted test that the picker's
  emitted sequence is unchanged.
- **Measured separately** by pooled-PGO NPS (`nps_build_pool.ps1`, then
  interleaved `nps_multibuild.ps1`) against the +0.5% floor.
- **Each also passes:** an exact bench, debug and release tests, fmt, clippy,
  and the allocation guard.

Candidate 3 waits for the re-profile. NPS is the evidence layer; no Elo is
claimed.

## Audit, 2026-10-02 (RAR-P30)

Asked for by the maintainer after candidate 3's falsifier. Everything is in
`tools/results/b72-audit-20261002/` (git-ignored); the four prototype
recipes are tracked as `analysis/b72_audit_*.patch`.

**The two accepted changes.** Read against their contract: the quiet stage's
exit happens in the same `next()` call in which the old loop ran dry, so a
later change of `skip_quiets` cannot tell them apart; the in-place list
leaves one by-value copy, the root's, once per iteration. Nothing to
correct.

**Candidate 3.** On the recorded tails and the engine's own 16-byte entries
(`scanbench2`, `scan_run1.log`; host in use, so a screen): both updates as
unpredictable selects `s` = 1.03; one packed 64-bit key per element 0.95,
with two accumulators 0.91; the picked entry read before the swap 1.07.
Every variant picks the engine's entry and leaves its order, also over full
selection sorts. The audit expected the scan to be bound by branch
misprediction and about twice as fast without the branch; it is not. No
layout or scan form reaches the 1.1 bound, so `NO_CHANGE` stands.

**The re-profile by source line** (`line_drill.py`, `callers.py`; 231,374
samples, 97.84% resolved; the summarizer's parser and classifier, the inline
chain with file and line from `llvm-symbolizer`):

| Where | Share | What the samples sit on |
|---|---:|---|
| `tt::probe_local`, first load | 6.89% | the cluster's cache line; the prefetch is issued after the move is made |
| `quiet_score`, lines 563 and 568 | 6.07% | loads of the pawn and continuation histories (0.8 MB and 4.7 MB) |
| `correction_value`, its loads | 2.45% | six slots in 26 MB of tables |
| `LazyLock` state check on `ATTACKS` | 0.71% | every attack lookup; SEE, check queries, generation, ordering, evaluation |
| `Vec` pointer of the slider tables | 0.55% | the same lookups |
| evaluation cache probe | 0.85% | one slot of the cache |
| `_alloca_probe` | 0.29% | `negamax`'s frame is over 4 KB |

**Candidates** (each a hint or a table layout, so exact by construction;
each prototype reproduces `bench` 11,171,726):

- **A. Prefetch the child's TT cluster before the move is made,** from
  `key_after_hint` (exact for a plain move or capture that changes no
  castling right), in the main move loop and in quiescence; the exact
  prefetch after the move stays. Lead time gained: the check predicate and
  the make.
- **B. The attack tables as one static with fixed arrays,** read with no
  state check and no pointer. The prototype fills it at startup through an
  `UnsafeCell`; the shippable form builds the static at compile time.
- **D. Prefetch the history rows quiet scoring reads,** when the picker
  enters `GenerateQuiets`: the side to move's half of the pawn row and of
  the four continuation rows.
- **I. Prefetch the child's six correction slots** once the move is made and
  pushed.

**Screen, predictions and rule:** PLAN B.7.2.13, registered before any
pooled read. A plain-build screen on a host in use read ±3% and says
nothing.

**Not candidates:** the SEE's per-recapture attacker and king-safety
queries (an exact rewrite is not small); the evaluation cache probe (Phase
C's restructure); the selections above the next survivor under a skip (no
exact shortcut with the swap's tie order).
