# C.10 — the joint search tune on the classical evaluation: research card

**Status: C.10.1 done 2026-10-19 (zero games). C.10 proceeds: the skip rule
does not fire. C.10.2's switch reads are registered (RAR-S100) and handed
over.** Leaf: PLAN C.10 (class `V`). The scripts and raw outputs are in
`analysis/artifacts/c10/`.

## Decision needed

Whether C.10's joint search SPSA runs, over which surface, with which fixed
values, and in what order of games. PLAN C.10's skip rule: no tune when no
mechanism's firing rate per interior node moved 10% or more against the
B.9 head and the cp scale ratio against the B era is within 5%.

### What the C phase already refitted

The margin block ran twice: `c2margin` (RAR-E24, +19.3 ± 9.3 Elo over the

**Neither skip condition holds, so C.10 runs.**

## Unknowns

- How far the optimum moved. A margin block bought +19.3 Elo after one
  unit; B.6 judged a restart on an unchanged search at +3 ± 3. Six units
  and a 5 to 17% scale change sit between those.
- Whether the aspiration shape, never fitted (its growth coordinates were
  staged inert), carries a gradient now that fail-lows rose 32%.

## Hypotheses

- **H-misfit:** the cp margins and the moved mechanisms' coordinates sit
  where the B-era evaluation put them, so a joint tune recovers Elo.
- **H-absorbed:** the two margin blocks and the units' own gates already
  absorbed the change, and the tune finds noise.
- **H-structural:** the moved rates come from the evaluation's new shape,
  not its scale, so no coordinate setting recovers them. A tune then
  stops on its movement rule.

## Interaction map

- The correction residual halved: the history-based correction now
  carries less signal per update, and its consumers (`CoreRfpCorrection`,
  `CoreFpCorrection`, `CoreLmrCorrection`, the singular `*Corr` terms) are
  on the surface with it.
- Quiescence futility and delta prunes fell by 40 to 50% while SEE flips
  rose 59%. These are the same captures seen by different gates, so the
  `Qs*` group is tuned together, with `QsCountLimit` fixed by its read.
- `LazyMargin` sets what the search sees (RAR-E58: the lazy score lacks
  the Material row above 500 cp) and sits on the surface.
- Evaluation and search never share a tune (rule 2): the evaluation is
  frozen at the C head.

## The surface (`surface.py`, `surface.tsv`)

Of the 142 tunables, **83 are coordinates**, one is read as a switch and
58 stay out. A coordinate is in when it is in evaluation units or
multiplies an evaluation-unit input, or when its mechanism's rate moved
10% or more:

- aspiration: `AspirationDelta` and the three growth terms;
- `LazyMargin`;
- quiescence: the seven `Qs*` except `QsCountLimit`;
- `ProbCutSeeGapScale` (carried from B.6);
- correction: the update slope and clamps, the six blend weights;
- `CoreEvalMaterialScale`;
- razoring (2) and reverse futility's six cp terms;
- `CoreHindsightReduceMargin` and `CoreLmpImprovement`;
- quiet futility (5) and bad-noisy futility (3);
- the two SEE constants on PROCESS's margin surface;
- null move: ten terms;
- ProbCut: six terms;
- singular, multi-cut and low-depth singular: seventeen terms;
- LMR's seven score- or correction-valued terms.

**Read as a switch:** `QsCountLimit` (3 on `2..=8`). B.6 carried it as
better read as a switch, and its rate moved 39%.

**Out:**
- the time-management group (D.1's);
- `CoreEvalRule50Damping` and `CoreNmpVerifyDepth`, rarely reached at
  `3+0.03`;
- `CoreIirMinDepth` (RAR-S96 read it) and `CoreSingularTtDepthMargin`
  (RAR-S80 read it). Both are depth gates whose population does not move
  with the evaluation's scale.
- `NmpMinNonPawnPieces` (a zugzwang guard, by material);
- `AspMaxFails` (seeded so it never fires first);
- every other coordinate: not in evaluation units, and its mechanism moved
  under 10% (LMR's structural terms, histories, LMP's count terms, SEE's
  depth terms, `CoreRfpLerp`, `CoreHindsightDeepenReduction`).

## The blended target (RAR-E68): recommend against including it

C.13.6's blended refit lost −13.0 ± 5.7 Elo under the head's margins
(RAR-E67), and scaled to the head's size it read −6.3 ± 6.6 (RAR-E68,
inconclusive). C.10's registration was to decide whether the blend joins
the tune. **I recommend against including it.**

- It is an evaluation change. C.10 tunes search coordinates on a frozen
  evaluation (rule 2). A blended C.10 would have to tune two arms, one for
  each evaluation, which doubles 12.5 to 37 hours of blocks for a candidate
  whose best reading is −6 Elo.
- The case for the blend is that its margins were never refitted. That is
  a margin block on the blended refit (one block, about 12.5 hours) and a
  gate. It is a unit of its own, not part of the joint tune.
- Retry trigger: the maintainer asks for it as a unit after C.10. A
  changed datagen budget or label source would also fire it (C.13.6's
  trigger).

## Cheapest discriminating tests, in order

1. **C.10.2 (RAR-S100):** `QsCountLimit` 2 and 4 against 3, 2,000 games
   each, on one tune build, about 50 minutes. Its rule fixes the value the
   pilot and the blocks use.
2. **C.10.3:** the widened ranges, then PROCESS's sensitivity pilot. It is
   128 iterations × 30 games (3,840 games, about 50 minutes) over the 83
   coordinates, seeded at the head. Pilot theta is never promoted. It
   reads which coordinates have a gradient, and is followed by the re-audit
   PROCESS step 6 asks for.
3. **C.10.4:** rule-7c blocks (2,000 iterations × 30 games, about 12.5
   hours each, at most three, the movement rule), from the head's values.
4. **C.10.5:** theta baked into a clean PGO build, SPRT `[0,3]` at
   `3+0.03` against the C head, then a `10+0.1` read.

## Prospective prediction (frozen here)

- C.10.2: neither `QsCountLimit` read finds a direction (0.75).
- The pilot moves at least five coordinates by a full step (0.6), led by
  the `Qs*` group and the correction blend.
- The joint tune's gate: **+8 Elo** (80% band +2 to +18), H1 under
  `[0,3]` with probability 0.65. The band comes from RAR-E24's +19.3 for
  one unit's margins and B.6's +3 for an unchanged search; it is a
  judgement, not a measurement.

## Falsifiers and stop conditions

- The pilot moves no coordinate by a full step and its journal reads inside
  the sign-redraw null (RAR-S95's statistic). C.10 then stops before the
  blocks and asks the maintainer whether 12.5 hours is worth a flat
  surface.
- Block 1 moves fewer than three coordinates a full step: the movement
  rule stops the tune (rule 7c).
- The gate's H0: the theta is not baked. C.10 closes `NO_CHANGE`, and the
  C head's margins stay.

## Cost

About 50 minutes of reads, a 50-minute pilot, 12.5 to 37.5 hours of
blocks, a PGO bake, and an SPRT. At a true +8 Elo the SPRT needs roughly
10,000 to 15,000 games, 2 to 3 hours. The total is about 16 to 43 hours of
the maintainer's compute.

## Decision

`READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION` does not apply (no engine mechanism is built).
C.10 proceeds to C.10.2, and the blend stays out unless the maintainer
asks for it as its own unit.
