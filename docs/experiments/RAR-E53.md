- **What RAR-E52 showed:** on the development cohort (seed 7100710, 46 clean wins at 60k) the head converts 9. With the corner drive at least as strong as the pawn term, every variant lost the pawn far more often (insufficient material 4 → 20 to 23) and converted 4 to 8. The drive works (DTZ progress 0.507 → 0.65, ply-limit failures 28 → 13 to 16); the pawn was traded for king progress.
- **Hypothesis:** the plan is ordered: hold the pawn (blockade it low), then drive the king. A pawn term that dominates the drive keeps the first part and lets the drive do the second, so conversion rises above the head's.
- **Competing explanations:** (1) *still a plan:* releasing the blockade at the right moment, to avoid stalemate and give the pawn the move that allows mate, cannot be expressed by a monotone pawn penalty; a dominant penalty would trade insufficient-material losses for stalemates and ply-limit draws. (2) *the drive is not needed:* the gain, if any, comes from the pawn term alone; the smaller `CORNER` point tests that.
