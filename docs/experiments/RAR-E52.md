# RAR-E52 — KNN-KP: a corner drive with the pawn held back, by maintainer decision against RAR-E51's expected-value clause

Indexed under *5. Evaluation and data experiments* in [`docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`](../EXPERIMENTS.md).

- **Date / owner:** registered 2026-10-12, before any number was computed; agent-run (zero games). **Maintainer decision 2026-10-12:** build KNN-KP knowledge although RAR-E51's rule did not send the family to a recogniser (its expected value is at most 0.03 Elo in the gate's games); the defect is real and the engine should play the ending correctly without tablebases.
- **Baseline SHA / candidate SHA / dirty-diff hash:** baseline the current head, engine `38cbb02` (**14,197,472 / EBF 2.557**), `analysis/artifacts/c523/rarog-head.exe`. Candidate: this registration's commit plus one engine commit adding the KNN-KP evaluation; its recipe (the constants) is recorded in the result. Reference: RAR-E51's Stockfish runs on the same cohort (`analysis/artifacts/c57/ref/`), reused, not rerun.
- **Measured defect (RAR-E51):** at 60k · 200k · 600k nodes the head converts 13 · 15 · 14 of 44 clean KNN-KP wins (200 positions, seed 6200600), Stockfish 24 · 29 · 26. More nodes do not help. Of the head's 29 failures at 200k, 14 end in insufficient material and 14 at the ply limit, and the win is usually discarded long before. A sample (`k7/4p3/4K3/8/2N5/N7/8/8 w`, mate in 12 by DTZ) shows the mechanism: the head plays Kd7 at about +300 cp and lets the pawn run. Nothing in the evaluation rewards keeping the pawn back or the defending king cornered; the generic mop-up's edge push is 5 cp a step.
- **Mechanism (from the donor's contract, written in Rarog's form):** two knights cannot mate a bare king, so the pawn is what makes the win possible: it must stay on the board and stay back (blockaded behind the Troitzky line) while the other pieces drive the defending king into a corner. For exact material K+N+N against K+P, replace the evaluation with `BASE + CORNER·(6 − d_corner) + KINGS·(7 − d_kings) − ADVANCE·(r − 2)`, from the knights' side: `d_corner` the defending king's Manhattan distance to the nearest corner (0 to 6), `d_kings` the kings' Chebyshev distance (1 to 7), `r` the pawn's relative rank (2 to 7, so `r − 2` is its advance, 0 to 5). `BASE = 5·ADVANCE + 20`, so the value stays at 20 or more and capturing the pawn (KNN-K, scored 0) never looks better than keeping it. The tuner's delta scale for the family becomes 0, as for KNN-K, because the weights no longer move the score.
- **Competing explanations:** (1) *a plan, not a gradient:* the win needs the blockade released at the right moment to avoid stalemate, which a static term cannot express; the term would help only the short wins. (2) *magnitude:* as RAR-E09's mop-up sweep found, a correct gradient that is too small does not survive pruning; the grid spans that.
- **Interactions:** the mop-up (its generic branch still runs inside the evaluation but the override replaces the result), the KNN-K zero, the search's tablebase probing (with Syzygy loaded the family is probed and this term is not reached at the root), entering trades from larger material (KNN-KPP, KNNP-KP), the texel delta scale. Shared signals: none outside exact KNN-KP; `bench 13` has no such position.
- **Invariants and tests:** fires only on exact K+N+N against K+P, either colour; colour-mirrored positions score negated; the value is above 0 and below the decisive band for every position (compile-time assertions on the constants' extremes); corner and pawn directions are tested; `linear_delta_scale` returns 0 for the family; Syzygy-verified KNN-KP won and drawn positions join `tests/endgames.epd`'s hard vetoes (static sign for wins, no mate claim for draws). `bench 13` reproduces 14,197,472 exactly. Debug and release tests, `cargo fmt --check`, `cargo clippy --all-features --all-targets` at zero warnings.

- **Disposition:** refuted in this form. The corner drive does what it says (DTZ progress 0.507 → 0.65 with the larger drive, the ply-limit failures 28 → 13 to 16), but in every variant the games that used to run out of plies now end in **insufficient material**, 4 → 20 to 23: the knights' side drives the king and loses the pawn, and with it the win. In this grid the pawn term never dominated the drive (`ADVANCE` at most equal to `CORNER`), so the search traded the blockade for king progress. The registered stop rule read the failure as "a plan, not a gradient"; the data narrow that: **the weighting is wrong in the direction the grid did not explore** (the pawn must be held before the king is driven), and whether a pawn-dominant weighting converts is untested. KNN-KP stays as RAR-E51 left it. Not Elo.
- **PREDICTION CALIBRATION (appended after exposure):**
  - Original prediction (not rewritten): items 1 to 4 above.
  - Observed: (1) **miss** (best −1, not +5). (2) to (3) not read (stop rule). (4) bench unchanged in all four builds, hit; the vetoes were not run.
  - Which assumption failed: I took the corner drive as the missing piece and the pawn term as a guard against the worst case; the engine already held the pawn better than it drove the king, and a stronger drive made it give the pawn up. The miss is in mechanism (which part of the plan was missing) and in the grid's span (no pawn-dominant point).
- **Conditional lesson:** in a two-part plan, a term for the second part alone can undo the first; a grid has to span the ratio between the parts, not only the size of one.
- **Retry trigger or `closed`:** closed in this form. Retry with a new registration whose grid makes the pawn term dominate (blockade first, then the drive), or with a Troitzky-line blockade term, and the same development cohort, stop rule and final read.
