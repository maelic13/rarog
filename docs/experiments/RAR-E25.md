- **Research question:** does king safety in the donor's shape (C.0.4's card, `analysis/c04_king_unit_2026-10-06.md`: the accumulated-attacker danger index through a capped quadratic map, shelter and storm by file and rank with the castling destination, the pawnless flank outside the index; the 40-bucket table, its inputs and the linear shelter and storm terms removed), fitted on the programme's corpus, make this engine stronger at `3+0.03` with the search frozen?
- **Hypothesis / proposed mechanism:** the danger map carries 92% of the only large family residual on Rarog's own positions (RAR-E17, RAR-E21), and its most informative components (safe checks, the weak ring) are the ones Rarog's bucket scale could not express; a fitted implementation absorbs that residual and plays better king attacks and defence. The search, fitted to the current evaluation's shape, pays per node for the scores it moves (RAR-E19/E20), which the margin block can recover.
- **Competing hypotheses:** (1) the static residual is real but the search already absorbs it in play, so the unit is near-neutral in games; (2) the unit's magnitude in the 101–600 bands (the seeds put the donor's king term at about twice Rarog's) moves the margins' meaning enough that gate 1 fails and only the block recovers it; (3) the per-node cost (C.3.2's unfitted read −1.67%) eats the gain at this time control.
- **Interacting mechanisms / consumers:** every cp-valued margin (the 28-coordinate surface), correction history, `LazyMargin` (the unit runs only below it), mobility (the index reads the mobility lead), the pawn cache (now keyed by king square and castling rights), the eval cache, the KRP-KR endgame scaling (C.3.1's known defect at the seeds: a won KRP-KR 260 → 116 cp).
