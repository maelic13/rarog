## Conditional lesson and retry trigger

**The programme's tree-shape premise is corrected before a line of it is written.** Rarog is not under-selective per ply — it branches slower than both references — and a branching *ceiling* would reward the over-selectivity RAR-S53/S54 measured; the excess is a shallow-depth constant factor, and the deficit is what each node buys, seen cleanly on WAC at equal nodes (42 fewer solved at 100k) against level results at equal nominal depth. **Two contract shapes are named that no constant candidate could reach:** nearly half of all reductions reduce into quiescence (both donors floor at one ply) and reductions are almost never verified (1.3% against ~4%). **The scale ratio cannot seed margins alone:** Reckless's razoring/RFP/futility margins encode NNUE accuracy; converted by 0.457 they would fire on HCE noise (mean residual 128 cp on a 389 cp mean eval), while the classical oracle plays +248 Elo with its own margins on this evaluation — hence the three-column seed rule in the analysis. Nothing here is Elo; every threshold derived from it is a screen (PLAN rule 8). **Retry trigger:** re-run `branching_profile.ps1` and `fixed_budget_probe.py` on the B.1 binary before B.2.1 (must reproduce these numbers except NPS), on every B.2.2 arm, and at B.9.
