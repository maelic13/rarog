# RAR-E29 — C.5.2 gate: the opposite-bishop rule at seven men or more, an opposite-bishop endgame-start cohort SPRT `[0,10]` and a 2,000-game STC harm read

Indexed under *5. Evaluation and data experiments* in [`docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`](../EXPERIMENTS.md).

- **Date / owner:** registered 2026-10-10, before any game; the games are the maintainer's. Maintainer decisions 2026-10-10: C.5.2 takes its own gate. Its form is an opposite-bishop endgame-start cohort SPRT at `[0,10]` with a cap of 8,000 pairs, plus a 2,000-game STC harm read.
- **Baseline SHA / candidate SHA / dirty-diff hash:** baseline = branch `c4` at `b1b0db2`, engine source equal to `master`'s `cc13320` (13,187,295 / EBF 2.546). Candidate = branch `c4` at `543fb59`, whose engine change is `78deb3d` (RAR-E28's rule: in a pure opposite-bishop ending with three pawns or more, `s = min(1 + 2·pawns + 10·passers, 48)` on `/48`; otherwise the old `32 + 4·pawns + 4·passers`), bench **13,562,404 / EBF 2.546**. No `src/search/` change; nothing else in the engine differs.
- **Binary / compiler / PGO identity:** `build_test.ps1` PGO builds (`cargo xtask build --arch pext --pgo`, `rustc 1.98.1`), clean trees, bench-verified sidecars. `tools/test_engines/rarog-c52head-pext-pgo.exe` SHA-256 `F13DF889…E7A0` (from a detached worktree at `b1b0db2`, bench 13,187,295). `tools/test_engines/rarog-c52ocb-pext-pgo.exe` `7B8B70C0…556B` (from `c4` at `543fb59`, bench 13,562,404).
- **Research question:** does the refitted opposite-bishop rule improve play where it acts, without measurable harm in ordinary games?
- **Hypothesis / proposed mechanism:** the rule makes the evaluation see that pure opposite-bishop endings of seven men or more are mostly drawn, and that passed pawns decide them. So the candidate values creating passers, avoids trading into a pure ending when ahead and seeks one when behind (the near stratum). Inside the class, its scores track the result better (RAR-E28: +15.7% of the held-out loss).
- **Competing hypotheses:** (1) *conversion:* the flattened score costs wins inside the class, as RAR-E27 measured in KBPP-KB at six men, and the cohort result is zero or negative. (2) *the boundary:* the candidate trades down into six-man pure endings, where the old rule overclaims (RAR-E28's registered hazard), and gives back what it gained. (3) *too rare at STC:* the class is a few percent of positions, so the STC read moves by nothing measurable either way.
- **Interacting mechanisms / consumers:** the search's pruning margins and rule-50 damping consume the scaled score. The eval cache and TT store it (a pure function of the position). The boundary incentive acts through move choice. Consumers: C.5.2's close, C.4 (after C.5.2), C.5.2.1 (the continuous rule, after C.4), and C.5.6 and C.5.7 (KBPPKB, KBPKB).
- **The book:** `tools/books/ocb_cohort_v1.epd` (ignored storage), SHA-256 `1C176D3B…4364`, 8,000 positions; manifest `ocb_cohort_v1.manifest.json`. Recipe:
  - The positions come from `tools/diag/ocb_book.py` (`543fb59`), run as `--dump analysis/artifacts/c52-gate/v3train_scale.csv --exclude tools/texel/data/hce-v4-tb/{train,validation,test}.csv --max-abs-cp 300 --per-stratum 4000 --seed 20261010`.
  - The dump is the head evaluation's `--dump-scores --scale` of `tools/texel/data/hce-v3-tb/train.csv` (self-play by the earlier `e08head` engine, games the rule was not fitted on), `E52169D0…E47F`, written by `rarog-texel-head.exe` `5B11A2B6…8EBE`, which reproduces RAR-E26's validation dump byte for byte.
  - Strata: 4,000 *pure* (opposite bishops and three pawns or more, nothing else) and 4,000 *near* (the same plus exactly one matching knight, rook or queen each).
  - Filters: legal, not in check, the head's static score within ±300 cp, unique by position, none present in `hce-v4-tb`. Counts at each filter are in the manifest: 32,720 pure and 16,648 near survive before the draw, and 206 were excluded as `hce-v4-tb` positions.
  - Colosseum reads the first four EPD fields of each line and starts at halfmove 0 (`plies` applies only to PGN books); all 8,000 parse.
- **PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION (freeze before exposure):**
  1. Cohort SPRT, candidate over head: nElo point estimate **+6**, 80% band [−4, +16]. Probability H1 0.35, H0 0.50, unresolved at the cap 0.15.
  2. STC harm read: the point estimate lies within ±10 nElo (probability 0.9). The harm rule does not fire (probability 0.95).
  3. Hazard read (RAR-E28's prediction 3), over the cohort games: the candidate makes more first crossings into a pure ending of six men or fewer than the head (probability 0.6). Those games are drawn more often than its other games (probability 0.6).
  - Expected Elo at STC overall: near zero (the class is rare); not a basis for a bound.
  - Most likely failure mode: competing (1) or (2) inside the cohort, ending at H0.
- **Falsification criteria:** void and rerun if the runner, a binary or the book differs from the registration (`colosseum.ps1` refuses each), if the run's manifest records a busy-host waiver, or if a game ends by forfeit or crash. H1 is refuted by H0 in the cohort SPRT.
- **Cheapest prior falsifier:** RAR-E27's KBPP-KB conversion loss, at six men, where this rule does not act (the split's check: the candidate reproduces the head's six-man truth and drawn reports position by position, 6,000 records).
- **Registered gate and stop rule:**
  - **Cohort SPRT:** Colosseum, pentanomial GSPRT `model=normalized`, `[0,10]` nElo, α = β = 0.05, cap 8,000 pairs (the book has 8,000 positions and does not wrap), `3+0.03`, 1T, Hash 64, concurrency 14, no adjudication, the book in random order at seed 2026101001.
  - **STC harm read:** Colosseum `match`, 2,000 games on the UHO book, the same conditions, seed 2026101002. Harm rule: the read fails if its nElo point estimate is below −12.8. That is a 5% false alarm at a true zero with a standard error of about 7.8 nElo (AGENTS, gating, repair class 2); it catches a true −10 with probability about 0.36 and −20 with about 0.82.
  - Nothing changes after a game is seen.
  - **Use, frozen:** H1 in the cohort and the harm rule not fired: C.5.2 is accepted. Its fingerprint becomes the development fingerprint, and C.4 opens on the branch after it. H0, or the harm rule fired: the engine change is reverted in its own commit, and C.5.2's opposite-bishop part closes on RAR-E26 to RAR-E29, with C.5.2.1 as its retry. Unresolved at the cap: not accepted (an unresolved stop is not "probably fine"), and the maintainer decides between revert and a new registration.
  - The hazard read runs on the cohort's PGN with `tools/diag/ocb_boundary_read.py` (`a9db5e4`) and decides nothing.
- **Full conditions / provenance:** run files `tools/colosseum/sprt-ocb-cohort.toml` (extends `sprt-wide.toml`, sets the book) and `match-fixed.toml`. Both commands passed `colosseum.ps1 -DryRun` on 2026-10-10: the host was idle, the runner pinned (`colosseum-cli 0.2.0`, `5A4ECEDB…`), the bench fingerprints matched the registration, the flavour and compiler matched, and the resolved configuration matched policy in every checked field (dry-run files `tools/results/colosseum_sprt_rar-e29-cohort-dryrun_20261010_105656.*` and `colosseum_match_rar-e29-stc-dryrun_20261010_105706.*`).
- **Result (played 2026-10-10 by the maintainer; both manifests record the registered binaries, runner, books and seeds, an idle host and no waiver):**
  - **Cohort SPRT** (`colosseum_sprt_rar-e29-cohort_20261010_152907.*`, `tools/results/rar-e29-cohort/`): **H1 accepted** at LLR 3.05 after 2,396 pairs (4,792 games, 20 minutes). Elo +3.0 ± 2.3, **nElo +12.7 ± 9.8**, W/D/L 449/3,935/408, pentanomial [0, 111, 2134, 150, 1]. 35 post-terminal pairs are kept as evidence and excluded from the sample.
  - **Faults:** one time loss (game 3068, pair 1534; 0.021%, under the runner's 0.5% ceiling). The head, playing White, flagged with 69 ms on its clock after a 92 ms OS hold in a 2 ms search. Both engines then scored the position for the candidate by 7 to 8 pawns (head −726, candidate +812), and the pair's other game was a threefold draw.
  - **STC harm read** (`colosseum_match_rar-e29-stc_20261010_160739.*`): Elo +4.5 ± 8.3, **nElo +8.3 ± 15.2**, W/D/L 519/988/493, pentanomial [27, 182, 553, 214, 24]; 2,000 games, all terminations normal, no fault. The harm rule (point estimate below −12.8) does not fire.
  - **Hazard read** (`analysis/artifacts/c52-gate/boundary-read.json`, `ocb_boundary_read.py` over all 4,862 cohort games including the post-terminal pairs): 540 games cross into a pure ending of six men or fewer. The candidate makes the first crossing in 280 of them (score 0.468, drawn 77.9%) and the head in 260 (score 0.506, drawn 85.8%). The cohort's overall draw rate is 82.1%.
  - **Wire:** in pure starts where the head's first-move score is 100 cp or more, the candidate's score is a median 0.73 of the head's (quartiles 0.42 and 0.98); in near starts 0.88 (read at 2,431 pairs).
- **Disposition:** **accepted.** Cohort H1, and the harm rule not fired. **Waiver (maintainer decision 2026-10-10):** the registered void clause ("a game ends by forfeit") is waived for the one time loss above. Its own record shows the game was decided on the board, and the clause was stricter than the runner's policy and every other gate. Scored as a draw, the pair would leave the SPRT at about LLR 2.93 and still running; nothing in the game supports that counterfactual. Fingerprint **13,562,404 / EBF 2.546** becomes the development fingerprint on branch `c4`, and C.4 opens after it. The hazard is real but small. The candidate trades across the boundary slightly more often (280 against 260), but those games are not more drawn than its others; C.5.2.1 remains the proper fix.
- **PREDICTION CALIBRATION (appended after exposure):**
  - Original prediction (not rewritten): items 1 to 3 above.
  - Observed: (1) H1 (probability 0.35) at +12.7 nElo, inside the band [−4, +16] and above its centre of +6. (2) hit on both clauses (+8.3, within ±10; no harm). (3) first clause hit, weakly (280 against 260, about one standard error); second clause **miss** (77.9% drawn against the cohort's 82.1%).
  - Which assumption failed: I discounted the in-cohort effect for RAR-E27's conversion loss, which acts at six men, where this rule does not. Sign right, magnitude under-predicted, confidence too low.
- **Conditional lesson:** a registration's void clause must match the runner's fault policy and be read before it is frozen; a clause that cannot survive one OS hiccup in 16,000 games was never a usable rule.
- **Retry trigger or `closed`:** closed, accepted.
