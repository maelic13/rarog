- **Research question:** does the refitted opposite-bishop rule improve play where it acts, without measurable harm in ordinary games?
- **Hypothesis / proposed mechanism:** the rule makes the evaluation see that pure opposite-bishop endings of seven men or more are mostly drawn, and that passed pawns decide them. So the candidate values creating passers, avoids trading into a pure ending when ahead and seeks one when behind (the near stratum). Inside the class, its scores track the result better (RAR-E28: +15.7% of the held-out loss).
- **Competing hypotheses:** (1) *conversion:* the flattened score costs wins inside the class, as RAR-E27 measured in KBPP-KB at six men, and the cohort result is zero or negative. (2) *the boundary:* the candidate trades down into six-man pure endings, where the old rule overclaims (RAR-E28's registered hazard), and gives back what it gained. (3) *too rare at STC:* the class is a few percent of positions, so the STC read moves by nothing measurable either way.
- **Interacting mechanisms / consumers:** the search's pruning margins and rule-50 damping consume the scaled score. The eval cache and TT store it (a pure function of the position). The boundary incentive acts through move choice. Consumers: C.5.2's close, C.4 (after C.5.2), C.5.2.1 (the continuous rule, after C.4), and C.5.6 and C.5.7 (KBPPKB, KBPKB).
- **The book:** `tools/books/ocb_cohort_v1.epd` (ignored storage), SHA-256 `1C176D3B…4364`, 8,000 positions; manifest `ocb_cohort_v1.manifest.json`. Recipe:
  - The positions come from `tools/diag/ocb_book.py` (`543fb59`), run as `--dump analysis/artifacts/c52-gate/v3train_scale.csv --exclude tools/texel/data/hce-v4-tb/{train,validation,test}.csv --max-abs-cp 300 --per-stratum 4000 --seed 20261010`.
  - The dump is the head evaluation's `--dump-scores --scale` of `tools/texel/data/hce-v3-tb/train.csv` (self-play by the earlier `e08head` engine, games the rule was not fitted on), `E52169D0…E47F`, written by `rarog-texel-head.exe` `5B11A2B6…8EBE`, which reproduces RAR-E26's validation dump byte for byte.
  - Strata: 4,000 *pure* (opposite bishops and three pawns or more, nothing else) and 4,000 *near* (the same plus exactly one matching knight, rook or queen each).
  - Filters: legal, not in check, the head's static score within ±300 cp, unique by position, none present in `hce-v4-tb`. Counts at each filter are in the manifest: 32,720 pure and 16,648 near survive before the draw, and 206 were excluded as `hce-v4-tb` positions.
  - Colosseum reads the first four EPD fields of each line and starts at halfmove 0 (`plies` applies only to PGN books); all 8,000 parse.
- **PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION (freeze before exposure):**
  1. Cohort SPRT, candidate over head: nElo point estimate **+6**, 80% band [−4, +16]. Probability H1 0.35, H0 0.50, unresolved at the cap 0.15.
  2. STC harm read: the point estimate lies within ±10 nElo (probability 0.9). The harm rule does not fire (probability 0.95).
  3. Hazard read (RAR-E28's prediction 3), over the cohort games: the candidate makes more first crossings into a pure ending of six men or fewer than the head (probability 0.6). Those games are drawn more often than its other games (probability 0.6).
  - Expected Elo at STC overall: near zero (the class is rare); not a basis for a bound.
  - Most likely failure mode: competing (1) or (2) inside the cohort, ending at H0.
- **Falsification criteria:** void and rerun if the runner, a binary or the book differs from the registration (`colosseum.ps1` refuses each), if the run's manifest records a busy-host waiver, or if a game ends by forfeit or crash. H1 is refuted by H0 in the cohort SPRT.
- **Cheapest prior falsifier:** RAR-E27's KBPP-KB conversion loss, at six men, where this rule does not act (the split's check: the candidate reproduces the head's six-man truth and drawn reports position by position, 6,000 records).
- **Registered gate and stop rule:**
  - **Cohort SPRT:** Colosseum, pentanomial GSPRT `model=normalized`, `[0,10]` nElo, α = β = 0.05, cap 8,000 pairs (the book has 8,000 positions and does not wrap), `3+0.03`, 1T, Hash 64, concurrency 14, no adjudication, the book in random order at seed 2026101001.
  - **STC harm read:** Colosseum `match`, 2,000 games on the UHO book, the same conditions, seed 2026101002. Harm rule: the read fails if its nElo point estimate is below −12.8. That is a 5% false alarm at a true zero with a standard error of about 7.8 nElo (AGENTS, gating, repair class 2); it catches a true −10 with probability about 0.36 and −20 with about 0.82.
  - Nothing changes after a game is seen.
  - **Use, frozen:** H1 in the cohort and the harm rule not fired: C.5.2 is accepted. Its fingerprint becomes the development fingerprint, and C.4 opens on the branch after it. H0, or the harm rule fired: the engine change is reverted in its own commit, and C.5.2's opposite-bishop part closes on RAR-E26 to RAR-E29, with C.5.2.1 as its retry. Unresolved at the cap: not accepted (an unresolved stop is not "probably fine"), and the maintainer decides between revert and a new registration.
  - The hazard read runs on the cohort's PGN with `tools/diag/ocb_boundary_read.py` (`a9db5e4`) and decides nothing.
