- **PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION (frozen 2026-10-21, before any game):**
  - *Rating:* **3,385**, 80% band **[3,340, 3,430]**, so about +100 over RAR-M64. Basis: Phase C's gates against their predecessors at `3+0.03` sum to about +152 (evaluation units about +106, first-crossing estimates and so overstated; C.12's speed +42.3; C.10's tune +4.5), less about 3 for rustc 1.99.1's 1.6% (RAR-P37). - **Falsification:** a rating at or below RAR-M64's 3,286.5 says Phase C bought no strength against other engines; it would be reported as such and investigated before the freeze. Outside the band is a calibration miss, not a verdict.
- **Stop rule:** one run of 2,400 games, no early stop, no extension. A run with engine faults, time losses or protocol failures above the harness's ceilings is refused by the harness and repeated, not read.
