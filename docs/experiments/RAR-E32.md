- **Research question:** does the C.4.3 unit (`analysis/c4_threats_mobility_2026-10-10.md`, *Implementation handoff*), fitted on the programme's corpus, absorb the threats residual RAR-E30 and RAR-E31 measured and make the engine stronger at `3+0.03`, search frozen?
- **Hypothesis / proposed mechanism:** the king attacking a weak piece and sliders bearing on safe, double-attacked squares around a lone queen are information Rarog's threats terms lack or condition differently (RAR-E31: −0.024 and −0.047 points by removal over all rows; the king threat −0.108 in the endgame band). Fitted, they take the threats family's residual from +0.107% towards zero, and the search plays the corresponding endings and queen manoeuvres better.
- **Competing hypotheses:** (1) *already absorbed in play:* the residual is real statically but the search finds these threats anyway, so gate 1 reads near zero. (2) *the restricted term's change:* sharing the strongly-protected set redefines restricted squares (a square both sides attack twice now counts), and the refit's re-weighting of that term moves scores more than the two new terms do. (3) *the omitted blockers:* the safe set leaves out the king's blockers, so the queen term over-counts pinned-piece squares and the screen keeps a residual. (4) *magnitude:* the king threat's eg seed (43 cp) is large for a flat bonus present in 6% of positions and moves the endgame score distribution.
- **Interacting mechanisms / consumers:** every cp-valued margin and the correction history (through the score); `LazyMargin` (the terms run only below the gate); the restricted term (changed definition); hanging, weak-piece and minor/rook threat terms (overlapping victims); king safety (the queen term's squares lie near the enemy queen, not the king, so no shared input); the eval and pawn caches (pure functions of the position; unchanged). The texel trace carries the two new slots and drops the removed one; `linear_delta_scale` is untouched.
- **Invariants and how they are tested:** unit tests on crafted positions in `src/eval/threats.rs`: the weak set and its strongly-protected exclusions, the king threat, every exclusion of the safe set, the double-attack requirement, the single-queen doubling, and exactly one enemy queen. The texel reconstruction test (exact over random playouts) and the activation test cover the new slots. Debug 390 and release 391 tests pass, clippy (`--all-features --all-targets`) at zero warnings, `cargo fmt --check` clean, all at `f962c0d`. One property holds by construction and is recorded rather than tested: a counted queen-threat square is attacked twice by us, so the strongly-protected set excludes it only through an enemy pawn attack.

  - Which assumption failed:
    - (2): the interaction map did not name the donor's own weak-queen term (a queen whose slider blockers allow a pin or discovered attack). It sits in the donor's pieces family, and Rarog's removed x-ray term, which counted rooks and bishops lined up on the queen through pawns, approximated it. Removing the term moved that information from Rarog's score into the donor's pieces residual. This is a missed interaction, not a failure of the threats terms, and the gate measures it.
    - (6): the per-node statement weighed the new terms' lookups against "the removed x-ray loop" but treated the two as comparable. The measurement says the removal dominates.
