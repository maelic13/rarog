- **Result:**
  - **Floor.** The budget's fifty-move horizon at every clock drifts a `3+0.03` game to a 155 ms equilibrium; 22–41% of the head's games touch 100 ms and 21% of its moves in RAR-M75's field are played under 200 ms. Stockfish 11, Dragon 1 and Komodo 13.3 never go under 200, 790 and 650 ms; Houdini 3 and 6.03 go as low as the head. Stockfish's sub-second contraction alone lifts the equilibrium to 400 ms.
  - **Holds and forfeits.** OS holds of 80 ms or more hit 0.11–0.15% of moves at one thread (0.77% in the daytime LTC run) and 0.4% at four threads; four time losses in 226,180 games at the 20 ms margin since 2026-10-10 (3 in 219,180 at `3+0.03`), each a 69–129 ms hold with 70–163 ms on the clock in a lost game. The reserve cannot cover them; the horizon can. Elo value here under 0.01.
  - **Spend.** Median 0.78–0.91 of the optimum, 90th percentile 1.9–2.3, 99th 3.7–4.5; 21–31% of all head time is past the optimum; 2.4–4.3% of moves spend three optima or more.
  - **Stalls.** RAR-R13's reading reproduces on the C head: won and mate moves at ≥ 0.9 × the maximum 0.30–0.44% and 0.24–0.53% at `3+0.03`, 1.44% and 3.40% at `10+0.1`; all in wins bar three gauntlet draws.
  - **Checklist.** Present: distinct budgets, immutable maximum at `go` receipt, completed-iteration adjustment by change, trend and effort. Absent: a stability-over-iterations discount (both donors have one), ponder credit, a first-iteration guard on the hard stop. Different: helper voting (D.2). The effort term has Stockfish's shape (a cut for concentrated effort, inert otherwise), not a defect; Reckless's is a continuous extender.
  - **Correction:** RAR-R13 said Stockfish has no clock-mode mate stop; it stops at `mate_in(3)` and `mated_in(2)` (checked at `sf_19` and master). rec2's `mate 5` lies outside that rule; the reading stands. Noted in RAR-R13.
- **Disposition: `READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION`** — one clock-only cluster (PLAN D.1.3): sub-second horizon contraction, stability factor, the effort band as coordinates, a proved-mate stop, a root fail-high stop past the soft target, a first-iteration guard; fitted by SPSA in registered blocks (D.1.4) and gated by one SPRT `[0,3]` with `10+0.1` and 4T direction reads and the probe and profile checks (D.1.5). - **Conditional lesson:** a forfeit margin is a horizon question when the clock drifts to the increment's equilibrium: the reserve that would cover a 100 ms hold at a 100 ms clock does not exist, the policy that never holds 100 ms does. Measure a clock's floor, not only its overruns.
