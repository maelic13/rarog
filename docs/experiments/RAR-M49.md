## Experiment and conditions

**A.5 conversion meter re-read on the 2.4.0 release binary, 2026-09-13, zero games.** RAR-M47's retry trigger, fired at the start of Phase B. The RAR-M45 rating tournament `5e539523-bfbf-4b5d-a838-13e75dff486d` (twelve engines, `3+0.03`, 1T, 600 games per pair, no adjudication) exported with `tools/diag/export_tournament_pgn.py` to 39,600 games, 43.6 MB, **sha256 `fe0cf072acaeba95427a9fa549ca50a9662350380c664f9071dfa0ffe0b863d5`**, held in ignored `analysis/artifacts/conversion-5e539523.pgn`; `tools/diag/conversion_audit.py` run with the registered parameters (12 plies, advantage >= 3, endgame at non-pawn material <= 26, lone-minor exclusion on) for **Rarog 2.4.0** (`rarog-v2.4.0-windows-pext-native-pgo.exe`) and, as the same-tournament comparator, **Basilisk 1.9.3** (`basilisk-v1.9.3-windows-x86_64-pext-pgo.exe`, the same executable path the 2026-09-04 pool ran, checked in Colosseum's engine configs) against the same six HCE-era opponents: Critter 1.6a, Fritz 16, HIARCS 14, Houdini 1.5a, Rybka 4, Shredder 12. ## Result / disposition

**Rarog 2.4.0: 88 draws and 19 losses after a persistent piece-up in 3,600 games - 24.4 and 5.3 per 1,000 games - against 23.8 and 5.0 per 1,000 on the 2026-09-04 pool (57/12 in 2,400). The release binary's rate is unchanged.** 81 of the 88 draws are in an endgame phase; terminations FiftyMove 57, Threefold 23, InsufficientMaterial 5, Stalemate 3, so 80 of 88 are failure to make progress with material in hand, the same composition as before. Saves from a persistent deficit: 54 draws and 15 wins. **Basilisk 1.9.3: 94 draws and 12 losses in its 3,600 games - 26.1 and 3.3 per 1,000 - against 16.7 and 5.0 on the 2026-09-04 pool (40/12).** Thrown away: Rarog **107**, Basilisk **106**. Per opponent, draws-up/losses-up in 600 games each - Rarog: Critter 10/6, Fritz 14/4, HIARCS 16/1, Houdini 1.5a 15/3, Rybka 4 14/2, Shredder 19/3; Basilisk: 10/2, 16/0, 16/3, 16/5, 18/2, 18/0. The comparator's rise is spread over all six opponents, not one pairing. Both engines' overall draw rate also rose about 1.3 points between the pools (Rarog 19.1% -> 20.4%, Basilisk 18.4% -> 19.7%).

## Conditional lesson and retry trigger

**RAR-M47's comparative claim is not reproduced and is SUPERSEDED.** It read Rarog's 57/12 against Basilisk's 40/12 as 'what makes this a defect rather than a consequence of being weaker'; on the same executables in a second tournament of identical settings the two engines throw away the same number of games, and the engine that moved is the comparator (draws-up 16.7 -> 26.1 per 1,000 on an unchanged binary, two-sample Poisson p ~ 0.017), while Rarog held within 3%. Basilisk 1.9.3 is also no longer 'a weaker engine': RAR-M45 measures it at -9 +/- 22. **What survives:** Rarog's absolute rate - about 3.0% of games against this field thrown away, four fifths of them by fifty-move or repetition with material in hand - is stable across two independent 2,400- and 3,600-game samples and is the figure C.5 works from; the 'worse than a weaker engine' direction does not survive and must not be cited for C.5.2. **Ceiling unchanged:** perfect conversion of the 88 draws and 19 losses would be worth about 8.7 and 3.7 Elo over this population, order-of-10, consistent with PLAN's endgame budget. **Instrument lesson:** a 2,400-game cross-engine comparison of a ~2% event has counts of 40-60 per arm and cannot carry a 'surplus' claim between two engines; compare an engine against its own prior sample first, and a competitor only inside the same tournament. Diagnostic layer; accepts nothing. **Retry trigger:** re-run at the B.9 and C.11 checkpoints and at each C.5 gate against a fresh tournament, frozen summary against frozen summary; Rarog's baseline pair is now 57/12 at 2,400 and 88/19 at 3,600.
