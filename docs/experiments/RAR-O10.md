# RAR-O10 — Rarog's and Basilisk's evaluations and moves against a reference, without hybrids

Indexed under *3. Search and selectivity › Search-oracle observations* in [`docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`](../EXPERIMENTS.md).

- **Date / owner:** registered 2026-11-14, before any measurement and before the drivers are built. Preparation and the zero-game reads are the agent's (PLAN D.7.2.1), run on an idle host after Basilisk's running SPRT ends and before any further oracle read (maintainer, 2026-11-14). The optional match (c) is the maintainer's command.
- **Kind:** observation, zero games for (a) and (b). It decides nothing by itself; it is an input to the maintainer's decision on D.7.3 to D.7.9.
- **Research question:** Basilisk's search programme gained about 100 Elo more than Rarog's from level starts, yet Basilisk was 79.5 ± 18.7 behind Rarog's head on 2026-11-08 and has not yet had an evaluation programme. Is Basilisk's evaluation already better or worse than Rarog's, and where do the two whole engines choose worse moves than a strong reference? That separates "Basilisk's lead is its search and its fit" from "Basilisk's evaluation carries it" without breaking either engine's fit.
- **Why now, and why not a hybrid:**
  - Rarog 2.4.0 and Basilisk 1.10.2 were level; Basilisk's search programme read +318.1 ± 24.5 against Rarog's +252.6 ± 20.4 (RAR-M84), and Basilisk added +15.6 ± 5.9 (root rebuild) and +28.2 ± 7.9 (joint tune) since, both self-play SPRTs stopped early (Basilisk `5f61c94`). On 2026-11-08 Basilisk's dev build was −73.2 ± 18.3 against Rarog 2.6.0 and −79.5 ± 18.7 against Rarog's head. Rarog's evaluation programme gave 2.6.0 +153.4 over 2.5.0 (RAR-M76); Basilisk's has not started, and the maintainer expects it may lift Basilisk above Rarog.
  - RAR-O09's match (1): Reckless's shape, unfitted, is level with Rarog's at equal work (+3.47 ± 8.37), so D.7 rests on its second aim and D.7.3 to D.7.9 are held.
  - A hybrid moves one engine's evaluation under another's search and so removes the fit each search was tuned around (maintainer, 2026-11-14). Here every evaluation is read alone, and every search only with its own evaluation.
- **Competing hypotheses:**
  - *H1, the search and its fit.* Basilisk's evaluation is weaker than Rarog's (Rarog's programme closed 82 of 182 Elo of the equal-node deficit to Stockfish's classical evaluation, RAR-O05 to RAR-O07; Basilisk has had only BAS-E03's distillation, +6.75); its larger search-programme gain is in its search and fit. Predicts (a) Rarog's evaluation more accurate, and (b) Basilisk's moves no worse than its evaluation alone would suggest.
  - *H2, the evaluation.* Basilisk's evaluation is already as good as Rarog's or better; its evaluation programme then has less room, and its deficit to Rarog is in the search, speed or clock.
  - *H3, a weaker start.* Basilisk 1.10.2 had the weaker search and the stronger evaluation behind a level total, so its search programme had more to gain; the two kernels now choose moves about equally well.
- **Instrument:**
  1. *Positions.* RAR-M84's round robin (`analysis/artifacts/dev-test-2026-11-08/dev_test.pgn`, 52,800 games, twelve engines; both engines played in it, neither in self-play). The position after every 8th ply from ply 16, file order, deduplicated by FEN, sampled with seed 20261114 to **10,000**. The *quiet* subset for (a): side to move not in check, the reference's best move neither a capture, a promotion nor a check, and the reference's score within ±600 cp.
  2. *Reference.* Stockfish 19 (`D:\chess\engines\stockfish\stockfish-19-windows-x86-64-universal.exe`), network evaluation, at `go nodes 1000000`, Hash 64, one thread, `ucinewgame` per position, `UCI_ShowWDL` on. The label is its expected score for the side to move, (W + D/2) / 1000. The game result is a second, independent label.
  3. *(a) The evaluations alone.* One static evaluation a position, side to move: Rarog's head (`rarog::eval::Evaluator::evaluate` through a small driver linking the crate at the RAR-O09 arms' revision, as the hybrid patch does), Basilisk's head (`Evaluator::evaluate(const Board&)` through a small C++ driver on its sources at the build after its running SPRT), and two anchors of known order: Stockfish 11's classical evaluation (`eval`, *Final evaluation*), and Rarog's evaluation at Phase C's start (the RAR-O05 revision, through `tools/oracle-hce-ffi` or the same driver). For each, fit expected score = 1 / (1 + exp(−eval / k)) by cross-entropy against the reference label on half the quiet positions (fixed split), and report on the other half: cross-entropy relative to the constant predictor, and Spearman rank correlation. By class: game phase (material in four bands), pawn endings, minor endings, middlegames with queens. Intervals by a paired bootstrap over positions, 2,000 resamples.
  4. *(b) The whole engines.* Each engine as it plays, its own evaluation and search, Hash 64, one thread, a fresh process per batch of 100 positions with `ucinewgame` between, at `go movetime 100` on all 10,000 positions. Each move is scored by the reference on the position after it, at the same 1,000,000 nodes; the loss is the reference's expected score at the root minus its expected score after the move (zero for its own best move). Reported: mean loss, the share of moves losing more than 0.10, nodes and time a move (speed is visible, not removed), by the classes above; paired per position, bootstrap intervals. The engines' actual thinking times are checked on a 200-position smoke first and the commands adjusted by whole milliseconds to equal medians, as in RAR-O09.
  5. *(c) The current gap, optional.* Rarog's head (RAR-O09's `rarog-head.exe`) against Basilisk's head, fastchess through `tools/sprt.ps1`, 3,000 games at `3+0.03`, seed 20261114, no adjudication, about 36 minutes. Run only if a use is named: a decision that needs the Elo scale of today's gap. Basilisk's own ledger measures what its evaluation programme gains.
- **Validity, frozen:** the anchors must come out in their measured order on the held-out quiet half, intervals excluding zero: Stockfish 11's evaluation more accurate than Rarog's head (RAR-O07, +99.2 ± 16.6 at equal nodes), and Rarog's head more accurate than its Phase C start (RAR-O05 against RAR-O07, 181.7 against 99.2). If either fails, (a) does not rank evaluations by strength here and is reported as uninformative; (b) stands on its own. Every reference search must end with a WDL line, every engine move must be legal, and both drivers must reproduce their engine's own static evaluation on 100 positions, read through an existing evaluation path in each engine that the preparation names and records, or the preparation stops.
- **Interacting mechanisms / confounds:** loss against a reference is not Elo: Stockfish distillation improved Rarog's holdout loss by 4.9% and lost 17.11 Elo (RAR-X03), so (a) locates and ranks, it accepts nothing. Static accuracy ignores what a search needs from an evaluation (stable ordering of nearby positions, scale across phases, cost). The reference is a network: where it and every HCE disagree, all HCEs look worse together, which cancels in the comparison but not in absolute numbers. Equal move time keeps speed and the language in (b). The positions come from games of these engines and their field, so neither engine is out of its distribution.
- **PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION (frozen 2026-11-14, before any measurement):**
  - (a) Rarog's head more accurate than Basilisk's head on the quiet half, interval excluding zero: **0.65**. Basilisk more accurate, interval excluding zero: 0.15. The anchors pass: 0.80.
  - (b) Mean loss a move at 100 ms: Basilisk's within ±10% of Rarog's overall: 0.55; Basilisk's lower in middlegames with queens and higher in endgames: 0.45.
  - (c) If played: Rarog's head over Basilisk's head **+35** [0, +70] at 80% (RAR-M84's −79.5 plus Basilisk's +44 since, which overstates).
  - Expected Elo for Rarog: none; an instrument.
  - Most likely failure mode: (a)'s anchors pass but the two engines' evaluations differ by less than the interval, which then says only that neither evaluation is far ahead.
- **Use of the reading, frozen:**
  - (a) Basilisk's evaluation clearly worse than Rarog's, anchors passing: H1 gains weight; Basilisk's evaluation programme has room and the maintainer's expectation stands; Basilisk's search programme, not Reckless's, becomes the candidate source if D.7 reopens for strength. Not worse: H2 or H3; Basilisk's evaluation programme has less room, D.7's hold continues, and its case stays the second aim.
  - (b) The classes where each engine loses most go to D.7.8's problem list and to E.4; a class where Rarog loses clearly more than Basilisk at equal time is a named lead.
  - None opens or closes a leaf by itself; all go to the maintainer's decision on D.7.3 to D.7.9.
- **Stop rules:** a driver that does not reproduce its engine's evaluation stops the preparation; anchors failing make (a) uninformative; no re-run of (a) or (b) on the same binaries and positions.
- **Cost:** the two drivers and the scripts, about an hour's agent work, built only on an idle host; the reference labels, about 10,000 + 20,000 searches of 1,000,000 nodes, about 40 minutes at 14 at once; the engines' 20,000 searches at 100 ms, about 3 minutes. (c) about 36 minutes if named.
- **Result:** not measured.
