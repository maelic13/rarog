# RAR-E22 — C.0 audit: RAR-E18's "excess above six men" re-read with one Stockfish version, and RAR-E21's king gains with clipped features

Indexed under *5. Evaluation and data experiments* in [`docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`](../EXPERIMENTS.md).

- **Date / owner:** registered 2026-10-06, before either read was computed; agent-run (zero games, static evaluation calls only), during the maintainer's audit of C.0.
- **Baseline SHA / candidate SHA / dirty-diff hash:** `dev` at `d63d0ef`; engine source unchanged since `ee02ed1` apart from the behaviour-neutral commits PLAN D.3 lists (fingerprint re-measured at this head on 2026-10-06: 11,171,726 / EBF 2.512). No candidate.
- **Binary / compiler / PGO identity:** the RAR-E21 instrumented Stockfish `9587eeeb` (`D33AC7A5…0D5B`, `tools/diag/patches/sf9587_king_subterms.patch`); RAR-E17's `scores.csv` (`F684F571…60DB`) for Rarog's played score, the control total and the labels; RAR-E21's `ksdump.csv` (`F78EC903…7A04`).
- **Research question:** (a) RAR-E18 read, at seven men or more, `rarog+stockfish` +2.73% against `rarog+all_families` +1.62%, and called the 1.1-point excess "total-level" information that the families do not carry, naming the scale factor; RAR-E21 found the scale factor adds +0.09% there. But RAR-E17's families were Stockfish 11's trace while its total was `9587eeeb`'s: six months of patches apart, and `9587eeeb`'s own `Winnable` row folds the scale factor into its eg. Does the excess survive when the families and the total come from the same version? (b) RAR-E21's king features were not clipped (the white-minus-black king row reaches 3,638 cp); do its headline gains hold when the king features are clipped at ±500 and ±300 cp?
- **Hypothesis / proposed mechanism:** (a) most of the excess is the version mismatch and the unprinted scale effect: with `9587eeeb`'s own families (its `Winnable` row standing in for Stockfish 11's `Initiative`), `rarog+all_families` closes to within 0.4 points of `rarog+stockfish` at seven men or more. (b) The king gains are carried by the bulk of the distribution, not the tail: clipping changes them by under 0.1 points.
- **Competing hypotheses:** (a′) the excess is a genuine joint effect of the families or of the lazy exits and grain (the total is not the sum of the printed rows): the gap stays above 0.8 points with one version. (b′) the tail carries the king gain: clipping at ±300 cp lowers it by 0.2 points or more.
- **Interacting mechanisms / consumers:** none in the engine. (a) decides whether C.5.1's first zero-game step is already answered and how much "total-level" knowledge Phase C can still chase above six men; (b) qualifies RAR-E21's attribution.
- **PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION (freeze before exposure):** gains in percent of Rarog's held-out mean squared error, RAR-E17's method, refitted inside each cohort.
  1. Seven men or more, `9587eeeb` families: `rarog+all_families` gains at least **+2.3%** (probability 0.5) and the gap to `rarog+stockfish` is under 0.5 points (probability 0.5).
  2. Over all rows, `rarog+all_families` with `9587eeeb` families gains at least **+2.5%** against Stockfish 11's +1.22% (probability 0.6), because the `Winnable` row carries the scale effect that RAR-E18 read as +5.4% at six men or fewer.
  3. The wire: the rank correlation of the `9587eeeb` family sum with its own total is at least 0.99 (probability 0.8).
  4. Clipping: `rarog+king9587` and `rarog+ks_danger` at ±500 cp are within 0.10 points of RAR-E21's +0.655% and +0.604% (probability 0.7); at ±300 cp within 0.15 (probability 0.6).
  - Expected Elo sign/range: none; static loss only.
  - Most likely failure mode: prediction 1 fails with the gap near 1 point, which would say the excess is real and joint, and C.5.1's cut keeps its question.
- **Falsification criteria:** hypothesis (a) is refuted if the gap at seven men or more stays above 0.8 points; (b) is refuted if the ±300 cp clip lowers either king gain by 0.2 points or more. The instrument is void if any row fails to parse or prediction 3's wire check fails.
- **Cheapest prior falsifier:** RAR-E21's direct scale-factor direction (+0.09% at seven men or more), which already removed the named candidate.
- **Registered gate and stop rule:** no gate; one collection, one `donor_residual.py analyse --within` run on the new scores, one clipped re-run of RAR-E21's two models; no further cohorts. **Use, frozen:** if (a) holds, C.5.1's first zero-game step is recorded as done here and its text points at the joint remainder only; if (a′) holds, C.5.1 keeps the step as written. If (b′) holds, RAR-E21's disposition is amended to say the king gain is tail-driven and C.3's cap is tightened before implementation.
- **Full conditions / provenance:** `tools/diag/donor_terms_9587.py collect` runs the instrumented build over RAR-E17's rows and writes a file in `scores.csv`'s format with `9587eeeb`'s rows in the family columns (`Winnable` in the `initiative` columns) and its `Final evaluation` in `sf11_total`; `rarog` and `sf_control` are copied from RAR-E17's file. `python tools/diag/donor_residual.py analyse --within --scores <new> --out <report>`. The clipped read: RAR-E21's dump, the king bundle and the danger part clipped to ±500 and ±300 cp before the two-fold fit, cohort `all`.
