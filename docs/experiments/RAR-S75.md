## Result / disposition

**Finished 2026-09-20: 5,000 of 5,000 iterations, 160,000 games** (`t = 160,000`), about 31 s per iteration over 62 active hours at 62 games per minute against the registered estimate of 82–97. Pilot 2026-09-15, tune 2026-09-15 to 2026-09-20 in seven sessions; the staged reviews at 1,250 and 2,500 were not taken (the run was launched without `-StopAfter`), which changes nothing: they were read-only. **Final theta** (the maintainer's paste matches `state.json` rounded half away from zero on all 82 coordinates; no coordinate at a range limit) **baked in engine `14a7079`**, 80 of 82 differing from the donor-shaped seeds. Two `b2core` tests that pinned the seeds were made coordinate-reading first, in `54a8115`. **New fitted fingerprint 7,185,678 / EBF 2.444** against the unfitted arm's 4,706,910 / EBF 2.391; binary `rarog-b24b-core-pext-pgo.exe`, sha256 `2A586F3B…`. **The tail, zero games:** from 3,900 to 5,000 the median coordinate moved 0.15 of a step, one moved at least half a step and none a full step, so the last 1,100 iterations sharpened values rather than moving any of them; from the seeds, 17 coordinates moved at least one step and 2 at least three. **Harness note:** the tune's PGN carries 314 time forfeits in 125,710 games, all past ply 78, from the start-up stall B.2.8 fixed; weather-factory restarts engines every iteration. Both arms of every mini-match ran the same binary, so it is symmetric noise, not bias. The registered binary was deliberately not rebuilt mid-tune.

## Conditional lesson

**Calibration: missed in magnitude by more than ten times.** The frozen **+12 ± 11** for the fitted arm was scored at B.2.4b, which measured **+138.60 ± 30.66** over the same pair (2026-09-20), so the falsifier — "≤ 0 means the donor's seeds were already near the STC optimum" — is refuted in the opposite direction: the seeds were far from Rarog's optimum, as RAR-S76's +118.72 at 3,900 iterations already indicated. The three measurements agree: +118.72 at 3,900, plus RAR-S77's +4.43 for the tail, against +138.60 ± 30.66 for the whole fit. B.6's joint tune therefore starts from the fitted defaults, and its prior should assume donor constants transfer poorly to this evaluation, not well.
