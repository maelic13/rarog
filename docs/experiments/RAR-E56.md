- **What counts as a regression (frozen):**
  - *Void, investigated before anything is read:* bench other than 14,197,472, a dirty engine tree, a cohort hash differing between arms, any Group A per-position record differing from `38cbb02`'s, or the truth read's family aggregates differing from RAR-E55's read of the same tree. These are instrument or identity failures, not engine findings.
  - *Regression:* `endgame_floors.py` returning FAIL (its own rule: a metric more than 3 SE under its floor, or the weighted conversion aggregate more than 2 SE under); any test failing in either profile; at 200k, any family converting 3 or more fewer clean wins than Stockfish (RAR-E48's defect clause) or 3 or more fewer than its earlier head on the same cohort. KNN-KP's recorded residual (39 against Stockfish's 50 of 77 at 200k, RAR-E55) is the recorded state, not a regression.
  - *Reported, not acting:* the floors' 2-to-3 SE reports. KRP-KB's DTZ progress (0.3222 → 0.2867, −2.7 SE on every read since RAR-E50) is known sample noise (RAR-E49, RAR-E50). Any other report in KRP-KB, KPP-K, KRP-KR, KNN-KP or KBP-K, whose per-move standard error is too small by 1.3 to 1.7 times, is read per game with `analysis/artifacts/floors400/analyse.py`'s estimator before it is called anything. KNN-KP's drawn overclaim (54.7%, RAR-E55) is a recorded cost and is not re-read. Group B differences inside the line, the game-level bound, the A.4 audit and the floors' improvements are reported.
- **Use, frozen:** with no void and no regression, C.5.8 and C.5 close DONE on this head, KRPP-KRP's exclusion is recorded and GUIDE's hold row resolved.
