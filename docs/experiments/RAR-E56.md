- **What counts as a regression (frozen):**

  - *Regression:* `endgame_floors.py` returning FAIL (its own rule: a metric more than 3 SE under its floor, or the weighted conversion aggregate more than 2 SE under); any test failing in either profile; at 200k, any family converting 3 or more fewer clean wins than Stockfish (RAR-E48's defect clause) or 3 or more fewer than its earlier head on the same cohort.
