- **Research question:** does the C.6 unit (`analysis/c6_pawns_passers_2026-10-11.md`, *Implementation handoff*), fitted on the programme's corpus, absorb the passed and pawns residual RAR-E39 and RAR-E41 measured and make the engine stronger at `3+0.03`, search frozen?
- **Hypothesis / proposed mechanism:** our king's distance to the square in front of a passer (and the next square), weighted by rank and fitted separately from the enemy king's, and the weak-unopposed penalty are information Rarog's terms lack (RAR-E41: +0.131 and +0.060 points by removal in the endgame band). Fitted, they take the donor's passed family in the endgame band from +0.205% towards zero and the pawns family from +0.123%, and the search plays pawn and passer endings better. The square rule makes the unstoppable bonus fire only on real races (RAR-E40).
- **Competing hypotheses:** (1) *absorbed in play:* the search already finds king support and races, so gate 1 reads near zero though the screen moves. (2) *the passed set:* the residual follows the donor's passed set, not its king terms, so the screen keeps most of it. (3) *removed information:* Rarog's old symmetric proximity carried the enemy king's race at the pawn itself; the new form measures at the block square, and the refit cannot recover what the old form held (watch the passed family and the endgame bands). (4) *magnitude:* the new proximity counts reach 85 a term, so the refit moves endgame scores and the magnitude read flags.
- **Interacting mechanisms / consumers:** every cp-valued margin and the correction history (through the score); `LazyMargin` (the proximity and unstoppable terms run below the gate; weak unopposed is in the pawn cache, before it); the free- and safe-path passer terms and the mop-up (overlapping positions); C.5.2's opposite-bishop rule (counts passers; the set is unchanged); the eval and pawn caches (pure functions of the position).
- **Invariants and how they are tested:** `bc69c46`'s tests: the proximity counts (the fourth-rank start, the seventh-rank skip of the next square, the cap, colour symmetry), weak unopposed (isolated, backward, opposed, once per pawn), the square rule in both move orders (failing on the old threshold); the texel reconstruction over random playouts; debug 390 and release 391 tests, clippy at zero warnings, fmt clean.
