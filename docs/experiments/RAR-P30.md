## Experiment and conditions

**B.7.2.13 audit screen, four further exact candidates — REGISTERED 2026-10-02, before any pooled read; SCREEN COMPLETE 2026-10-02.** An audit of the re-profile (`0d95763`, 231,374 samples) by source line and inline caller (`tools/results/b72-audit-20261002/`) located: the TT probe's first load at 6.89% of samples with its prefetch issued only after the move is made; quiet scoring's history loads at 6.07%; the correction's six loads at 2.45%; a `LazyLock` state check (0.71%) and a `Vec` pointer (0.55%) on every attack lookup. Prototypes on `b727b9b`, each `bench` 11,171,726: **A** a TT prefetch before the make from a predicted key; **B** the attack tables as one static with fixed arrays (prototype form, filled at startup through an `UnsafeCell`); **D** a prefetch of the history rows at `GenerateQuiets`; **I** a prefetch of the child's correction slots after the make (recipes `analysis/b72_audit_*.patch`). Screen: `screen.ps1`, four pext PGO builds per arm (A, B, D, I, all four) from the scratch tree against the head's pool (`analysis/artifacts/b72-nps-c1/c2`), 20 interleaved cycles each by `nps_multibuild.ps1`, idle host, about three and a half hours; a one-cycle dry run on a host in use checked the wiring and is not a read. **Rule, fixed here:** +0.5% or more with the 95% lower bound above 0 goes forward to its own commit and a clean-pool acceptance read; an upper bound under +0.5% closes `NO_CHANGE`; between, the maintainer decides. The screen accepts nothing. **Predictions, frozen:** A +0.5% to +3% (under the floor with probability 0.35); B +0.3% to +1.5% (0.5); D −0.5% to +3% (0.5); I +0.3% to +2% (0.45); all four +1.5% to +7% (0.1), under the sum of the parts. **Beside it, done:** candidate 3's scan on the engine's own entries, host in use: unpredictable selects `s` = 1.03, a packed key 0.95 and 0.91, read-before-swap 1.07.

## Result / disposition

**Screen, 2026-10-02 08:39–12:30** (`screen.log`, `screen-<arm>.log`), 20 cycles of 3 repeats per arm, CPU 4.2–14.2% at the arm boundaries: **all four +1.71%** (95% CI +0.88% .. +2.34%); **A +1.48%** (+0.91% .. +2.41%); **I +0.14%** (−0.29% .. +0.75%); **D −1.87%** (−2.36% .. −1.48%); **B −0.49%** (−1.00% .. +0.07%). In every arm the four candidate builds' medians move together. **By the rule:** A goes forward; I lies between, and by the maintainer's decision of 2026-10-02 is implemented and measured after A (RAR-P31); D and B close `NO_CHANGE`. The bundle is not the sum of its parts (+1.71% against −0.74%), an interaction or a per-source code-layout effect the screen cannot separate. Candidate 3: no variant reaches 1.1; the maintainer closed it `NO_CHANGE`.

## Conditional lesson and retry trigger

**Calibration:** the audit predicted the scan was bound by branch misprediction and about twice as fast without the branch; it read 1.03, a mechanism miss. A plain-build NPS screen on a host in use read ±3% and is no evidence. Screen: **A** hit, mid-range. **I** below its range at the point (+0.14 against +0.3 to +2), its interval overlapping the range: a magnitude miss, unresolved. **D** a sign miss beyond the range: the audit priced the history loads' latency, not the cost of issuing 65 prefetches per quiet generation nor the lines they evict (mechanism). **B** a sign miss: samples on a predictable state check and a pointer load were not removable cost (mechanism); the prototype's form is not the shippable one, so the instrument is in doubt too. **All four** inside its range at the low end, but its premise that the parts add failed (interaction). Retry D only as a prefetch of a few lines with its own screen; B only if the tables are made static for another reason.
