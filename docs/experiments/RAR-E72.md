- **What the review proposed, and one correction found at registration:** the review names `rarog-texel --dump-groups`' `king_danger` column as "Rarog's `king_danger` output per row". It is a flag (`u8::from(trace.king_danger)`: the map penalised at least one king); the map's output is untraced and sits in the trace's `frozen_mg` and `frozen_eg`, where the mate-drive mop-up also adds its eg share (`apply_mop_up`, which fires wherever the running score passes 200 cp, 48% of these rows by RAR-E71). - **Instrument:**
  - *Scratch patch* (`analysis/artifacts/c72k/patch_scratch.py`, each anchor asserted unique, line endings preserved): the texel trace gains `mop_up_eg`, written beside `frozen_eg` by the one site that adds the mop-up; `--dump-groups` prints `phase;frozen_mg;frozen_eg;mop_up_eg` after its flag. - **Disposition:** by the frozen rule two clauses fire in the deciding band, and the registration gave them no precedence: **(B) is at the line** (+0.194 ± 0.047, 4.15 clustered SE), the *conditioning* clause, and **(A) is under it** (+0.280 ± 0.078, 3.57 SE), the *closed* clause. The rule assumed, as the review did, that a free direction on Rarog's map could only lower the donor's gain and its resolution with it; it lowered the gain by three tenths and the clustered error by two fifths, so the control resolved where the plain reading did not. The read script, written before exposure, ordered *closed* first (`read.json`'s `decision`); that order was an implementation choice the registration did not state, so it settles nothing. The reading the experiment was built to make is (B), and it answers the review's question: with Rarog's own map output free to be re-weighted, the donor's king term still predicts the band's outcomes at the registered line, and over all rows (+0.137 ± 0.028, 4.9 SE) and at seven men or more (+0.152 ± 0.032, 4.7 SE) as well, with the null control at zero. The fitted weights say what kind of signal it is: Rarog's map alone over `C` is under the line in every cohort, and in B's model it takes a small negative weight beside a positive weight on the donor's term, so the outcomes neither want Rarog's map rescaled (magnitude) nor re-weighted on its own; they substitute the donor's king conditioning for part of Rarog's. **I read this as conditioning, not magnitude.** By the registered order stage 2 (reading C, the component ranking) is due, and its instrument needs the maintainer's one build command (`build_sf9587.sh`; the archive and the patch check pass, the compile was not run). Whether the lead opens a king-refinement research card before C.11 is the maintainer's decision, as the frozen use says; nothing is built from this read. Size, for that decision and at the static layer only: in the band +0.19% against the +1.58% C.3 started from there (RAR-E18) and over all rows +0.14% against +0.66% (RAR-E21); not Elo.
  - *What (A) under the line means:* the plain reading missed four clustered SE by the margin the registration named as its likeliest failure (3.57 against 4) on a point estimate that reproduces RAR-E70's (+0.280 against +0.282); a resolution miss, not evidence against the mechanism, and (B) is the sharper instrument for the same question.

  - Over all rows and at men ≥ 7, (B) exceeds (A): the substitution again (the donor's term net of Rarog's map is a cleaner direction than the donor's term alone; the two correlate at +0.74 to +0.82).

- **Conditional lesson:** when a control can change a reading's resolution and not only its size, register the rule on the discriminating reading alone, or order the clauses; a rule written as three unordered cases assumes the control is a pure nuisance.
