- **What the review proposed, and one correction found at registration:** the review names `rarog-texel --dump-groups`' `king_danger` column as "Rarog's `king_danger` output per row". It is a flag (`u8::from(trace.king_danger)`: the map penalised at least one king); the map's output is untraced and sits in the trace's `frozen_mg` and `frozen_eg`, where the mate-drive mop-up also adds its eg share (`apply_mop_up`, which fires wherever the running score passes 200 cp, 48% of these rows by RAR-E71). RAR-E59's `frozen` column therefore mixes the map with the mop-up and would contaminate the re-weighting control on about half the rows. The instrument below separates them exactly with a trace-only scratch patch; no column of the repository's tuner is changed for this read.
- **Instrument:**
  - *Scratch patch* (`analysis/artifacts/c72k/patch_scratch.py`, each anchor asserted unique, line endings preserved): the texel trace gains `mop_up_eg`, written beside `frozen_eg` by the one site that adds the mop-up; `--dump-groups` prints `phase;frozen_mg;frozen_eg;mop_up_eg` after its flag. Nothing on the evaluation path changes.
  - *Rarog's map output* `rarog_map`, per row, white minus black, in cp: `((frozen_mg)·phase + (frozen_eg − mop_up_eg)·(24 − phase)) / 24 × scale`, with `phase` the trace's (0..24) and `scale` the tuner's `linear_delta_scale` (the endgame factor the score receives, 1 almost everywhere in the band). The deciding direction is the **full path** (`--full`, the gate out of reach): what Rarog's map says about the position, whether or not the lazy gate let it into the played score (the gate is C.10's coordinate, not the map's conditioning). The played-path output is `rarog_map_played`, reported.
  - *Donor king* `donor_king`: the scores file's `King safety` row tapered by `sf11_phase` as `donor_residual.py` tapers it, the exact direction RAR-E70 read as `rarog+king`.
  - *Model:* RAR-E58's nested held-out logistic (two folds by alternating row, `donor_residual.py`'s fit): the gain of `C + D` over `C` in percent of `C`'s held-out squared error, where `C` is the hinge spline of the played score (`r` and hinges at the 25/50/75% quantiles of |r| inside the cohort, each side). Standard errors: the per-row one and RAR-E59's game-clustered bootstrap (2,000 resamples of the per-game sums, seed 20261019), the linearised ratio estimator beside it. The clustered error decides.
  - *Readings:* **A** `donor_king | C`; **B** `donor_king | C + rarog_map`; **C** (stage 2) `B` with one component of the donor's danger index left out, RAR-E21's list (attackers count × weight, weak ring, unsafe checks, blockers, king-adjacent attacks, flank attack², mobility difference, no-queen, knight defender, shelter feedback, flank defence, constant, safe checks), each component recomputed through the donor's own map from the dumped index, as `king_subterms.py analyse` does; the cost of a component is `B`'s gain (from the sub-term bundle) minus the gain without it. Reported, deciding nothing: `B'` `donor_king | C + rarog_map_played`; `rarog_map | C` (does re-weighting Rarog's own map gain anything at all); `rarog_map | C + donor_king`; a shuffled `donor_king` over `C + rarog_map` (the null control, seed 20261026); the standardised weights of `rarog_map` and `donor_king` in `B`'s model fitted on the whole cohort; rows per game; the correlation of the two directions; in stage 2 the danger part alone and the bundle without it over `C + rarog_map`, and the bundle without each of the five sub-terms.
  - *Cohorts* (each refitted inside): **phase 32–95 decides** (the donor's phase scale, `sf11_phase`, the band RAR-E70 read; 50,906 rows); reported: all rows, men ≥ 7, and the band at men ≥ 7.
- **Wires (frozen; any failure exits 1 and voids the read):** (1) the scores file's `rarog` column equals `head_played.csv` on every row, and this tree's tuner (`--dump-scores`) reproduces `head_played.csv` byte for byte; (2) RAR-E70's `rarog+king` gain in the band, `rarog` alone as the base, reproduces from the scores file to the last digit (**+0.2817 ± 0.0521**, per-row SE); (3) the scratch tuner's played dump equals `head_played.csv` byte for byte, its full dump's score equals `head_full.csv` on every row, and its `--dump-groups --full` columns equal the repository tuner's on every row; (4) in both scratch dumps `frozen` equals the taper of `frozen_mg` and `frozen_eg` within 6e-4 cp and the flag off means both map parts are zero on every row; (5) the provenance joins every row and reproduces the manifest's game counts. Stage 2 adds RAR-E21's own checks (the danger identity on every row, the five sub-terms within 0.011 pawns of the printed row) and: the dump's `King safety` row equals the scores file's on every row (one donor version), and `B` reproduces stage 1's value exactly.
- **Rule (frozen).** *At the line* means a gain of at least **0.10%** at **four game-clustered standard errors**, in the band:
  - **conditioning** when **B** is at the line;
  - **magnitude or re-weighting** when **A** is at the line and **B** is not;
  - **closed** when **A** is under the line.
  - Stage 2 (reading C) runs **only if B is at the line**, since the rule does not need it and its instrument must be rebuilt; it then names the components for the card. Nothing is added after the numbers are seen.
- **Use (frozen):** conditioning opens a king-refinement research card before C.11, decided with the maintainer (a C.3-shaped unit with a `[0,3]` gate, whose card starts from stage 2's component ranking); magnitude or re-weighting is recorded for the map's scales (`ks_map_mg`, `ks_map_eg`) and C.10's margins and closes the lead; closed closes the lead with nothing recorded beyond this read. Nothing is built from the read.
