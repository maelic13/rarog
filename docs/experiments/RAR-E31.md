# RAR-E31 — C.4.2 sub-term attribution: which of the donor's threats components carry its residual on the C.5.2 head, and whether its mobility residual is the area, the x-rays or the pin restriction

Indexed under *5. Evaluation and data experiments* in [`docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`](../EXPERIMENTS.md).

- **Date / owner:** registered 2026-10-10, after the components were rebuilt and verified against the donor's printed rows and before any model was fitted on them; agent-run (zero games, static evaluation only).

- **Research question:** RAR-E30 read the donor's threats family at +0.107 ± 0.020% of the head's held-out squared error over all rows (signal in four of six registered cohorts) and its mobility family at +0.184 ± 0.044 in the opening band only. (a) Which threats components carry the gain, and is it conditioning Rarog's terms cannot express by re-pricing (the weak and strongly-protected gating of minor and rook attacks, the safe-pawn and safe-push conditions, the king threat, weak queen protection, the queen threats on safe squares) or pricing of counts Rarog already has? (b) In the opening band, is mobility's gain the area definition (blocked and low pawns, the king and queen squares, the king's blockers), the x-rays through queens and own rooks, or the pin-line restriction?
- **Hypothesis / proposed mechanism:** H1: threats' residual is conditioning. The gated minor and rook attacks and the safe-pawn attack carry most of it by leave-one-out, and replacing them by their ungated counterparts (every attacked piece scored; pawn threats from any pawn) loses a measurable part of the family's gain. H3: mobility's opening-band residual is the area; the donor's tables under Rarog's area recover under half of it, and removing the x-rays or the pin restriction costs little.
- **Competing hypotheses:** H2: threats' residual sits in the terms Rarog lacks outright (the king threat, weak queen protection, the queen threats). H4: mobility's residual is the x-rays. H0: no component separates; the family is joint (RAR-E18 found the opening-band families joint), the ungated forms recover the gated gain, and the residual is pricing a refit could absorb.
- **Known exposure:** RAR-E30's family readings (disclosed there); the C.3 screen; no component reading of either family exists. The donor's constants are known (king threat S(24, 89), safe pawn S(173, 94), hanging S(69, 36)); they inform prediction 1.
- **Interacting mechanisms / consumers:** none in the engine. The reading decides C.4's design: what a unit builds, and whether mobility is in it. A unit that adopts x-rays or the pin restriction in the shared attack-map producer moves C.3's inputs (the research record's interaction map).
- **PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION (freeze before exposure):** gains in percent of the head's held-out squared error, two-fold logistic models refitted inside each cohort as `donor_residual.py` fits them; paired differences ± one standard error.
  1. **Leave-one-out, threats, all rows:** the three costliest removals are among `hanging`, `safe_pawn`, `by_minor` and `by_king` (probability 0.7); no single removal costs more than 60% of the family's gain (probability 0.6: the family is more joint than king's, whose danger score carried 92%).
  2. **Endgame band and no queens:** removing `by_king` costs at least 0.04 points there (probability 0.5); removing `safe_pawn` at least 0.04 (probability 0.5).
  3. **Gating:** the family with every gated term replaced by its ungated counterpart (`rarog+threats_ungated`) gains at least 0.03 points less than the gated family over all rows (probability 0.6), and the minor/rook gating alone (`threats_with_by_minor_ungated`, `..._by_rook_ungated`) accounts for at least half of that loss (probability 0.5).
  4. **Separate weights:** `rarog+threats_components` (the ten components as separate regressors) gains at most 0.05 points more than the single family over all rows (probability 0.7).
  5. **Mobility, opening band:** `rarog+mob_rarog` (the donor's tables under Rarog's area and attacks) gains at most half of `rarog+mob_v0`'s +0.18 (probability 0.6); `mob_no_xray` and `mob_no_pin` each within 0.03 points of `mob_v0` (probability 0.7 each); `rarog+mob_rarog+mob_v0` over `rarog+mob_rarog` at least +0.10% at four standard errors (probability 0.6).
  6. **Null:** `rarog+shuffled_threats` within ±0.03% in every cohort (probability 0.95).
  - Expected Elo: none; static loss ranks questions and accepts nothing.
  - Most likely failure mode: H0 on threats (every removal costs under 0.02 points and the ungated family is within one standard error of the gated one), which would make the threats residual a refit question.
- **Falsification criteria:** the instrument is void if `verify` reports a mismatch on the full file, if the null control exceeds ±0.05% in any cohort, or if `rarog+threats` in the `all` and `men>=7` cohorts differs from RAR-E30's threats reading by more than 0.01 points (same rows, same family, same model). H1 is refuted if the ungated family is within one standard error of the gated one over all rows and at seven men or more. H3 is refuted if `rarog+mob_rarog` recovers at least three quarters of `rarog+mob_v0`'s opening-band gain.
- **Cheapest prior falsifier:** RAR-E30 (the family level); nothing cheaper separates components.
- **Registered gate and stop rule:** no gate; one `analyse` run on the full dump, no cohort, model or component added after the numbers are seen. **Use, frozen (C.4's design input):**
  (i) **Threats.** A set of components whose joint removal costs at least 0.04 points at four standard errors (paired) in a cohort where the family has signal, and which Rarog's current terms cannot express by re-pricing (a gated or safe-set term whose ungated counterpart recovers under half of its contribution, or a term Rarog lacks), is the unit's threats content, built in the donor's shape and replacing the Rarog terms it overlaps: C.4's threats part becomes `READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION` with that content, and the research record freezes its handoff. If the ungated family recovers the gated family's gain (within one standard error) and no term Rarog lacks separates at 0.04 points and four standard errors, the residual is pricing: C.4's threats part closes `NO_CHANGE`, with the retry trigger the next whole-surface refit (C.8).
  (ii) **Mobility.** The unit includes the donor's mobility area only if `rarog+mob_rarog+mob_v0` over `rarog+mob_rarog` gains at least +0.10% at four standard errors in the opening band; the x-rays only if `mob_no_xray` loses at least 0.05 points to `mob_v0` at three standard errors (paired) there; the pin restriction only if `mob_no_pin` loses the same. Otherwise mobility closes `NO_CHANGE` inside C.4.
  (iii) Both parts `NO_CHANGE`: C.4 closes `NO_CHANGE` on RAR-E30 and RAR-E31.

- **Disposition:** observation, applied by the frozen use.
  (i) **Threats: `READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION`** with two components. **The king threat** (`by_king`: a flat bonus when the side's king attacks a weak enemy piece), a term Rarog lacks, costs 0.108 and 0.100 points by removal in the endgame band and without queens at five standard errors. **The slider threat on the queen in the donor's form** (bishop or rook attacks on the squares around the enemy's only queen that are safe, that is in the side's mobility area, not its own pawns and not strongly protected, counted where the side attacks twice, doubled when that queen is the only one on the board) costs 0.047 and 0.048 points over all rows and at seven men or more at six standard errors; Rarog's x-ray slider-on-queen term is in the head's fitted score, so this is beyond its re-pricing, and the unit replaces it. Not certified, measured and recorded for a later registered read: the rook-threat gating (removal under 0.04 in every signal cohort, but its ungated form is worse than nothing at seven standard errors), the push safety (the same shape, smaller), weak queen protection (−0.011 at five standard errors, under the line), the knight form of the queen threat (nil). The donor's `hanging`, `restricted`, `safe_pawn` and `by_minor` forms are not carriers: removing each helps or does nothing, so Rarog's own versions of those terms suffice. H1 is refuted in its specific claim (the minor and rook gating and the safe pawn are not what carries the gain over all rows) and right in its general one (gating matters: the ungated family loses 0.062 at eight standard errors); H2 is supported for the two terms above.
  (ii) **Mobility: `NO_CHANGE` inside C.4.** The registered line for the area was +0.10% at four standard errors in the opening band; the reading is +0.144 at 3.7, so the unit does not include the donor's mobility area, and neither the x-rays (−0.029 at 1.8 standard errors against a line of 0.05 at three) nor the pin restriction (−0.045 at 4.1 standard errors, under the 0.05 line) qualifies. The reading is real and small: the donor's definition adds about a seventh of a point in the opening band and about a tenth everywhere, most of it the pin restriction and the blockers' exclusion, and it would reach the shared attack-map producer that C.3 consumes. The retry trigger is below.

Mechanism miss, sign right. On mobility I under-weighted the pin restriction and over-weighted the x-rays, and set the four-standard-error line where the reading would land at 3.7.
- **Conditional lesson:** a family residual decomposes by where the receiving evaluation has *no* term or a differently conditioned one, not by where its gate is weaker: the donor's better-gated versions of terms Rarog already prices add nothing or hurt. And separate weights per component gained 0.09 points: the donor's internal weighting is not a given, so a unit's refit must fit each term's weight.
