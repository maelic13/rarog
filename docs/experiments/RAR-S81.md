## Result / disposition

**Stopped 2026-09-25 at iteration 940 of 2,000** (`tools/results/b33-spsa-1/`, 28,200 games, 0 faults), by maintainer decision after the review of a rail: `CoreSingularMargin` fell from 4 to its floor 1 by iteration 413 and stayed there, the steepest gradient on the surface; the other movers at the stop were `CoreNmpCutoff` +0.60, `CoreNmpDepth` +0.60, `CoreSingTripleQuiet` +0.57, `CoreNmpRBase` −0.51, `CoreProbcutImproving` −0.50 (steps), 6 of 36 at half a step. **Closed as a registration error**, not an implementation defect: the packet's own conversion put the donors' margin at about `0.5·d` and the surface seeded `4·d` with a floor of `1·d`; the −3 negative extension at cut nodes (27.7% of singular attempts, against the donor's −2) was the only population the margin could switch off, and the exact-bound span had no attribution (packet, *Research amendment 7*). The block is kept as evidence and seeds nothing. Superseded by RAR-S82.

## Conditional lesson

**Calibration:** the block-1 prediction (≥ 8 of 36 a full step, 0.7) was not resolved; at 47% of the horizon one coordinate had moved a full step, so it was on course to miss. The miss was in the instrument: a coordinate parametrised so that its range excluded the donor value.
