The oracle searched about 2 plies deeper than the head and lost; at RAR-O03 the gap was 1.39 plies (14.94 / 16.33). **Calibration, appended after exposure; the prediction is not rewritten.** Predicted −40, 80% band [−100, +15], P(G(0) ≥ 0) about 0.2: **a miss in sign**, 9 Elo above the band. The failed assumption is the discount: the prediction placed G(0) below transitivity because "gains measured within one lineage carry less against a different search". Transitivity held almost exactly: 2.4.0 lost 247.97 to the oracle, the head beat 2.4.0 by +272.4 in RAR-M64's head-to-head, and their sum, +24.4, is the measured +24.24. A second, smaller miss was in the input: the head stood +272 over 2.4.0, not the +250 used. The miss is in mechanism (the transfer assumption) and confidence; the instrument agrees with RAR-O03's to the recipe. Depth: head 15.0 ± 0.6 hit (14.65); oracle 16.2 ± 0.3 missed by +0.5 (16.70), unexplained (the oracle met different positions against a stronger opponent).

## Conditional lesson

**The search programme recovered the whole measured equal-time search deficit**, and more, while searching about 2 plies shallower than the oracle and, in bench NPS, 26% slower than 2.4.0 (RAR-P35). Under these conditions depth is not the currency: what each node buys is. The oracle still pays its FFI evaluation cost per node (RAR-O03's prior: 1.64 against 2.95 Mnps at 2.4.0), so G(0) remains a meter of search quality per unit time, not of node efficiency. G(0) is a diagnostic layer and accepts nothing. **Retry trigger:** re-measure at C.11 with the C head's evaluation in the oracle's DLL (the premise must be re-checked: the evaluation will have changed), and at E.1.
