# RAR-S01 — Pruning/margin SPSA Group B in the early search state

Indexed under *3. Search and selectivity › Accepted or retained* in [`docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`](../EXPERIMENTS.md).

## Experiment and conditions

Pruning/margin SPSA Group B in the early search state.

## Result / disposition

**Accepted, +6.17 ± 4.88 nElo** after 19,458 SPRT games.

## Conditional lesson

Joint fitting helped an under-tuned early parameter group. It does not imply repeated retunes of a mature group have similar value.
