- **Research question:** the rule beats C.5.2's split statically on both sides of the six-to-seven-men boundary and removes it (RAR-E45), but it scores more Syzygy wins under +100 cp than the head in KBP-KB and KBP-KBP. Does it cost wins when the engine plays them, in particular in KBPP-KB, where RAR-E26's count rule lost conversion through drift and simplification (RAR-E27)?
- **Hypothesis / proposed mechanism:** free passers restore the gradient the count rule flattened: a KBPP-KB ending with two free passers takes `s = 38` (the count rule's 26), so the strong side's moves that keep the passers free stay distinguishable to the search, and conversion holds; in KBP-KB the search finds the promotion whatever the level (RAR-E27: 253 of 253 under the count rule).
- **Competing hypotheses:** (1) *flattening again:* positions with blockaded passers drop to `s` of 14 to 22, and KBPP-KB drifts to the ply limit or trades into insufficient material as under the count rule. (2) *a free-passer oscillation:* `s` jumps by 8 when the defending king or bishop touches a path, so the search chases or avoids those squares and loses tempo. (3) *neutral:* the search converts whatever the factor, and only the drawn overclaim moves.
- **Interacting mechanisms / consumers:** the search's pruning margins and rule-50 damping, consuming the scaled score; the eval cache and TT (the factor is a pure function of the position); the texel delta scale (follows the evaluator); `tools/diag/ocb_scale_screen.py`'s offline copy of the rule (follows if the change lands); C.5.6 and C.5.7; GUIDE's closed boundary hold.
