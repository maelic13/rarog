## Result / disposition

**Fingerprints exact: 7,601,220 / EBF 2.474 and b2core 4,706,910 / EBF 2.391, 40/40 on magic and PEXT.** `info` stream byte-identical on both arms (440 lines each, no `multipv` token); at MultiPV 2 it differs with 400 `multipv 2` lines. Suites 295 release / 294 debug / 312 b2core release, 0 failed (+9 over B.2.1: seven MultiPV, two tablebase). **NPS +0.62%, 95% bootstrap [−0.29%, +1.18%]** (base 3,137,758, cand 3,157,308; best-of +0.63%). Infinite sessions: default arm depth 24, b2core 4T depth 31, four distinct lines, `bestmove` equal to line 1, stop answered at once, exit 0; the final default-arm session was stopped inside depth 24 and reported two ranked lines, an `upperbound` line and a carried depth-23 line identical to depth 23's line 2. **Two defects found and fixed before commit:** (1) the root's stored move is line 1's, which later lines exclude, so their TT move was nulled and IIR reduced lines 2..k a ply below their reported depth; later lines now skip the root store (both kernels' cutoff and final stores) and take their own candidate as the root TT move, as Stockfish (`rootMoves[pvIdx].pv[0]`, no store when `pvIdx`) and Reckless (`!(NODE::ROOT && pv_index > 0)`) do; by construction, not measured. (2) A stopped depth filled unsearched lines from root records: the first b2core 4T session reported `depth 32 multipv 4 score cp 7 pv c4f7`, a fail-low bound depth 32 never reported; lines now come from the last completed depth's report, and the node-budget sweep test fails with the record fill restored. **Reviewed and accepted 2026-09-15** by a separate session: the diff read in full against the frozen contract and both kernels; fresh builds of `4024c72` and its parent `b93f7a4` on both arms reproduce 7,601,220 / 2.474 and 4,706,910 / 2.391; the depth-10 `info` stream over the 40 bench positions is byte-identical to the parent on both arms (480 lines each) and MultiPV 2 changes it (400 `multipv 2` lines); fmt, clippy on all features and on `b2core`, suites 295 / 294 / 312 / 311 (off release, off debug, core release, core debug), 0 failed; stopped `go infinite` sessions at MultiPV 4 on 1T, 4T and the 4T candidate answered `stop` within 1 ms, `bestmove` equal to line 1, lines distinct, an interrupted line carrying `upperbound` and an unsearched line carried from the previous depth. One deviation from the frozen text beyond the two recorded: after each line the implementation sorts the lines ranked at this depth by score and leaves the unranked moves in previous-depth order, where the contract said stable-sort the remaining records; the code states the reason (an unranked move proved only an upper bound), and it is accepted. Logs in ignored `tools/results/b202-review-20260915/`.

## Conditional lesson and retry trigger

A frozen protocol contract still misses the interactions its mechanism has with the kernel: both defects came from the root restriction meeting consumers the contract did not list (IIR on a nulled TT move, root records holding bounds), and one was invisible at fixed depth, so a feature that changes stop-time output needs a stopped-search test. The retry trigger for B.5: any change to root TT reads, IIR at the root or root record semantics reruns `tests/multipv.rs` and the stop session.
