## Experiment and conditions

**Re-tune of the most-moved B.2.3 coordinates on Colosseum — DESIGN REGISTERED 2026-09-19. The full registration (surface list, steps, horizon, binaries, Colosseum version, cap) is completed before launch, and no game is played before it.** Question (maintainer): did RAR-S75 miss the optimum of its most-moved coordinates because 82 were tuned together? **Competing explanations:** (i) *dimension*: the other coordinates' perturbations add noise to each gradient. In SPSA a coordinate adds noise in proportion to its own gradient, so near-optimal ones add little, and most of the noise is the 32-game mini-match itself; predicted the weaker explanation. (ii) *travel*: the gain schedule ran out before trending coordinates arrived (RAR-S76's +119 Elo says the seeds were far from the optimum). (iii) *coupling*: correlated coordinates move slowly along a valley (the RFP linear, square and constant terms moved in compensating directions). (iv) already at the optimum within noise. **Surface rule, fixed now:** every coordinate whose theta_5000 lies at least one RAR-S75 step from its seed (17 at 3,900; the count at 5,000 is what it is), seeded at the rounded theta_5000; all others fixed at theta_5000. Steps are sized so one perturbation is detectable rather than range/16, by a method stated at full registration. **Discriminating reads:** coordinates that keep moving in their RAR-S75 direction through the first third support (ii); oscillation around theta_5000 supports (iv); pairs moving together along a line support (iii); (i) predicts a faster, cleaner trend than the same coordinates had in RAR-S75 at comparable gain, the one read that separates it from (ii). **Gate:** an SPRT of the re-tuned arm against the head B.2.4b accepts, `[0,3]` nElo, cap from RAR-M10 at full registration. **Prediction, frozen here:** +3 ± 5 nElo, low confidence, (ii) the main mechanism. ## Conditional lesson

**Calibration:** gate predicted +4 ± 5 nElo (H1 0.5); read +20.9 ± 8.7 nElo, a magnitude miss on the good side, outside the band. The failed assumption was 'near the optimum': the 41-of-82 read already said the surface was still moving, and the Elo agrees. Mechanism: coupling (iii) rather than travel (ii), since the gain came with the coordinates B.2.3 had left near their seeds (LMR TT-shallow, exact-bound, alpha-gap, child-cutoffs, TT-PV terms) moving a full step together, not with the 17 movers travelling further. Instrument: 150,000 games moved ten coordinates a step and bought about +13 Elo, so the horizon was not the limit; the seeds were. Earlier reads: rails 0.9 → none, hit; 'of the 65 at most 10 move a full step' 0.6 → 9, hit; 'at least 60 of 82 within half a step' 0.4 → 41, missed: the surface moved more than an at-optimum surface would, mostly along LMR terms that RAR-S75 had left near their seeds, which favours coupling (iii) over optimum (iv); travel (ii): read at N, 8 of 17 continuing; the registered first-third read was taken later the same day from `spsa history` of `cli-v0.2.0` (rows 0..5,000 of the journal; row 5,000 rounds to `tuned-options.txt` on all 82): at iteration 1,667 only **4 of 17** had continued half a step in their RAR-S75 direction (`CoreLmrQuiet` −0.81, `CoreLmrCutNode` +0.81, `CoreLmpSquare` −0.74, `CoreLmrNoisy` −0.54), and of the three big movers only `CoreLmpSquare`, so the 0.55 prediction **missed**: travel was not the mechanism; coupling was.
