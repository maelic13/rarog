- **Hypothesis:** valuing a knight on the pawn's front square (and, more weakly, a knight that can reach it next move) makes the search hold the blockade while the king and the free knight drive, and establish it when it is the winning move; that converts more of RAR-E51's wins than `5e29043` and clearly more than the head.
- **Competing explanations:** (1) *the drive is the limit, not the blockade:* with the blockade held, the king and one knight cannot corner the defending king, so ply-limit failures with the blockade intact rise and wins do not. (2) *the release is the limit:* the engine holds the blockade when the mate needs that knight; the sign is ply-limit failures with the blockade intact and the defending king at Manhattan corner distance 0 or 1. (3) *the only-moves at ply 0 to 2 are out of reach:* the knight is two or three moves from the front square and a one-move gradient does not help; the `REACH` axis of the grid tests this cheaply.
- **Mechanism:** for exact K+N+N against K+P, from the knights' side, `scale_endgame` returns the generic score plus `5e29043`'s adjustment (`CORNER` 60 per step of the defending king's Manhattan distance to the nearest corner, `KINGS` 10 per step of the kings' Chebyshev distance, `ADVANCE` 10 per rank of the pawn's advance) plus `HOLD` when a knight of the strong side stands on the square in front of the pawn, else `REACH` when a knight attacks that square (so it can occupy it next move), else 0. The term is non-negative. No base, no replacement; the texel delta scale stays 1; nothing else changes.
- **Interactions:** the corner drive (a release must gain more than `HOLD` in drive steps), the mop-up edge push (same direction, mild), KN-KP and KNN-K one capture away (a non-negative term cannot push KNN-KP below a neighbour the generic score keeps it above, but the test runs), mate scores (they dominate the term, so the final release is found when the mate is inside the horizon), the drawn-KNN-KP overclaim (rises; reported), rule 50 in real play (a held blockade can shuffle; Syzygy decides those positions when loaded, and the 100-ply limit bounds it here). `bench 13` has no KNN-KP position.
- **Invariants and tests:** fires only on exact material, either colour; colour-mirrored positions adjust by the negated amount; the adjustment's extreme stays far below the decisive band (compile-time assertion, now including `HOLD`); direction tests (corner, pawn held back, blockaded above reachable above neither); the **one-capture-neighbour test** of RAR-E54 unchanged; the eight Syzygy-verified KNN-KP vetoes of `5e29043` restored in `tests/endgames.epd`.
