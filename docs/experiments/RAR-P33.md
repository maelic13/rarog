## Conditional lesson and retry trigger

NPS only, no Elo. **Calibration:** a sign miss, outside +0.8% to +2.0%. The board-code read located samples, not a step's cost: the rewrite moves work to the start of every exchange (both colours' attackers, the slider sets, each king's rays and sliders), and its savings arrive only at later recapture steps, which most calls never reach. That premise, about exchange length, was never measured. **Retry trigger:** a counter read of recapture steps per SEE call showing most calls reaching two or more, and then only in a form whose first step costs no more than today's. **Verified by the auditor 2026-10-03, from the artifacts:** registration (`523d9d2`, 07:47:48) precedes the pool (built 07:50:07) and the read; the pool's source and the engine commit have identical engine sources (0-byte diff), the revert returns the engine source to `a42fadc` byte for byte, and `nps_read.py --report` reproduces −2.09% (−2.34 .. −1.84) with no disturbed cycle; the 26% CPU snapshot before cycle 1 is the tool's sample taken as the warm-up readings ended, and both cycles agree. The rule was applied as written. **The retry trigger was then read and did not fire:** 62.5% of 30.1M recapturer calls are an exchange's first step, 31.6% the second, 6.0% later; a lazy form's ceiling is near +0.8%, under the floor once halved. `verify_normalized_see.py`'s failure on the 2026-09-07 bundle's `build-basilisk.log` hash reproduces on the head and predates this leaf (the tool last changed at `4a738d2`); recorded for B.8.
