## Experiment and conditions

**Comparative claim SUPERSEDED by RAR-M49, 2026-09-13: the 57/12-against-40/12 surplus over Basilisk is not reproduced on the release games (107 against 106 thrown away in 3,600); Rarog's own rate is, and it remains the C.5 figure.** **A.5 conversion baseline, instrument built and baseline recorded, 2026-09-10.** Rarog throws away won games: over 2,400 games against the six HCE-era engines it drew 57 and lost 12 after holding at least a minor piece for 12 consecutive plies, while **Basilisk 1.9.3 - a weaker engine - threw away 40 and 12**. That comparison is what makes this a defect rather than a consequence of being weaker, and it is the measurement the C.5 conversion cluster is aimed at. **This row exists because the figure had none.** Until now 57/12 appeared only in GUIDE and PLAN prose, produced by a 69-line ad-hoc script in an ignored directory reading a 117 MB Colosseum SQLite that will not outlive the measurement - the RAR-S54 shape, a cited result whose evidence someone else is storing. **Instrument:** `tools/diag/conversion_audit.py`, PGN in and a frozen JSON summary out, with no knowledge of any harness's database; `tools/diag/export_tournament_pgn.py` is the only tool that knows Colosseum's schema, so when that program changes or disappears only the exporter breaks and the archives it has already written stay readable. **Evidence:** Colosseum tournament `41768fe9-fa35-43fc-99c8-ab42360e7361` exported to 36,400 games, 37.8 MB, **sha256 `c1335a6c1dfe7b32c869e90937a75c604db8e506eed10333c8675499b85cb00a`**, held in ignored `analysis/artifacts/conversion-41768fe9.pgn`; the tracked summaries are `tools/diag/conversion_baseline_v1.json` and `conversion_baseline_basilisk_v1.json`, 0.9 KB each, and each cites the archive by that hash rather than by path. Parameters: 12 plies, advantage >= 3, endgame at non-pawn material <= 26, lone-minor exclusion on.

## Result / disposition

**FIDELITY PROVEN TWICE BEFORE THE NEW NUMBERS WERE TRUSTED.** The seed had no lone-minor exclusion, so `--no-exclude-lone-minor` reproduces its arithmetic exactly, and it does: Rarog **57 / 12 with 52 of the draws in an endgame phase**, Basilisk **40 / 12** - every figure identical to the recorded baseline. **The lone-minor guard is proven LIVE rather than assumed.** Enabling it moved no total, which is precisely the shape of a guard that is dead code, so the tool counts its own suppressions: it fired **once** for Rarog and **twice** for Basilisk and **excused nothing**, because those games had already qualified earlier on other material. The corrected figure therefore equals the seed figure at 57/12, and the reason is a measurement, not an argument: **none of the 57 draws are excused by dead-drawn material; all are genuine failures to convert.** **The breakdown the seed could not give:** of Rarog's 57 draws, **35 are FiftyMove** and 15 Threefold, 4 Stalemate, 3 InsufficientMaterial; Basilisk's 40 are 22, 9, 4 and 5. Rarog also saved 28 draws and won 17 from a persistent deficit, against Basilisk's 32 and 12.

## Conditional lesson and retry trigger

**Read the excess where it actually is.** Rarog's surplus over a weaker engine sits almost entirely in **fifty-move and threefold: 50 against 31**, while stalemates are level at 4 apiece. That is failure to make progress while holding material, which points at C.5.2 (generic winnability and scaling) rather than C.5.3 (mating technique). Treat this as a direction for the cluster, not as its acceptance criterion. **This instrument accepts nothing.** It is a diagnostic layer re-run at every programme checkpoint; only a registered SPRT accepts a conversion change, and a improved count here is not Elo. **Ceiling, so the prize is not overstated:** perfect conversion of all 57 draws and 12 losses would be worth roughly 8 and 3.5 Elo respectively over this population - order-of-10, consistent with PLAN's evaluation budget listing endgame conversion among six families sharing 100-160 Elo. Perfect conversion is not available. **Storage rule this row establishes for game evidence:** export once to PGN, keep the archive in ignored storage, cite it by sha256, and track only the tool and a small frozen summary. No games in Git, ever. **Retry trigger:** re-run at each B and C checkpoint against a fresh tournament, comparing frozen summary to frozen summary - never by re-reading an old database, which may no longer exist.
