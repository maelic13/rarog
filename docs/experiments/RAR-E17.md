# RAR-E17 — C.0 donor-direction residual screen: what the classical Stockfish evaluation predicts that Rarog's does not

Indexed under *5. Evaluation and data experiments* in [`docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`](../EXPERIMENTS.md).

- **Date / owner:** registered 2026-10-05, before the corpus named below was scored; agent-run (zero games, about five minutes of evaluation calls).

- **Research question:** on positions with known outcomes, does the classical Stockfish evaluation carry outcome information that Rarog's evaluation lacks, how much, and in which of Stockfish's term families? RAR-E09 found no structural residual on Rarog's own cohorts and said that telling "harder" from "wrongly modelled" would need an oracle's loss on the same positions, which did not exist. This is that instrument.
- **Hypothesis / proposed mechanism:** PLAN's Phase C premise is that Stockfish's families condition the same concepts more strongly (king, material, pawn, attacker-victim and conversion context). If so, a Stockfish family's score is a direction outside the span of Rarog's fitted features, and adding it to Rarog's score lowers held-out outcome loss. A fitted evaluation's own features cannot show this: the fit's first-order condition makes them uncorrelated with its residual.
- **Competing hypotheses:** (1) *Calibration, not structure:* Rarog's surface already predicts outcomes as well as Stockfish's and the same-search gap (RAR-O02) is not visible statically; then the donor adds little and the gap lives in search interaction, score scale or the instrument (RAR-O05 measures those). (2) *Label ceiling:* 8,000-node self-play outcomes from the pre-B search are too noisy, or too much Rarog's own policy, for any static score to add to another. (3) *Collinearity:* the donor's information is real but spread across families so evenly that no family separates.
- **Interacting mechanisms / consumers:** none in the engine. The reading feeds C.0's cluster order and the first cluster's research card; it interacts with RAR-O05, which prices how much of the 329-Elo figure is evaluation quality at all.
- **PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION (freeze before exposure):**
  - Expected diagnostic movement, cohort `all`, gains as percent of Rarog's held-out mean squared error:
    1. `stockfish` alone is **worse** than `rarog` alone, by 1% to 6% (probability 0.75 that it is worse): Rarog was fitted on this distribution and Stockfish never was.
    2. `rarog+stockfish` gains **+1.0%**, 80% band [+0.3%, +2.5%].
    3. `rarog+all_families` gains **+1.5%**, 80% band [+0.5%, +3.5%], and more than `rarog+stockfish` (probability 0.8).
    4. Largest single family: king 0.35, mobility 0.20, threats 0.15, passed 0.10, material 0.10, any other 0.10.
    5. `rarog+shuffled_king` (the null control) moves less than 0.02% either way.
    6. `rarog+phase_slope` gains under 0.1% (probability 0.7): scale across the game phase is already fitted.
    7. The `rarog+all_families` gain is larger with queens on the board than without (probability 0.6).
  - Expected Elo sign/range, if defensible: none. Loss has no exchange rate to Elo here (RAR-E03 lost 17 Elo at 4.9% lower loss; RAR-E06 gained 22 at 0.63%).
  - Probability positive/useful and confidence basis: 0.6 that at least one family separates from the rest by more than its error. Low confidence: no earlier reading of this kind exists in this project.
  - Most likely failure mode: gains of order 0.1% for every family, inside each other's error, so the screen orders nothing.
- **Falsification criteria:** the Phase C premise is contradicted *at the static layer* if `rarog+all_families` gains less than 0.3%. A family "has signal" only at a gain of at least 0.10% and at least four standard errors (four, not two: rows from one game share a label, so the per-row error is understated). The instrument itself is void if the null control exceeds 0.05%, if the rank correlation between the oracle's Stockfish total and Stockfish 11's total is below 0.95, or if any row fails to score.
- **Cheapest prior falsifier:** RAR-E09's own-cohort residuals (king-attack +3.8%, threat +3.1%, passer −6.1% against global loss) licensed no structure but could not see outside Rarog's span. Implementation of a family cluster is **not** justified by this screen alone whatever it reads.
- **Registered gate and stop rule:** no gate; one run on the whole corpus named below, no subsetting or re-running with other settings after the numbers are seen. Use of the reading, frozen: families with signal are investigated first, in order of gain; families whose gains are within two standard errors of each other keep PLAN's order (king safety, threats and mobility, endgame scaling, pawns and passers, material and pieces); with no family carrying signal the screen sets no order and C.0 says so. It accepts nothing and estimates no Elo.

- **Disposition:** observation. Two families carry signal by the registered rule (at least 0.10% and four standard errors): **king** (+0.661%, 16 SE) and **initiative** (+0.119%, 9 SE). Threats, passed and pawns are positive at four to five standard errors but under 0.10%; mobility, pieces, material and space show nothing. The static-layer falsifier did not fire (`rarog+all_families` +1.218% against the 0.3% line). By the frozen use: king safety is investigated first, the winnability term second, and the rest keep PLAN's order. Nothing is accepted.
- **PREDICTION CALIBRATION (appended after exposure):**
  - Original prediction (not rewritten): items 1 to 7 above.
  - Observed: (1) **miss in sign** — Stockfish's total alone predicts Rarog's own self-play outcomes *better* than Rarog's fitted evaluation, by 2.17%; I gave 0.75 to the opposite. (2) **miss in magnitude** — +3.59% against a band topping at +2.5%. (3) the level hit (+1.22% inside [0.5, 3.5]) and the comparison **missed**: the nine families together carry a third of what the total carries, where I expected more than the total. (4) hit: king, by a factor of five over the next family. (5) hit. (6) **miss**: the phase slope gains +0.29%, and with opposite signs by band (+1.07% at phase ≥ 96, −0.75% below 32), so Rarog's scale against outcome is not uniform across the game. (7) miss, inside the error (+1.18% with queens, +1.31% without).
  - Mechanism supported? The premise that the donor conditions king safety more strongly is supported at this layer. The failed assumption behind (1) to (3): that a fit on this distribution leaves nothing for an unfitted evaluation to add at the level of the total. Most of what Stockfish adds is not in its regular term families, so the trace-based family split cannot attribute it; RAR-E18 was registered to cut it.
  - Missed interaction or instrument limit: the single global fit lets the endgame half of the rows set the family weights for the other bands (the cohort lines above understate the families outside the endgame); Rarog's score is the *played* evaluation, which takes the lazy path above its 600 cp gate and omits imbalance there (found the same day by the donor read; it bears on rows with large scores only).
  - Confidence: over-confident on (1) and (3); the 0.6 chance that a family would separate was under-confident.
- **Conditional lesson:** a held-out comparison against a second evaluator sees what a fit's own cohort residuals cannot. On this corpus the donor's edge is mostly at the level of its total (scaling, exact endgames, magnitude), with king safety the one regular family that stands out; "feature-list" families (mobility, pieces, material, space) add nothing measurable to a fitted surface. Static loss still ranks questions only.
