This run did not record NPS; the 1.80x figure is the prior, not a measurement of this run. **Calibration, appended after exposure; the prediction is not rewritten.** **The band was hit, and the hit must not be read as confirmation of the reasoning behind it.** The prediction was built as 'RAR-S70-era 250.8 less the board cluster and the NPS gains, about +12 and +5'. **That subtraction differenced two different instruments.** The 250.77 +/- 13.12 prior was measured **with RAR-M13 adjudication** (`analysis/ablation_results.md` states it explicitly: 'a direct, adjudicated, 2,000-game measurement'), while this row registered and ran **no adjudication**. The project has already priced that difference: RAR-O01 against RAR-O02 moved the closely related hybrid-vs-Rarog comparison by about **74 Elo** (roughly 270.5 adjudicated against 196.5 un-adjudicated), albeit from runs whose intervals were on the order of +/-40. **So the natural headline - '250.8 to 247.97, the deficit is essentially unchanged' - is NOT a valid inference and must not be written**; it differences an adjudicated number against an un-adjudicated one. The band-hit is therefore partly coincidental: the prediction's arithmetic was performed in the wrong unit and happened to land inside its own tolerance. **What this run DOES establish is a new baseline, and a better one than either predecessor.** It is the first G(0) that is simultaneously well-powered (+/-10.89 over 3,000 games, against +/-13.12 and +/-40), un-adjudicated, measured on the released binary, and **proved to hold the evaluation constant** - the three checks recorded in the preparation above (FFI parity test, the 0.06-against-1.47 evaluator discrimination, and the exit-1 DLL binding) have no equivalent in the earlier numbers. **B.9 re-measures against -247.97 +/- 10.89 under exactly these conditions, not against 250.8.** **The depth result corroborates the matched ablation independently.** That work attributed 272.2 +/- 17.9 Elo to LMR plus shallow-depth pruning; a 0.97-ply gap carrying a 248-Elo deficit says the same thing from a different direction - the oracle is not out-searching Rarog, it is out-DECIDING it at nearly the same depth. That is the clearest single-number statement of what Phase B has to fix, and it is consistent with RAR-M46's finding that Rarog converts threads into quality that neither NPS nor depth predicts. **CORRECTION 2026-10-03 (B.9 preparation): the depth figures above are wrong, and the 0.97-ply gap is withdrawn.** The recorded 19.68 / 20.65 over 186,172 / 186,266 moves reproduces exactly (same counts, same means) only when every game's annotations are credited as if White moved first; **1,304 of the 3,000 UHO starts have Black to move**, so those games' moves were credited to the wrong engine, and the means also averaged in mate scores, whose depth is a pseudo-depth (the oracle reports 245). The committed reader, `tools/pgn_depth_at_nodes.py` (side to move from the FEN; non-mate columns since `ebbfc80`, with a test that fails on White-first attribution), reads this PGN as **Rarog 14.89 mean / 14 median over 174,897 non-mate moves, oracle 16.21 / 15 over 169,888**: a gap of **1.32 plies by the mean, 1 by the median** (all moves: 15.85 against 21.84, mate-inflated).
