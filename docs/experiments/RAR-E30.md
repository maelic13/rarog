- **Research question:** PLAN C.4 opens with its own residual step and closes `NO_CHANGE` if that step finds none. On the current evaluation (the `hce-v4-tb` refit, the king unit and the opposite-bishop rule since the last reading), do Stockfish's threats and mobility families still carry outcome information Rarog's played score lacks, by RAR-E17's rule (a gain of at least 0.10% of Rarog's held-out squared error at four standard errors or more), and in which cohorts?
- **Hypothesis / proposed mechanism:** the threats family carries a small residual that survives the refit, because the donor conditions its threat terms on what Rarog's do not read (the *weak* and *strongly protected* sets gating minor and rook threats, safe-pawn and safe-push attackers, king threats, weak queen protection, knight and slider attacks on the queen), while mobility's earlier residual was mostly table shape and fell with the refit, leaving a signal only in the opening band.
- **Competing hypotheses:** (1) *both nil:* the refit absorbed both families and neither reaches 0.10% in any cohort; C.4 closes `NO_CHANGE`. (2) *mobility, not threats:* the opening-band mobility signal is the larger and threats stays under the line, so the step points at the mobility area, not the threat conditioning. (3) *instrument:* the substituted score is not the engine's played score (unit, point of view or lazy-path handling), which the wire checks below catch.
- **Known exposure:** C.3's pre-game donor screen (RAR-E25, `analysis/artifacts/c3-screens/analyse-cand.log`) read the C.3 candidate, which differs from this head only by the opposite-bishop rule (pure endings of three pawns or more, about 1.8% of the rows): all rows mobility +0.055 ± 0.013, threats +0.109 ± 0.020; seven men or more mobility +0.069 ± 0.017, threats +0.125 ± 0.021; phase ≥ 96 mobility +0.185 ± 0.044, threats +0.138 ± 0.038; no queens threats +0.203 ± 0.043. RAR-E23 read the pre-refit evaluation at seven men or more: mobility +0.206, threats +0.144. The predictions below are informed by both; what this registration freezes is the reading on the accepted head and its use.
- **Interacting mechanisms / consumers:** none in the engine. The reading decides whether C.4 continues (sub-term attribution of the threats and mobility families, then a research card and handoff) or closes `NO_CHANGE`; it bears on C.6 and C.7 only through the shared attack maps a threats unit would read.

  1. Threats, all rows: **+0.11%**, 80% band [+0.07, +0.15]; at least 0.10% at four standard errors (probability 0.6). Seven men or more: +0.12%, band [+0.08, +0.17]; signal by the rule (probability 0.7).
  2. Mobility, all rows: **+0.05%**, band [+0.02, +0.09]; under 0.10% (probability 0.85). Phase ≥ 96: +0.18%, band [+0.10, +0.26]; signal by the rule there (probability 0.7).

- **Falsification criteria:** a family *has signal* only at a gain of at least 0.10% and at least four standard errors in a cohort of the registered six (all, the three phase bands, seven men or more, no queens). The instrument is void if the substituted file does not hold exactly 194,444 rows each joined by FEN with its label unchanged (`substitute.py` fails on an unjoined FEN; the join and label equality were checked before this registration: 194,444 of 194,444, rank correlation 0.985 with the pre-refit column, 0.985 white and 0.985 black to move), if the report's `rank_corr_control_vs_sf11_total` is under 0.99, or if `rank_corr_rarog_vs_control` is under 0.90.
- **Cheapest prior falsifier:** the C.3 candidate's screen above, which is the same instrument on a function one rule away.
- **Registered gate and stop rule:** no gate; one `substitute.py` run and one `analyse --within` run, no cohort or model added after the numbers are seen. **Use, frozen:**
  (i) A family with signal in any registered cohort keeps C.4 open for that family: the next step is a zero-game sub-term attribution (the donor's family rebuilt from its components offline and verified against its printed row, then leave-one-out as RAR-E21 did for king), registered separately before it is computed, and the research card decides readiness from it.
  (ii) A family with no signal in any registered cohort closes `NO_CHANGE` for that family inside C.4, with no game-level defect on record to override it.
  (iii) Both without signal: C.4 closes `NO_CHANGE` on this entry.

- **PREDICTION CALIBRATION (appended after exposure):**
  - Original prediction (not rewritten): items 1 to 4 above.
  - Observed: (1) hit on both clauses: +0.107 at 5.4 standard errors over all rows, +0.122 at 6.1 at seven men or more. (2) hit on both clauses: +0.048 over all rows, +0.184 at 4.2 standard errors in the opening band. A miss in mechanism (the family the rule touches), not in sign; the instrument is fine.
- **Conditional lesson:** the residual screen reads an accepted unit's effect as the fall of the donor family it overlaps (king +0.62 → +0.08 across C.3; winnable −0.12 to −0.17 across C.5.2), so the screen is also a cheap check that a unit captured what it was built for.
