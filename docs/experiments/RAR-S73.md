## Conditional lesson

A cluster implemented as one unit found three defects that no single mechanism exposed (the rule-50 table interaction, the skip dropping mating checks, the budget-chaotic anchor), each by a test or an instrument written to catch exactly that class, and one ticket (7) whose seeds alone regressed an endgame the next ticket repaired: intermediate tickets are not candidates. **Review 2026-09-15** (`analysis/b22_review_2026-09-15.md`, separate session): stride-1 counters of both arms show the candidate does less per interior node (moves made 1.46 vs 1.81, check tests 1.50 vs 9.23, quiescence nodes 0.67 vs 1.02, table probes equal) at 1.33x the time per node, so the NPS reading is about 13 points tree mix and about 20 points dearer nodes spread over five or six items; keyed correction tables 16x smaller change NPS by 0.0% (footprint refuted); the 825 cp mean training residual is mate-range scores (61 cp without them) and each of the two donor-faithful admissions the B.1 head refused (decisive residuals, singular-excluded nodes) shrinks the bench tree by 15–21%; the paired PGN puts the gain at 60–140 plies with the candidate throwing away 27 piece-up games against B.1's 54. Kernel re-read against Reckless and Stockfish: identical on every mechanism the results touched; razoring lost B.1's `!tt_pv` and depth ≤ 3 guards, the mechanism the ablation convicts. Recommends: the paired run governs; three categorical 2,000-game runs (razoring guards, decisive residuals, singular-node training; prediction for the guarded razoring +8 ± 11), the three eval-unit clamps converted, the registered curvature sweep, then B.2.3; NPS floor retired as an instrument for tree-shape changes.
