# RAR-O06 — C.3.6 same-search evaluation meter on the accepted C.3 evaluation: equal nodes

Indexed under *3. Search and selectivity › Search-oracle observations* in [`docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`](../EXPERIMENTS.md).

- **Date / owner:** registered 2026-10-09, before any game; maintainer-run (one fixed match, about 12 minutes).
- **Baseline SHA / candidate SHA / dirty-diff hash:** the evaluation of `master` at `cc13320` ("Rebuild king safety", fingerprint 13,187,295 / EBF 2.546): RAR-E24's refit and RAR-E25's king-safety unit with their margins. No candidate: both arms are one oracle package.
- **Binary / compiler / PGO identity:** `tools/test_engines/oracle-hybrid-c3/rarog-stockfish-hce-hybrid.exe`, SHA-256 `42ABBDCA38A4B1983FBFB5D589C7AAAA299EC4F611D4CCA207D58DF2FBFA41ED`, byte-identical to RAR-O05's executable, with `rarog_hce.dll` SHA-256 `DEEFAD1F108BFE520F70C1E0B2FAC05F9EE99C8CF89B1D56D44C1F59880F8300`, built by `tools/oracle-hce-ffi` against `cc13320`, `rustc 1.98.1`. The adapter equals Rarog's evaluator exactly (its tests: the tag's four positions and over 4,000 positions from random games). Wire check, `bench 16 1 8 default depth`: `Use Rarog HCE=false` 322,185 nodes on both RAR-O05's and this package (the same search), `Use Rarog HCE=true` 277,221 with RAR-O05's 2.4.0 DLL and **272,681** with this one (the C.3 evaluation is live). One binary on both sides: arm `sf-hce-c3` with `Use Rarog HCE=false` (Stockfish `9587eeeb`'s search and classical evaluation), arm `rarog-hce-c3` with `Use Rarog HCE=true` (the same search calling Rarog's C.3 evaluation).
- **Research question:** how much of RAR-O05's equal-node gap (+181.7 ± 19.0 Elo, Rarog's 2.4.0 evaluation) do the evaluation's accepted changes since then close inside the same search, and what does that say the programme can still recover (PLAN C.3.6)?
- **Hypothesis / proposed mechanism:** two accepted evaluation changes separate this DLL from RAR-O05's: the refit on `hce-v4-tb` (RAR-E24, +31.0 ± 8.5 Elo at gate 1 in Rarog's own search) and the king-safety unit (RAR-E25, +34.2 ± 8.9 at gate 1). Part of their gain transfers to Stockfish's search; the king unit, built in the donor's shape, transfers better than a refit. The gap shrinks by about 35 Elo.
- **Competing hypotheses:** (1) the gains are mostly a fit to Rarog's own search and barely transfer: the gap stays within RAR-O05's interval (above about +163). (2) The gains transfer in full or more (the donor-shaped term suits a search tuned on the donor's evaluation): the gap falls under +120.
- **Interacting mechanisms / consumers:** GUIDE's checkpoint ("Evaluation deficit"), PLAN's Phase C budget row and C.0's prior, the decision whether C.4 to C.7 open, C.11's re-measurement. Not separated here, as in RAR-O05: how much of any equal-node gap is Stockfish's search being fitted to its own evaluation's scale and shape.
- **PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION (freeze before exposure):** Elo of `sf-hce-c3` over `rarog-hce-c3`, equal nodes.
  - Expected diagnostic movement: **+145**, 80% band [+110, +175]; against RAR-O05, a gap smaller by about 35 (band 5 to 70).
  - Expected Elo sign/range: as above; an observation of an instrument, not a strength claim for Rarog.
  - Probability positive/useful and confidence basis: probability the gap is under +150: 0.55; under +100: 0.05; at or above +182 (no change or worse): 0.08. Basis: RAR-O05's +181.7; the two gate-1 results (+31.0 and +34.2, early-stopped SPRTs that overstate) as an upper guide; a transfer of 0.4 to 0.9 into a search fitted to another evaluation.
  - Most likely failure mode: reading the change as Elo Rarog gained. Rarog's own gates measured that; this reads the evaluation inside a fixed foreign search.
- **Falsification criteria:** hypothesis (1) (little transfer) holds if the gap is +163 or more; hypothesis (2) (full transfer) if under +120. Both are reported against the prediction; neither changes the use below.
- **Cheapest prior falsifier:** the wire check above (done: the DLL moves the search's node counts and nothing else).
- **Registered gate and stop rule:** no gate. One fixed match of 1,000 games, no early stop.
- **Use of the reading, frozen:** the equal-node figure replaces RAR-O05's as Phase C's current meter in GUIDE's checkpoint, with RAR-O05's beside it, and corrects PLAN's budget row in proportion. At **+100 or more**, C.4 opens as PLAN has it, with its own residual step first, and C.5 to C.7 keep their order. **Under +100**, the remaining clusters are reviewed with the maintainer before C.4 opens. The difference to RAR-O05 is reported with its interval (two independent 1,000-game samples on the same openings).
- **Full conditions / provenance:** RAR-O05's equal-node conditions, the runner kept for comparability (Colosseum is the main harness, but a re-read on a different runner would add an unmeasured difference): `tools/sprt.ps1 -Mode fixed -Games 1000 -Nodes 150000 -NoAdjudication -Seed 20261005`, `-EngineA` and `-EngineB` the package executable, `-NameA sf-hce-c3 -NameB rarog-hce-c3`, `-OptionsA "Use Rarog HCE=false" -OptionsB "Use Rarog HCE=true"`; 1T, Hash 64, concurrency 14 with affinity, UHO_Lichess_4852_v1 in random order at the fixed seed (RAR-O05's seed, so the same openings), fastchess, no adjudication. Driver: `analysis/artifacts/c36-meter/run.ps1` (SHA-256 `b11a3c3b…6cda`, ignored storage), which checks both binaries' SHA-256, refuses an existing result, waits for an idle host and logs to `run.log` beside it.
- **Delivery:** this record and its result reach `master` with C.4's PR, or with the next PR if C.4 does not open (maintainer decision 2026-10-09).
- **Result (run 2026-10-09 18:02–18:16 by the driver, exit 0; one idle check at 1.4% CPU; an earlier start at 16:21 found the host busy for 30 minutes and ran nothing):** `sf-hce-c3` over `rarog-hce-c3` **+129.35 ± 17.11 Elo** (nElo +178.43 ± 21.53), 1,000 games, W/L/D 522/166/312 (67.8%), draw ratio 28.8%, pentanomial [10, 43, 144, 187, 116]. Checked from the PGN, not the summary: 1,000 results recounting to 522/166/312, 500 games each colour, every termination natural; the manifest records both options, the book's SHA-256, seed 20261005 and the fourteen-core affinity; the 1,000 opening FENs are the same multiset as RAR-O05's equal-node match; the executable, DLL and driver hashes equal the registration's. Against RAR-O05's +181.70 ± 18.99 (W/L/D 610/130/260, draw ratio 22.8%): **52.4 ± 25.6 Elo smaller** (nElo 67.7 ± 30.4), the two intervals combined as independent; the shared openings correlate the samples positively, so that interval is if anything wide.
- **Disposition:** observation, recorded. +129.4 replaces +181.7 as Phase C's current same-search meter (GUIDE's checkpoint, with RAR-O05 beside it) and PLAN's budget row is corrected to it. Hypothesis (1), little transfer, is refuted: the interval's top, +146.5, is below +163. Hypothesis (2), full transfer, is not met by the point estimate and not excluded: +120 lies inside the interval. The reading is at or above +100, so by the frozen rule C.4 opens as PLAN has it (after C.5.2 in the register, with its own residual step first) and C.5 to C.7 keep their order. It does not separate the refit from the king unit, and it says nothing of the equal-time gap, which was not re-read. Not Elo Rarog gained: Rarog's own gates measured that.
- **PREDICTION CALIBRATION (append after exposure):** predicted +145, 80% band [+110, +175]; observed +129.4, inside the band, 16 below its centre. Predicted movement about 35 (band 5 to 70); observed 52.4, inside. Of the stated probabilities, under +150 (0.55) happened, under +100 (0.05) and +182 or more (0.08) did not. The miss is in magnitude, toward more transfer: 52.4 is 0.80 of the two gate-1 results' sum (65.2) and 0.76 of the gate-2 sum (68.6), at the top of the assumed 0.4 to 0.9, with a standard error near 0.2 of that sum. The assumption that early-stopped gates overstate what transfers is not supported at this resolution; the donor-shaped term transferring better than a refit is not tested, since one reading cannot split them.
