- **Research question:** how much of RAR-O05's equal-node gap (+181.7 ± 19.0 Elo, Rarog's 2.4.0 evaluation) do the evaluation's accepted changes since then close inside the same search, and what does that say the programme can still recover (PLAN C.3.6)?
- **Hypothesis / proposed mechanism:** two accepted evaluation changes separate this DLL from RAR-O05's: the refit on `hce-v4-tb` (RAR-E24, +31.0 ± 8.5 Elo at gate 1 in Rarog's own search) and the king-safety unit (RAR-E25, +34.2 ± 8.9 at gate 1). Part of their gain transfers to Stockfish's search; the king unit, built in the donor's shape, transfers better than a refit. The gap shrinks by about 35 Elo.
- **Competing hypotheses:** (1) the gains are mostly a fit to Rarog's own search and barely transfer: the gap stays within RAR-O05's interval (above about +163). (2) The gains transfer in full or more (the donor-shaped term suits a search tuned on the donor's evaluation): the gap falls under +120.
- **Interacting mechanisms / consumers:** GUIDE's checkpoint ("Evaluation deficit"), PLAN's Phase C budget row and C.0's prior, the decision whether C.4 to C.7 open, C.11's re-measurement. Not separated here, as in RAR-O05: how much of any equal-node gap is Stockfish's search being fitted to its own evaluation's scale and shape.
- **PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION (freeze before exposure):** Elo of `sf-hce-c3` over `rarog-hce-c3`, equal nodes.
  - Expected diagnostic movement: **+145**, 80% band [+110, +175]; against RAR-O05, a gap smaller by about 35 (band 5 to 70).
  - Expected Elo sign/range: as above; an observation of an instrument, not a strength claim for Rarog.
  - Probability positive/useful and confidence basis: probability the gap is under +150: 0.55; under +100: 0.05; at or above +182 (no change or worse): 0.08. Basis: RAR-O05's +181.7; the two gate-1 results (+31.0 and +34.2, early-stopped SPRTs that overstate) as an upper guide; a transfer of 0.4 to 0.9 into a search fitted to another evaluation.
  - Most likely failure mode: reading the change as Elo Rarog gained. Rarog's own gates measured that; this reads the evaluation inside a fixed foreign search.
- **Falsification criteria:** hypothesis (1) (little transfer) holds if the gap is +163 or more; hypothesis (2) (full transfer) if under +120. Both are reported against the prediction; neither changes the use below.
- **Cheapest prior falsifier:** the wire check above (done: the DLL moves the search's node counts and nothing else).
- **Registered gate and stop rule:** no gate. One fixed match of 1,000 games, no early stop.
- **Use of the reading, frozen:** the equal-node figure replaces RAR-O05's as Phase C's current meter in GUIDE's checkpoint, with RAR-O05's beside it, and corrects PLAN's budget row in proportion. At **+100 or more**, C.4 opens as PLAN has it, with its own residual step first, and C.5 to C.7 keep their order. **Under +100**, the remaining clusters are reviewed with the maintainer before C.4 opens. The difference to RAR-O05 is reported with its interval (two independent 1,000-game samples on the same openings).
