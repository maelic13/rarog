- **Research question:** RAR-E26's candidate cuts the drawn opposite-bishop positions a static score overclaims, but it lowers the static score of true wins: KBP-KB wins scored under +100 cp rose from 12 to 75 of 253. Does that cost wins when the engine actually plays them, or does the search still find the promotion?
- **Hypothesis / proposed mechanism:** a scaled-down win keeps its sign and its ordering of moves, and a promotion lifts the score out of the scaled class (to K+Q+B against K+B, which the rule no longer touches), so a 60,000-node search still finds it. Conversion of Syzygy-won opposite-bishop positions holds.
- **Competing hypotheses:** (1) *flattened gradient:* with `s` as low as 12/48 the differences between the strong side's moves shrink below the search's pruning margins, so it drifts in long wins and conversion falls, most in KBP-KB. (2) *draw-seeking:* at +50 cp, a repetition or a simplification scored 0 looks almost as good, and the engine takes it. (3) *neutral where it matters:* KBPP-KB keeps its wins because two passers lift `s` to 24 or more.
- **Interacting mechanisms / consumers:** the search's pruning margins and its rule-50 damping, both consuming the scaled score; the eval cache and TT (the scale is a pure function of the position); `tests/endgames.rs` vetoes. Consumer: C.5.2's build decision.
- **PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION (freeze before exposure):** cohort: `endgame_truth.py`'s generator at seed `0x5E9D18`, 2,000 positions per family (the positions RAR-E26's static read used), kept where the bishops stand on opposite colours (read from each per-position FEN) and Syzygy says White wins cleanly (WDL 2); expected about 253, 754 and 203 such wins.
  1. Conversion (`mated` inside 100 plies): candidate minus head within two standard errors of the paired difference in each family (probability 0.65); in KBP-KB the point estimate falls by no more than 3 points (probability 0.6).
  2. Win preservation per graded move, opposite-bishop wins pooled over the three families: within 0.005 of the head (probability 0.6).
  3. Search-level drawn overclaim (`endgame_drawn.py`, 2,000 positions per family, 60,000 nodes, +100 cp; both bishop colours, so the change comes from the opposite-bishop subset alone): KBP-KB falls by at least 15 points (probability 0.6).
  - Expected Elo sign/range: none; this is a veto read, not a strength read.
  - Probability positive/useful: 0.6 that the candidate passes the rule below.
  - Most likely failure mode: competing (1) in KBP-KB.
- **Falsification criteria:** void if any arm's `cohort_sha256` differs from the other's in a family, or if the head's run does not read the archived head binary. H1 is refuted if conversion falls by more than two standard errors in any family with at least 25 opposite-bishop clean wins.
- **Cheapest prior falsifier:** RAR-E26's static read (done; it is why this read exists).
- **Registered gate and stop rule:** no gate. One `endgame_truth.py --per-position` run per arm (`--families KBP-KB,KBPP-KB,KBP-KBP --positions 2000 --nodes 60000 --max-plies 100 --seed 0x5E9D18 --workers 14`, the engine's own tablebase use off, as the tool enforces) and one `endgame_drawn.py` run per arm (the same families, `--positions 2000 --nodes 60000 --threshold 100`, default seed, `--workers 14`). Nothing added after the numbers are seen. **Use, frozen (maintainer decision 2026-10-10):** if conversion holds within two standard errors of the paired difference in every family with at least 25 opposite-bishop clean wins, C.5.2 builds the candidate and registers its own gate (an opposite-bishop endgame-start cohort SPRT plus an STC SPRT). Otherwise C.5.2 falls back to the seven-men split: a new registered screen fitting the pure rule on rows of seven men or more, the head's rule kept at six or fewer. Win preservation and drawn overclaim are reported and decide nothing.
- **Full conditions / provenance:** per-position records are paired by `index`; the opposite-bishop test is `tools/diag/ocb_scale_screen.classify` on each record's FEN. The standard error of a paired conversion difference is the standard deviation of the per-position differences (−1, 0, +1) over the square root of their count. The analysis is a short script kept beside the reports; its numbers are recomputable from the two JSON files.
- **Result (run 2026-10-10 10:06–10:16, after the registration commit `0c988fe`; `run.sh`, four exits 0; host idle, CPU 2%, no engine or harness process):** cohort `1475414494ba900e…` in both truth reports. The opposite-bishop clean wins are 253, 754 and 203, the same positions RAR-E26's static read counted. `analyse.json` (`25b33e70…a7da`; reports `truth-head.json` `58750bc2…6d29`, `truth-cand.json` `125c87e7…c0d7`, `drawn-head.json` `39391111…5814`, `drawn-cand.json` `fb63b9e1…bb53`):
  - **Conversion, head → candidate:** KBP-KB **253 → 253 of 253**; **KBPP-KB 732 → 717 of 754, −1.99 ± 0.78 points (−2.55 SE)**, 25 wins lost against 10 gained; KBP-KBP 202 → 203 of 203 (+0.49 ± 0.49).
  - The 25 lost KBPP-KB wins ended at the ply limit (12), in a trade to insufficient material (12) and in stalemate (1). The 10 gained had ended at the ply limit (5) and in insufficient material (5) under the head.
  - **Win preservation**, opposite-bishop wins pooled: 0.99790 (16,229 graded moves) → 0.99713 (16,031).
  - **Search-level drawn overclaim** at +100 cp, both bishop colours: KBP-KB 21.5% → 11.7% of 1,189 draws; KBPP-KB **71.3% → 38.9%** of 293; KBP-KBP 9.6% → 5.8% of 1,254.
- **Disposition:** by the frozen use, **the candidate is not built**. Conversion does not hold within two standard errors in KBPP-KB (754 wins). C.5.2 falls back to the seven-men split: a new registered screen fitting the pure rule on rows of seven men or more, with the head's rule kept at six or fewer. H1 is refuted in KBPP-KB and holds in KBP-KB and KBP-KBP. The static veto pointed at the wrong family: KBP-KB's lowered win scores cost nothing in play, because the search finds the promotion. KBPP-KB's wins, whose static scores barely moved (0 → 10 of 754 under +100 cp), lose games to drift and to simplification. Those are competing (1) and (2), and they fit a factor that flattens the score across the strong side's choices more than it lowers its level. The same candidate removes almost half of KBPP-KB's drawn overclaim, so the trade sits inside one family.
- **PREDICTION CALIBRATION (appended after exposure):**
  - Original prediction (not rewritten): items 1 to 3 above.
  - Observed: (1) first clause **miss** (KBPP-KB at −2.55 SE); second clause hit (KBP-KB unchanged). (2) hit: −0.0008. (3) **miss:** KBP-KB fell 9.8 points, not 15. The head's search-level overclaim there is 21.5%, against the static 72%, so the search already removes most of what the static score overclaims, and there was less to remove.
  - Which assumption failed: I took the static read's family ranking of risk (KBP-KB first) as the search's. The static underclaim measured the score's level; play is lost through its gradient and through simplification choices. I had no instrument for those before the playout.
  - Confidence: about right on the pass probability (0.6, failed by a margin of 0.55 SE). Wrong on where the failure would be.
- **Conditional lesson:** a static underclaim count does not rank a scale change's risk to conversion. The playout read must precede any build decision, including for families where the static read looks safe.
