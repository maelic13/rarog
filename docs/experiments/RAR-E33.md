- **Research question:** the engine's opposite-bishop rule has two regimes. In pure endings of three pawns or more, `s = 1 + 2·pawns + 10·passers` on `/48`. Otherwise it is the old `32 + 4·pawns + 4·passers`. So one exchange across the six-to-seven-men boundary can roughly double the score. RAR-E26's single count rule removed the jump but lost KBPP-KB conversion (RAR-E27), because a passer count cannot tell won from drawn six-man endings. What position knowledge separates them on Syzygy truth? Does one rule built on it, continuous in pawns and the same at every men count, predict outcomes as well as the head above the boundary and better below it, without losing conversion?
- **Hypothesis / proposed mechanism:** opposite-bishop endings are drawn when the defender can stop the passed pawns. The defending bishop covers a square of a passer's path (opposite colours, so it is never blocked by the attacker's bishop), or the defending king stands on or beside the path. A passer *free* of both is the one that wins. Counting free passers instead of all passers lets one rule `s = clamp(a + b·pawns + c·free, 0, 48)` keep the wins (free passers keep `s` high) and scale the draws (blockaded passers, low `s`) at every men count.
- **Competing hypotheses:** (1) *tempo:* control "now" misjudges because the defender's bishop or king reaches the path in a move or two, so static freedom separates little, and the separation read shows it. (2) *bishop only:* the king's part adds nothing beyond the bishop's, and the bishop-free form is as good. (3) *still a trade:* the free rule gains held-out loss below the boundary as the count rule did, but still flattens the wins it should keep, and conversion falls in KBPP-KB as in RAR-E27. (4) *above the boundary:* free passers carry less than the head's count there, and one rule loses at seven men or more what it gains below.
- **Definitions (fixed; `tools/diag/ocb_free_passers.py`, `fe8286c`):** a passed pawn as the engine's (no enemy pawn ahead on its or an adjacent file). Its path is the squares in front of it up to promotion. It is **free** when the defending bishop neither attacks (with the board's occupancy) nor stands on a path square, and the defending king neither stands on nor is next to one. It is **bishop-free** when only the bishop condition holds. Counts are over both sides, as the engine's are. Three forms of `s = clamp(a + b·pawns + c·q, 0, 48)`, with q the passers (*count*), the free passers (*free*) or the bishop-free passers (*bishop_free*). Each is fitted by exhaustive search (`a ∈ 0..48`, `b ∈ 0..12`, `c ∈ 0..24`) on the training dump's pure endings at every men count, then read on validation against the head's own two-regime rule, by region: all, six men or fewer (pawns 1–2, Syzygy-true labels), seven or more.
- **Interacting mechanisms / consumers:** as RAR-E26 and RAR-E28 (the evaluation fitted with the rule fixed; the search's rule-50 damping; the eval cache and TT, which store the scaled score, a pure function of the position). In addition, the boundary incentive the single rule is meant to remove. Consumers: C.5.6 (KBPPKB) and C.5.7 (KBPKB), whose recognisers would sit beside the rule.
- **PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION (freeze before exposure):**
  1. *Separation* (`separation` on the 2,973 generated positions; White has the extra pawn):
     - In KBP-KB, the win rate with the passer free is at least 0.45 and with it not free at most 0.15 (probability 0.6; the family's base rate was 26%).
     - In KBPP-KB, the win rate with two free passers is at least 0.90 and with none at most 0.55 (probability 0.55).
     - In KBPP-KB, pawns three or more files apart win more often than pawns zero or one file apart (probability 0.7).
  2. *Rule fits* (`rule`), gains in percent of the head's held-out squared error in each region:
     - The free form gains at least +15% at six men or fewer (80% band [+8, +45]) and stays within ±2% of the head at seven or more (probability 0.6).
     - The free form beats the count form at six men or fewer (probability 0.55); the bishop-free form lies between the two.
  3. *Static drawn read* (`drawn`, the selected form): in KBPP-KB the wins scored under +100 cp rise by at most 5 points against the head (probability 0.6), and the draws scored above +100 fall by at least 20 points (probability 0.6).
  4. *Conversion*, RAR-E27's instrument on a scratch build of the selected rule against the head: KBP-KB, KBPP-KB and KBP-KBP all within two standard errors (probability 0.5).
  - Expected Elo: none; this is a research read.
  - Most likely failure mode: competing (3) in KBPP-KB.
- **Falsification criteria:** H1 is refuted if neither the free nor the bishop-free form gains at least +5% at three standard errors at six men or fewer, or if the selected form loses KBPP-KB conversion beyond two standard errors.
- **Cheapest prior falsifier:** the separation table (zero games, seconds), then the rule fits.
