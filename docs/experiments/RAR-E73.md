# RAR-E73 — The king-danger lead's fit test: the 23 coordinates refitted alone from the donor's seeds, at caps 1,600 and 2,400

Indexed under *5. Evaluation and data experiments* in [`docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`](../EXPERIMENTS.md).

- **Date / owner:** registered 2026-10-22, before any build or fit; agent-run at the maintainer's request (2026-10-22: "do the read"). Zero games.
- **Kind:** research, the one read PLAN C.11's note (maintainer decision 2026-10-19, on RAR-E72's stage 2) puts before the freeze of the classical evaluation. It changes no source, default or PLAN state by itself.
- **Question:** RAR-E72 found the donor's king signal beyond Rarog's map in the middlegame band to be its danger index's internal weighting: (B) `donor_king | C + rarog_map` **+0.194 ± 0.047%** (4.15 clustered SE) on the held-out rows, carried by safe checks, attackers × weight, unsafe checks and the weak ring, all inputs Rarog's index already has at fitted weights about half the donor's. Is that a fit matter (a refit of the king-danger coordinates captures it) or the index's structure (it does not)?
- **The fits.** `rarog-texel --tune-kingsafety` (integer coordinate descent over exactly the 23 king-danger coordinates: attacker weights 4, safe checks 8, weak ring, unsafe checks, blockers, king attacks, mobility lead, no queen, knight defender, constant, shelter feedback, and the two map scales; every other weight fixed at the head's value), on `tools/texel/data/hce-v6-tb` (train, with the pass's own early stop on its validation split), `--max-positions 200000` and `--epochs 40` as `fit_complete.ps1` runs the pass, `--fix-k` at the head's K read by `--buckets` on that validation split. **Seeds: "the donor's seeds" are C.3's**, the donor's values at the index's scale that C.3's fit started from, read from `src/eval/params.rs` at `05c792b`: attacker weights 81/52/44/10, safe checks 792/1283/645/967/1084/1897/772/1119, weak ring 185, unsafe checks 148, blockers 98, king attacks 69, mobility lead 206, no queen 873, knight defender 100, constant 37, map scales 49/49, shelter feedback 155. The initial vector is the head's complete defaults (`--write-defaults`) with those 23 replaced. Two fits: **R16** at `KS_INDEX_CAP` 1,600 (the head's), **R24** at 2,400 (RAR-E69's cap point, the map's static magnitude bound relaxed as RAR-E69's scratch did).
- **The read.** RAR-E72's instrument on its rows (`hce-v4-tb` validation, 194,444 rows, Stockfish `9587eeeb`'s scores, game provenance): for each variant a scratch tree (an archive of HEAD with RAR-E72's trace-only `patch_scratch.py`, the cap edit for the 2,400 variants, and for R16 and R24 the fitted vector baked into the defaults by `tools/texel/bake_params.py`), its tuner built and dumping the played scores and the full and played groups. Variants: **H16** (the head as it is), **H24** (the head at cap 2,400, unrefitted), **R16**, **R24**. Per variant, in the band (phase 32–95, the deciding cohort) and over all rows: (B) with C the hinge spline of that variant's played score and `rarog_map` that variant's full-path map output, game-clustered bootstrap as RAR-E72; and the held-out loss of C alone, so the refit's own static change shows. `analysis/artifacts/e73/read_e73.py`, RAR-E72's arithmetic unchanged.
- **Wires (else the read is void and the defect fixed and the whole run repeated):** the reader fed RAR-E72's own inputs (its scores file's played column and its `groups-full.csv`) reproduces RAR-E72's band (B), +0.1940, to its recorded digits; each scratch tuner's played dump equals the repository tuner's for H16; the scratch patch applies with every anchor matched once; each fit's log shows its seeds as the initial vector and converges or stops at the epoch cap, said either way.
- **Why the base is recomputed:** RAR-E72 ran before C.10 moved `LazyMargin` 423 → 475, which changes the played score on rows whose cheap score lies between them. H16's (B) is the base the rule reads against; its difference from +0.194 is reported.
- **Rule, frozen:** a refit **captures at least half** of the lead when its band (B) is at most half of H16's band (B). If R16 or R24 does, the lead is a fit matter and becomes a king-coordinate refit cycle gated by gate 1, before the freeze (PLAN's note); if neither does, **the lead closes as the index's structure**, which Phase F supersedes, and C.11's freeze is recorded. H24 is reported and decides nothing (RAR-E69 already read the cap as statically fitted). The refits' loss changes are reported and decide nothing.
- **PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION (freeze before exposure):**
  1. H16's band (B) within 0.03 of +0.194 (probability 0.85): the lazy margin moves few rows.
  2. Neither refit captures half (probability **0.75**): C.3's fit started from the same seeds on outcome labels of the same kind and moved far from them (safe checks 1283 → 611), and an in-sample fit on the same objective is unlikely to land on the donor's weighting if the joint fit did not; I expect R16's band (B) near **+0.15**, 80% band [+0.09, +0.20], and R24's within 0.02 of R16's.
  3. The refits' band loss within ±0.05% of H16's (probability 0.8): the king coordinates are already near their fitted optimum for this evaluation.
  - Most likely failure mode: the descent from the donor's seeds stalls in a basin closer to them than C.3's joint fit, giving a lower (B) that reflects the seeds rather than a better fit; the loss change then shows it (a lower (B) with a worse C-alone loss).
- **Falsification:** prediction 2 fails if either refit's band (B) is at most half of H16's.
- **Stop rule:** one run of each fit and each dump; no point, seed, cap or cohort is added after the numbers are seen.
- **Result:** not run.
