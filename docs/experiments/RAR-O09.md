- **Instrument, known today:**
  - *Node unit.* Rarog counts a position when a move or a null move reaches it (`src/search/mod.rs` `count_node`; called after the null move at `node.rs:1139`; D.6.8's contract counts the null move by rule). Reckless counts in `make_move` only (`search.rs:1475`), not on the null move (`:597`). Neither counts the root, a re-search, an exclusion search or a verification. A null-move child is a full node of work, so equal `go nodes` hands the donor its null-move children free.
  - *Node limits.* Both are hard and both finish the first iteration: Rarog stops at `nodes >= N` (`mod.rs:1907`), Reckless at `nodes > N` (`time.rs:80`), each tested at every node.
  - *Move time.* Rarog thinks for all of `go movetime T` (`src/search/time.rs:132-147`). Reckless stops at `T − 15 ms` (`time.rs:20`, `:55-56`) and ignores `MoveOverhead` there. Equal thinking time needs unequal commands.
  - *Budgets.* The head's own mean at `3+0.03` in Colosseum's summaries is 130,493 to 130,889 reported nodes and 66.5 to 66.9 ms a move (RAR-S124 and RAR-M87's head-to-head, `results.txt`).
  - *Opening counters.* Colosseum CLI 0.5.0 starts every EPD opening at `0 1` (`colosseum-engine/src/openings.rs:343-355`); the book's positions are at moves 3 to 9. Both time managers read the move number: Rarog's optimum scales with the game ply (102.9 ms on the first move against 121.7 ms under fastchess, RAR-M88), and Reckless's soft bound at `3.380+0.034` is 51 ms at move 1 against 93 ms at move 9, before its multiplier (`time.rs:35`, `:55`). The clock legs therefore differ by runner until Colosseum keeps the counters; matches (1) and (2) do not.
  - *Harness wires.* `sprt.ps1`'s per-engine node budgets and move times are proved live (D.7.1). Colosseum CLI 0.5.0's `match` resolves all four legs in dry runs; its wrapper needs D.7.1.1 for a move time, a per-side node budget and a declared foreign arm.

  - *Why the means miss, and what the bands are for* (investigation 2026-11-14, after the smoke and before any game of the four; `classify_short.py`). Match (2) exists to give each engine the same thinking allowance a move, the donor's scaled by the bridge's odds, with time management removed, so that (2) − (1) is the kernel's speed (`analysis/search_shape_decision_2026-11-13.md` section 4, hypothesis 3). The bands check that allowance: Rarog thinks all of `T` (70 to 74 is `T` plus up to 4 ms of latency), the donor `T − 15` (79 to 83 is that at ×1.127's 94 ms), the ratio near the odds. Under `go movetime` a move takes the allowance except where the engine's own rule ends the search first. Rarog's are one legal move (`forced_move_shortcut`, on under `movetime`; `src/search/mod.rs:1168`) and its iteration cap of 99 (`MAX_DEPTH − 1`, `:963`); its mate stop is off under `movetime` (`src/search/time.rs:228`), so *Instrument, known today*'s "thinks for all of `go movetime T`" holds except at these two. The donor's is its iteration cap of 239 (`MAX_PLY − 1`, Reckless `search.rs:78`); it has no single-move rule. Every short move of the smoke is one of them: Rarog 42 single legal moves and 61 at depth 99, the donor 7 and 29 at depth 239, none other. Under `movetime` unused time is not carried to another move, so these exits change no other move's allowance; they are positions where more time buys nothing. Without them the means are **70.09** and **78.39** ms, the commands, and the ratio 1.118. The mean therefore reads the allowance diluted by the share of such positions, which depends on the games and on two rules in which the engines differ: it overstates the donor's allowance (1.147 against 1.118), and on means match (2)'s ratio rule could void a correct instrument (the smoke's range reaches 1.158). The median sits on the allowance while those exits stay under half the moves (4.2% and 1.5% here): Rarog's 10th and 90th percentiles are both 70.0 ms, the donor's 78 and 79. Its blind spot, a fault in fewer than half the moves, is bounded above by the forfeit rule (20 ms margin, void at 0.5%) and is watched by reporting the means and the short moves by cause beside it. The donor's literal band 79 to 83 is the ×1.127 instance of its command less 15 to less 11 ms; held at ×1.1211 it would move the donor to 94 ms and hand back the expected odds (×1.129) in place of the measured ones. Verdict: read the bands on the median, the donor's re-centred on its command. Amended below (*Move times*, *Validity*).

- **Interacting mechanisms / confounds:**
  - *Unfitted donor.* The donor's constants are fitted to its network. A deficit at equal work is not what a fitted rebuild would reach; a surplus is a floor.
  - *The shares are differences of matches* (about ±11 Elo at 95% each) and assume Elo adds across conditions. They are descriptive.
  - *Speed is entangled with shape.* A tree with more quiescence nodes costs less per node. "Kernel speed" here means positions per second in games, whatever the cause.
  - *The bridge's price comes from bench*, not from games.
  - *Runner.* Match (3) replicates RAR-O08's match (5) only on the same runner or with the book's counters kept.
