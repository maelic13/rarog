# RAR-E38 — C.5.4 before any design: where a rook-against-minor scale can act, how often it would in games, and its expected value

Indexed under *5. Evaluation and data experiments* in [`docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`](../EXPERIMENTS.md).

- **Date / owner:** registered 2026-10-11, before the gate games were read for these endings; agent-run (zero games, no engine calls).
- **Baseline SHA / candidate SHA / dirty-diff hash:** head = branch `c5` at the registration commit, engine source equal to `master`'s `36f04d5` plus comment and texel-only changes (12,567,796 / EBF 2.535). No candidate.
- **Binary / compiler / PGO identity:** none; the games are RAR-E32 gate 1's (`tools/results/rar-e32-gate1/games.pgn`, 5,930 games, the arms `rarog-c43threat-pext-pgo` at 12,567,796 and `rarog-c43head-pext-pgo` at 13,562,404, `3+0.03`, no tablebases), the latest gate played. Truth is Syzygy (`D:/chess/tablebases/syzygy3456`) through python-chess.
- **The tablebase condition (stated before the design, as C.5.4's handoff requires):** with `SyzygyPath` set, the search probes Syzygy WDL at interior nodes of depth 1 or more up to `SyzygyProbeLimit` men (defaults `SyzygyProbeDepth` 1 and `SyzygyProbeLimit` 7; `can_probe_syzygy` in `src/search/mod.rs`), and at the root. No gate run file sets `SyzygyPath` (only `sprt-repair-tb.toml` and `tb-activation.toml` do), so gates play without tables; CCRL may play with them. So a KR-KN or KR-KB scale can act:
  - *without tables* (every gate): in the ending itself, and in every transition into it from five and six men and above (the trade that enters it, chosen or avoided by either side);
  - *with tables* (possibly CCRL): almost nowhere: every node of six men or fewer at depth 1 or more returns a WDL score before the evaluation is consulted, so the scale reaches only depth-0 quiescence leaves and positions entered from seven men with no seven-man table.
- **Research question:** RAR-E36 found the static overclaim persists (KR-KN 98.4%, KR-KB 94.6% of draws above +100 cp) while the wins convert. A static overclaim is a question, not a defect in play: does it cost results in games, through the ending itself or through the trades into it, often enough that a scale could be worth a gate?
- **Hypothesis / proposed mechanism:** the overclaim can cost in play two ways. (a) *Transition:* the rook side trades a won position of five or six men (for example KRP-KN) into a drawn KR-KN because it still scores +200; a scale would make that trade unattractive. (b) *The ending:* the rook side, scoring +200 in a drawn ending, avoids a repetition and walks into a loss or wastes nothing; the defender, scoring −200, declines a drawing line. (b) costs nothing when the ending is drawn and stays drawn.
- **Competing hypotheses:** (1) *too rare:* the exact endings occur in well under 1% of games, so any cost is below a gate's resolution. (2) *harmless overclaim:* the entering trades are mostly correct by Syzygy (the position before was already drawn, or the trade kept the win), so there is nothing for a scale to fix. (3) *the cost is on the defender's side:* it shows as positions the minor side should have entered and did not, which this read cannot see (a stated limitation).
- **Interacting mechanisms / consumers:** as RAR-E36 (the mop-up term and its |score| > 200 gate, `krpkb_scale`, the search's rule-50 damping, the TT and eval cache); the interior tablebase probe above; C.5.8's conversion re-read.
- **PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION (freeze before exposure):**
  1. *Occurrence:* games reaching exact KR-KN or KR-KB (no pawns) in RAR-E32's games: **0.7%**, 80% band [0.3%, 1.2%] (the 2.4.0-era pool read 0.51% and 0.46%). KRP-KB: 0.2% to 0.8%.
  2. *Transitions:* of the moves that enter exact KR-KN, KR-KB or KRP-KB from six men or fewer, at most 15% worsen the mover's Syzygy result (win to draw, draw to loss) (probability 0.7).
  3. *The ending:* games that enter a Syzygy-drawn exact KR-KN or KR-KB end drawn in at least 95% (probability 0.8).
  4. *Expected value* (the rule below): under 1 Elo at `3+0.03` (probability 0.85), so the recommendation is `NO_CHANGE`.
  - Expected Elo: none; this read decides whether a candidate is worth designing.
- **Falsification criteria:** the read is void if a game fails to parse or the cohort count differs from the PGN's game count.
- **Cheapest prior falsifier:** this read (minutes).
- **Registered order and rule (frozen):**
  1. `analysis/artifacts/coherence-2026-10-11/e38/rook_minor_games.py` over the PGN: replay every game; record each game that reaches a position with one side holding exactly one rook and the other exactly one minor and no other pieces (pawns any: the *rook-against-minor class*), the first ply of exact KR-KN, KR-KB and KRP-KB, the move that entered them, who made it, the Syzygy WDL from the mover's side before (when six men or fewer) and after, both engines' last reported scores, and the result from the rook side's view. Counts are reported over all 5,930 games and over the 5,898 the SPRT scored.
  2. **Affected games** are those where a scale could have changed the result: (a) an entering move by the rook side that turned a Syzygy win into a draw or a draw into a loss, or by the minor side that turned a draw into a loss; (b) a game that entered a Syzygy-drawn exact ending and did not end drawn, or a Syzygy-won one that the rook side did not win.
  3. **Expected value** = affected games / games × 0.5 points × 7 Elo per 1% of score (near 50%), as an upper bound (every affected game assumed fixed, in the candidate's favour, and each worth half a point).
  4. **Decision:** below 1 Elo (the `[0,3]` nElo bracket's midpoint is about 1 Elo, below which an STC gate resolves H0 more often than H1 at any budget), the recommendation is to close C.5.4 `NO_CHANGE` with a named retry trigger and build nothing. At 1 Elo or more, a candidate is registered with the drawn cohort deciding, RAR-E36's three vetoes, the king-to-minor distance as its shape and the gate form fixed before any game.
  - Nothing is added after the numbers are seen.
- **Result (run 2026-10-11, once, after the registration commit `57a22bb`; `analysis/artifacts/coherence-2026-10-11/e38/report.json`, exit 0, 5,930 games parsed without error, 5,898 of them the SPRT's):**
  - *Occurrence:* the rook-against-minor class (pawns any) appears in 571 games (9.6%), reaching six men or fewer in 312 (5.3%). Exact KR-KN in 42 games, KR-KB in 43, **85 games (1.43%) for the two**, 84 of them scored by the SPRT; KRP-KB in 46 (0.78%).
  - *Entering moves:* 128 of 131 are captures (the rest a pawn move and an underpromotion), almost all from five men (six for KRP-KB). The rook side entered 79 times and the minor side 52. **No entering move changed its mover's Syzygy result**: KR-KN rook draw → draw 26, minor draw → draw 7 and loss → loss 9; KR-KB 29, 6 and 8 the same way; KRP-KB rook win → win 16 and draw → draw 8, minor loss → loss 22.
  - *The ending:* every Syzygy-won entry was won (KR-KN 9 of 9, KR-KB 8 of 8, KRP-KB 38 of 38). Of the 68 Syzygy-drawn exact KR-KN and KR-KB entries, **65 ended drawn (95.6%)**; in the other three (games 105, 2591 and 5256) the minor side lost a drawn ending: the head defended in game 105 and the candidate in the other two. The 8 drawn KRP-KB entries all ended drawn.
  - *Affected games:* 3 (all in the SPRT's sample, all of kind (b)). **Expected value, upper bound: 3 / 5,930 × 0.5 × 6.95 = 0.18 Elo.**
- **Disposition:** observation; **recommendation: close C.5.4 `NO_CHANGE` and build nothing.** The expected value is under the 1 Elo line by a factor of five, even with every affected game counted as fixed in the candidate's favour. The static overclaim is real (RAR-E36) but costs nothing measurable in play: the trades into these endings are correct by Syzygy, the wins convert, and the overclaim on drawn endings leaves them drawn 96% of the time; the three losses were the defender's, where a scale on the rook side's score is not the obvious lever, though the king-to-minor distance RAR-E36 found would be. With tables (possibly CCRL) a scale here would be almost inert. Competing (1) and (2) hold; (3) cannot be read from games. **Retry trigger:** a game record in which a trade into KR-KN, KR-KB or KRP-KB worsens its mover's Syzygy result, or drawn exact KR-KN and KR-KB endings lost at more than 5% over at least 100 entries in gate games; the candidate's shape would then be the defending king's distance from its minor (RAR-E36). Not Elo.
- **PREDICTION CALIBRATION (appended after exposure):**
  - Original prediction (not rewritten): items 1 to 4 above.
  - Observed: (1) **miss** for KR-KN and KR-KB (1.43% against the band [0.3%, 1.2%]); KRP-KB hit (0.78%, at the band's top). (2) hit (0 of 131 worsened). (3) hit (95.6%, just over the line). (4) hit (0.18 Elo).
  - Which assumption failed: I scaled the 2.4.0-era pool's occurrence, which mixes opponents; self-play between two Rarog versions reaches these endings about three times as often. The miss is in magnitude; it does not move the decision, which the upper bound clears by a factor of five.
- **Conditional lesson:** read occurrence in the gate's own games, not a pool's, when the gate is what the expected value is compared with.
