## Conditional lesson

**Calibration, part 1:** (a) predicted +4 ± 10, read +0.9, inside: the donor's −2 at cut nodes is not what the margin was compensating for, so the '−3 too harsh' explanation is refuted as the driver of RAR-S81's rail; (b) predicted +6 ± 10, read +24.2 ± 9.4, a magnitude miss on the good side and outside the band: a quarter of the seed margin is worth about as much as the whole unfitted cluster read against the old head (+18.4 ± 9.4, RAR-S79), which says the seed itself, Rarog's legacy `4·d`, was the largest single misfit on the surface, and the fixed-node sweep that preferred `6·d` (b33 sweep) pointed the wrong way for the fourth time.
