# RAR-E54 — KNN-KP, third attempt: the drive and the pawn term added to the evaluation, not replacing it

Indexed under *5. Evaluation and data experiments* in [`docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`](../EXPERIMENTS.md).

- **Date / owner:** registered 2026-10-12, before any number was computed; agent-run (zero games). Maintainer instruction 2026-10-12 to make a third attempt after RAR-E53, and, if it fails, to hand a summary of the findings to the next model.
- **Baseline SHA / candidate SHA / dirty-diff hash:** baseline the current head, engine `38cbb02` (**14,197,472 / EBF 2.557**), `analysis/artifacts/c523/rarog-head.exe`. Candidate: one engine change, its constants recorded in the result and its patch kept under `analysis/artifacts/c57knnkp/`. Reference: RAR-E51's Stockfish runs (`analysis/artifacts/c57/ref/`).
- **What RAR-E52 and RAR-E53 established:** a score that *replaced* the evaluation for exact KNN-KP put the position only about 35 cp above KN-KP (the generic evaluation: 216 cp, a knight), so the search gave knights away and the wins ended in insufficient material (4 → 10 to 23 of 46 development wins). The corner drive raised DTZ progress (0.507 → 0.65) and cut ply-limit failures (28 → 13), but was never tested apart from that defect.
- **Hypothesis:** with the same terms *added* to the generic evaluation, every material neighbour keeps its normal difference (a knight stays worth a knight, the pawn's loss still drops to KNN-K's 0), and the drive plus the pawn term convert more wins than the head.
- **Competing explanations:** (1) *the drive was never the limit:* without the knight defect the gain is small, because the remaining failures need the blockade released at the right moment (a plan). (2) *the generic evaluation is the problem:* if it already scores advanced-pawn KNN-KP near KN-KP or 0, an additive pawn penalty cannot be large without crossing them; the grid is bounded for that reason.
- **Mechanism:** for exact K+N+N against K+P, `scale_endgame` returns the generic score plus, from the knights' side, `CORNER·(6 − d_corner) + KINGS·(7 − d_kings) − ADVANCE·(r − 2)` (definitions as RAR-E52: Manhattan distance of the defending king to the nearest corner, the kings' Chebyshev distance, the pawn's relative rank). No base, no replacement. The texel delta scale stays 1 for the family (the adjustment is additive and frozen); nothing else changes.
- **Interactions:** the mop-up's generic edge push (still applied, and in the same direction), KN-KP and KNN-K one capture away, entering trades from larger material, the texel trace (unchanged: the adjustment is after `raw`). `bench 13` has no KNN-KP position.
- **Invariants and tests:** fires only on exact material, either colour; colour-mirrored positions adjust by the negated amount; the adjustment's extreme stays far below the decisive band (compile-time assertion); direction tests (corner, pawn held back); and the **one-capture-neighbour test**: over 4,000 deterministic pseudo-random KNN-KP positions (both colours, knights' side to move), wherever the generic score of KNN-KP is above a neighbour's (each KN-KP, and 0 for KNN-K), the adjusted score must stay above it. A grid point that fails the test is excluded before any playout. Debug and release tests, `cargo fmt --check`, `cargo clippy --all-features --all-targets` at zero warnings; Syzygy-verified KNN-KP won and drawn positions added to `tests/endgames.epd` if the candidate proceeds.
