- **Research question:** RAR-E17 read a donor-direction residual of +0.66% for Stockfish's king term as one bundle (+1.58% in the middlegame band). (a) Which of its parts carries it: shelter and storm, the king-to-pawn distance (eg), the quadratic danger map, the pawnless-flank penalty or the flank-attack penalty; and inside the danger index, which components (attacker count × weight, weak ring, unsafe checks, safe checks, pinned blockers, attacks on king-adjacent squares, flank attack², mobility difference, the no-queen and knight-defender reductions, the shelter feedback). (b) Why Rarog's `ks_weak_ring`, `ks_flank_attack` and `ks_shelter_storm` were fitted to zero: their activation on `hce-v3-tb`, their covariance with the attacker-unit sum, and the held-out loss as a function of each weight at 1, 2 and 4. (c) RAR-E18 left the donor's excess above six men (about 1.1 points beyond its families) unattributed and named the scale factor, which Stockfish 11's trace does not print, as the candidate: how much do the donor's scale factor, its complexity term and the rule-50 damping of its total each add as directions, by material signature. (d) The magnitude the donor's king term and Rarog's danger table add by |score| band, for the unit's magnitude contract.
- **Hypothesis / proposed mechanism:** H1: the danger map carries most of the king residual, because it is the part whose conditioning differs most from Rarog's (attackers × weight, weak squares by defender type, unsafe checks, pinned blockers, the mobility difference, the quadratic map into mg *and* eg) while Rarog's shelter and storm already exist linearly; the three zeroed inputs are zero because the nonlinear pass moves them in integer steps of at least 1 and one unit of weight moves the 40-bucket table index by several buckets per position, so the smallest step overshoots (a saturation of the instrument, not an absence of information); the scale factor adds a measurable direction above six men because Rarog scales only opposite bishops and a few exact recognisers.
- **Competing hypotheses:** (a) *shelter/storm:* the residual sits in the pawn-shelter model (by file, rank and blocked storm, with the castling destination), which Rarog's four linear shelter terms miss; (b) *joint:* no sub-term separates and the information appears only when the parts enter together, so the unit must stay whole; (c) *dead inputs:* weak ring and flank attack are zero because they are collinear with the attacker units (the same attacks counted twice) and add nothing even at a fine step; (d) *rule 50:* the donor's excess above six men is mostly the linear rule-50 damping of its total, which no family carries and which is information about the game's end, not about the position; (e) *complexity, not scale:* the winnable term's complexity carries the excess and the scale factor little.
- **Interacting mechanisms / consumers:** none in the engine. The readings fix C.3's design (which king sub-terms and which scale rules the unit builds), the C.5.1 material cut, and the magnitude contract in C.0.4's card.

  2. The danger score alone (`rarog+ks_danger`) carries at least 60% of `rarog+king9587`'s gain and is the largest single sub-term (probability 0.6); shelter/storm is second; king-to-pawn distance, pawnless flank and flank attack each under 0.10% (probability 0.7 each).
  3. Leaving the danger score out (`rarog+king_minus_danger`) loses the most of any leave-one-out model; leaving the king-to-pawn distance out loses under 0.05% (probability 0.7).
  4. Inside the danger index, the three components whose removal costs the most are attackers count × weight, weak ring and safe checks, in some order (probability 0.5).
  5. The five sub-terms with separate weights (`rarog+king_parts`) gain at least 0.10% more than the bundle (probability 0.7).

- **Falsification criteria:** the instrument is void if the `ksdump` identity fails on any row (the printed danger components must sum to the printed danger index with C++ truncation), if `rarog+king9587` differs from RAR-E17's king gain by more than 0.25 points (then the sub-term shares are reported against the `9587eeeb` bundle only and prediction 1 is a miss), or if a shuffled-danger null control exceeds ±0.03%. H1's first clause is refuted if the danger score carries under 40% of the bundle's gain; its second clause if the Rarog inputs at weight 1 move the index by fewer than 2 buckets (then the zero is informative and competing (c) stands); its third if `rarog+sf_term` gains under +0.10% at seven men or more.
- **Cheapest prior falsifier:** RAR-E17 and RAR-E18 themselves, whose trace prints the king term as one line and no scale factor at all. The fit logs of `tools/results/hce-fit-20260902_221030` already show the nonlinear pass trying the three inputs at steps 4, 2 and 1 and leaving them at zero; they do not say why.
