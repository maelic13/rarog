- **Research question:** how large is the same-search equal-node gap between Stockfish's classical evaluation and Rarog's at the end of Phase C, and how much did the units accepted since RAR-O06 close?
- **Hypothesis / proposed mechanism:** the units accepted after RAR-O06 transfer part of their per-node gain into Stockfish's search, as C.2 and C.3 did (RAR-O06: 0.8 of the gate sums). Since RAR-O06 those are C.5.2's opposite-bishop rule (RAR-E29: STC +4.5 ± 8.3 Elo; it acts in a few percent of games) and C.4.3's threats unit (RAR-E32: gate 1 +13.0 ± 5.3 Elo, a first-crossing estimate of which about +5 is speed, and speed does not exist at equal nodes; `10+0.1` +12.9 ± 12.2).
- **Competing hypotheses:** (1) *no transfer:* the gains since RAR-O06 are fitted to Rarog's own search and the gap does not move. (2) *more than its share:* the donor-shaped threats terms suit a search fitted to the donor's evaluation and transfer in full or more.
- **Interacting mechanisms / consumers:** GUIDE's checkpoint ("Evaluation deficit"); PLAN's Elo budget row for C, which this reading corrects; C.11's freeze of the classical evaluation; E.1's attribution checkpoint. Not separated here, as in RAR-O05 and RAR-O06: how much of any equal-node gap is Stockfish's search being fitted to its own evaluation's scale and shape.
- **PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION (freeze before exposure; this part is frozen 2026-10-11):**
  - *After C.4 and C.5.2 only*, Elo of `sf-hce` over `rarog-hce` at equal nodes: **+122**, 80% band [+106, +138]: RAR-O06's +129.4 less about 7. Basis: per-node gains since RAR-O06 of about +8 to +10 Elo in Rarog's own search (C.4.3's +13.0 less its speed share and its first-crossing overstatement; C.5.2's near zero overall), transferred at 0.4 to 0.9 (RAR-O06 observed 0.8); the band adds one 1,000-game sample's spread (about ±11 at 80%).
  - Probability the gap is below RAR-O06's +129.4: 0.70; below +100: 0.03; at or above +140: 0.10.
  - *Units accepted after this registration* (C.6, C.8's cycles, C.10 and any C.5 or C.7 unit) each append an expected movement here, dated and with its basis, **before** the C.11 match; the prediction the match is judged against is this item plus those addenda. Nothing is appended after a game is played.
  - Expected Elo for Rarog: none; this is an instrument, not a strength claim.
  - Most likely failure mode: reading the change as Elo Rarog gained; Rarog's own gates measured that.

- **Disposition:** observation. By the frozen use the reading replaces RAR-O06's as Phase C's final meter in GUIDE's checkpoint and corrects PLAN's budget row for C: **82.5 ± 25.2 recovered at equal nodes** against the planned 100–160, below that band and inside the upper half of C.0's 30–90 prior for the family clusters (which did not count the refits).
- **PREDICTION CALIBRATION (appended after exposure):** the amended prediction was **+108, 80% band [+90, +126]**: a **hit**, 8.8 below the centre. Of its probabilities, below RAR-O06's +129.4 (0.9) held, below +100 (0.3) held by 0.8, at or above +122 (0.15) did not. Since RAR-O06 the predicted movement was about −21 (−7 frozen for C.4 and C.5.2, −14 for C.6 and C.8) and the reading moved −30.1 ± 23.8: consistent, with the gains transferring into Stockfish's search at about the upper end of the assumed 0.4–0.9 (RAR-O06 observed 0.8), or above it, which one 1,000-game reading cannot tell apart. C.10's lazy margin (expected 0) is not separable here. Hypothesis (1), no transfer, is refuted (the gap is far below +129.4); (2), more than its share, is not shown (the reading is inside the band, not below it).
- **Conditional lesson:** Phase C's evaluation work transferred into a foreign search at roughly the rate it was gained in Rarog's own games; the evaluation still trails Stockfish's classical one by about 99 Elo at equal nodes inside Stockfish's search, with RAR-E19/E20's caveat that a search fitted to Stockfish's evaluation favours its shape.
