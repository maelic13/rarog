# RAR-O13 — Stockfish's classical evaluation inside the compact kernel: `sf_11`'s and `sf_16`'s against Rarog's, at equal nodes

Indexed under *3. Search and selectivity › Search-oracle observations* in [`docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`](../EXPERIMENTS.md).

- **Date / owner:** registered 2026-11-24 (PLAN E.4.0.1), before any game. The instrument and the registration are the agent's; the two matches are the maintainer's (about one and a half hours together).
- **Kind:** observation, E.4.0's first read and RAR-O07's mirror. It sizes lever 1 of E.0's packet (`analysis/e0_remaining_strength_2026-11-23.md`) and answers E.4.0's donor question in play; the rule it feeds was frozen there.
- Disposition: observation — at equal nodes `sf_16` +80.3 ± 10.8 and `sf_11` +74.4 ± 10.6 Elo over Rarog's evaluation inside the kernel; on the clock, the bridge returned, `sf_16` +68.8 ± 10.4.
- **Research question:** how much stronger does the compact kernel play on Stockfish's classical evaluation than on Rarog's, at equal nodes, and which tag's evaluation is the better donor?
- **Revisions:** Rarog `cf1c377` (the `donor-eval` hook, engine) and `dbf265b` (the bridge, tooling) on `e0-research`; the engine with no feature benches **3,694,298 / EBF 2.324**, the immediate fingerprint, so the base arm's search and evaluation are the head's. Stockfish `sf_11` (`c3483fa9`) and `sf_16` (`68e1e9b3`) from `D:\code\stockfish`.
- **The instrument** (`tools/donor-eval/README.md`):
  - *Stockfish's side.* Per tag, a patch adds a placement setter that takes the FEN setter's steps in its order, (`sf_16`) the classical evaluation without `evaluate()`'s network choice, halfmove damping and clamp, and a C ABI. MSYS2 MinGW g++ 16.2.0 builds it as a static DLL (`tools/donor-eval/build_dll.sh`). `sf_11`'s contempt is zero, as its `eval` command sets it. `sf_16`'s lazy cut reads the root's last score: the kernel hands it after each root window, and minus `VALUE_INFINITE` before the first, as `sf_16`'s search starts.
  - *Rarog's side.* The `donor-eval` feature: an installed evaluation replaces `Evaluator::evaluate`, read into each evaluator when it is made, under the kernel's own wrapper (correction, material weighting, halfmove damping, optimism), which `sf_16`'s dropped wrapper duplicated. The FFI lives in the bridge crate, so the engine's `unsafe` floor is unchanged (17).
  - *Parity.* On B.0's 10,039 positions not in check, each DLL's placement path equals the tag's own FEN setter at four root scores: 0 mismatches in 40,156 for each tag. Against the release binaries' `eval` command (`stockfish-11-x64-bmi2.exe`; `stockfish-16-x64-bmi2-classical-pgo.exe` with `Use NNUE=false`) every value matches to the printed precision, largest gap 0.0050 pawns. The negative control, `sf_11`'s values against `sf_16`'s binary, flags 274 of 300 positions. Rebuilt from the tags by the stored patches, both DLLs give byte-identical parity tables.
  - *The root wire.* `sf_16`'s value moves with the root score in 1,440 of the 10,039 positions, `sf_11`'s in none. `tests/donor_eval_hook.rs` shows the search hands the root score over: none before a search's first window returns, then always.
  - *The hook is neutral when idle.* Built with `donor-eval` and nothing installed, `bench 13` gives 3,694,298 / 2.324.
- **The scale, frozen before any game:** the donor's value times `SCALE_1024`/1024 enters the kernel as Rarog's raw evaluation. The rule is each evaluation's K on `hce-v4-tb`'s validation rows (194,444 positions not in check, white-perspective results), fitted by least squares as the tuner fits its K; the scale is K_donor/K_rarog, so a score predicts the same result in both. K_rarog 1.64307; `sf_11` K 1.09777, **0.6681, 684/1024**; `sf_16` K 1.15066, **0.7003, 717/1024**.
  - *The rules not taken*, all measured on B.0's corpus before the choice: RAR-O08's mean |·| ratio, 0.2831 and 0.2974. Stockfish's known-win scores (about 10,000 of its units) dominate that mean, so it would shrink every ordinary donor score to about half Rarog's size, under margins fitted to Rarog's scale. The per-position median ratio is 0.5438 and 0.6051. The donor's own pawn convention, 100/PawnValueEg, is 0.469 and 0.481. The read bundles the rule chosen; it is the named confound below.

  Geometric-mean ratio of positions visited. The tail is in decided endings: on bench positions 15 (a won pawn race) and 39 (queen and rook against rook) the kernel's line reaches seldepth 92 to 110 on the donor's evaluation where Stockfish 11's own search reaches 20 (11,944 nodes at depth 13 on position 15, against more than 20 million inside the kernel). On position 15 at depth 12 the kernel's futility pruning fires on 7.0% of its asks against 20.1% on Rarog's evaluation, singular extension is asked 0.073 times a node against 0.050, and the TT estimate fires on 54.5% against 19.4% (`--features probes`). The mechanism is not isolated: under the pawn-convention scale (481) position 15 still grows fivefold and position 39 tenfold. The bridges' `bench 13` therefore runs for minutes on `sf_16`; their fingerprint is `bench 11`.

- **Interacting mechanisms / confounds:** the kernel's margins and its fit (D.7.11) were made on Rarog's evaluation, the wrapper too; the donor runs under them unfitted, as Rarog's evaluation ran unfitted in Stockfish's search in RAR-O07. The scale rule. The trees above: at equal nodes a larger tree is a shallower search, and in decided endings the donor arm loses depth the native arm keeps. Equal nodes removes the evaluation's cost and the bridge's, both read later on the clock. Selection: the rule reads the better of two arms, which favours the better read by up to about half a standard error when the two are equal.
- **PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION (frozen 2026-11-24, before any game):** Elo of the donor arm over the base:
  - `sf_11`: **+40**, 80% band [−5, +80].
  - `sf_16`: **+45**, 80% band [0, +85].
  - P(the better arm ≥ +25) 0.65; P(`sf_16` above `sf_11`) 0.55.

  Basis: RAR-O07's +99.2 ± 16.6 in Stockfish's search, roughly halved for the kernel's margins fitted to Rarog's evaluation and for the unfitted scale; the trees' tail takes a little more; `sf_16` and `9587eeeb` are equal in Basilisk's games and `sf_11` was never measured, so the two are nearly even with `sf_16` slightly ahead on its terms added since. Expected Elo for Rarog: none, this is an instrument.
- **Falsification criteria:** the prediction misses if either arm lands outside its band; the sign of `sf_16` over `sf_11` is read only when their difference exceeds its interval.
- **Cheapest prior falsifier:** the parity and wire checks above, passed.
- **Registered gate and stop rule:** no gate. Two fixed matches of 2,000 games, no early stop, no extension, no re-run on the same instrument.
- **Use of the reading, frozen in E.0's packet:** under **+25** for the better arm at equal nodes, a rebuild in the donor's form is not indicated and E.4 is repairs; the programme's band is +25 to +70.
