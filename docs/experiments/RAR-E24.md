# RAR-E24 — C.2 attribution baseline: the unchanged surface refitted on `hce-v4-tb`, gated by PROCESS's *Evaluation change under a fitted search*

Indexed under *5. Evaluation and data experiments* in [`docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`](../EXPERIMENTS.md).

- **Date / owner:** registered 2026-10-06, before the corpus exists, before any fit and before any game. The fit is local and long (hours) and the games are the maintainer's; the static screens and the tree read are agent-run once the candidate exists.
- **Baseline SHA / candidate SHA / dirty-diff hash:** both arms build from `dev` at the commit the fit starts from (engine source `79c6808` or a behaviour-neutral successor, fingerprint 11,171,726 / EBF 2.512, named in the result). Candidate = that tree plus `fit_complete.ps1`'s `candidate-eval.patch` (`src/eval/params.rs`, `src/eval/material.rs`); its hash is recorded with the fit.
- **Binary / compiler / PGO identity:** `rustc 1.98.1`; each arm a clean `cargo xtask build --arch pext --pgo` of its tree, revision-matched; the baseline must bench 11,171,726. Engine names carry the fingerprint (`rarog-c2head-pext-pgo`, `rarog-c2refit-pext-pgo`, sidecars by `build_test.ps1`).
- **Research question:** what does refitting the unchanged surface on the programme's corpus give, so that C.3's gate does not conflate corpus, labelling search and structure (RAR-E12: a corpus change alone measured +11.8 ± 5.3 Elo)? The candidate is a bundle and is read as one: (a) labels from the B.9 search (`hce-v4-tb`) instead of the RAR-E08 head's (`hce-v3-tb`); (b) the fit describes the played function (the `texel` build honours `LazyMargin`, `79c6808`; 16.4% of validation rows sit above the gate); (c) the fitting manifest: `king_safety_table` is fitted by the coordinate stage only, no longer also by the linear stage, and 80 never-active slots are fixed (no numerical effect). No term is separable inside this gate.
- **Hypothesis / proposed mechanism:** cleaner labels and a fit of the function the engine plays lower held-out loss and move weights toward what the B.9 search realises; the search, fitted to the current evaluation, pays for the shape change per node (RAR-E19/E20), so gate 1 is small and gate 2 recovers part of the mismatch.
- **Competing hypotheses:** (1) the corpus change is near-neutral for a fixed surface (the B.9 search's labels differ little at 8,000 nodes), and any gate result is the search mismatch; (2) the played-function fit moves the activity weights enough (fitted on 16% fewer rows) that the tree changes more than RAR-E12's +12.3%, gate 1 fails, and the margin block decides.
- **Interacting mechanisms / consumers:** every cp-valued margin (the 28-coordinate surface below), correction history, `CoreEvalMaterialScale`, `LazyMargin` (the gate's own meaning moves with the scale), the eval and pawn caches (unchanged keys), C.3's gate baseline.
- **PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION (freeze before exposure):**
  1. Offline: the frozen test improves against the source vector on `hce-v4-tb/test.csv` (probability 0.95), by about −0.0005 (RAR-E12: −0.00043); best validation epoch in the polish's first half.
  2. Static screens (PROCESS step 1, read before any game): the magnitude contract of the C.0.4 card applied unchanged (`--dump-scores` played for the candidate against the head's, per band 0–100, 101–300, 301–600, 601–1200, > 1200 on `hce-v4-tb/validation.csv`): no band ≤ 600 shifts toward the score's sign by more than 20 cp and the share above the gate moves by under 2 points (probability 0.6). The donor-direction screen against the candidate (RAR-E23's instrument): king family within ±0.15 points of the head's reading (probability 0.7).
  3. Tree read (PROCESS step 2): `positions_v1.epd`'s 72 game positions (C.0.3), depth 12, cleared table, both arms: total nodes change between −3% and +12%, growing with probability 0.6; `bench 13` moves by more than 2% (probability 0.8).
  4. Gate 1 (`[0,3]`, search unchanged): **+3 Elo**, 80% band −8 to +12; probability of passing 0.35.
  5. Gate 2 after the margin block, if the block runs: probability of passing 0.5; expected +5 Elo, 80% band −5 to +14. The block moves the RFP, razor and futility margins and `LazyMargin` most (5–15% from the seeds), the SEE constants and `CoreCorrUpdate*` under 5%.
  6. `10+0.1` read of an accepted candidate: non-negative (probability 0.75).
  - Most likely failure mode: gate 1 fails inside the harm bound with a flagged tree read, and gate 2 passes narrowly or not at all.
- **Falsification criteria:** the hypothesis is refuted if both gates fail. A frozen-test delta of 0 or worse refutes the offline half and stops before any game.
- **Cheapest prior falsifier:** the offline frozen test (prediction 1), then the static screens; neither costs a game.
- **Registered gate and stop rule (frozen):**
  - **Order:** fit (`fit_complete.ps1 -DatasetDir tools/texel/data/hce-v4-tb -TargetTrain 3500000`, manifest `tools/texel/hce_fit_manifest_v1.tsv`, schedule and settings as RAR-E12's) → static screens → tree read → PGO bake of both arms → gate 1 → margin block when gate 1 fails **or** the tree read flags → gate 2 → `10+0.1` read of an accepted candidate.
  - **Flags (before any game):** the magnitude rule above (a band ≤ 600 shifting by more than 20 cp toward the sign, or the share above the gate moving by 2 points or more); the tree read flags when its total nodes change by more than 5% either way, or its per-position geometric-mean ratio leaves 0.95 to 1.05. A flag sends the candidate to the margin block after gate 1 whatever gate 1 reads; it rejects nothing.
  - **Gate 1 and gate 2:** Colosseum (`tools/colosseum.ps1 -Mode sprt -Bracket default`), pentanomial GSPRT `[0,3]` nElo, `3+0.03`, one thread, the harness's UHO book and adjudication, cap **20,000 pairs** each. Cap derivation (RAR-M10, prospectively): drift per game 8.3e-6 × 3 × (4.5 − 1.5) = 7.47e-5 at the expected +4.5 nElo (+3 Elo at RAR-E12's 1.49 nElo per Elo), so ln(19) / 7.47e-5 ≈ 39,400 games to the upper bound.
  - **Margin block:** group `c2margin` (`tools/spsa_configs/config_c2margin.json`, committed with this registration): the 28 coordinates PROCESS names, `LazyMargin` included, each seeded at the head's default and bounded by its declared range, step max(2, round(range / 16)); `fixed_c2margin.json` Hash 64, Threads 1, MultiPV 1. One block: `colosseum.ps1 -Mode spsa -ConfigGroup c2margin -Iterations 2000 -TotalGames 60000` on the candidate's tune build, evaluation weights fixed. The block's final theta, rounded, is gate 2's candidate options; no second block.
  - **Stops:** frozen-test delta ≥ 0: no games, recorded. A tree read above +20% with gate 1 failing below −5 Elo: stop before the block and return to the maintainer. Bounds, caps, book, adjudication and the block's surface never change after games.
  - **Acceptance and use:** gate 1 passing without a flag accepts; otherwise gate 2 decides. An accepted candidate becomes Phase C's baseline (its weights, and its margins if gate 2 accepted) and C.3 fits on `hce-v4-tb` from it. If both gates fail, the head stays the baseline and C.3's gate is read as structure plus corpus, the corpus alone measured not positive here.
